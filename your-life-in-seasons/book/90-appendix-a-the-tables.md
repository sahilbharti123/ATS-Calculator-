# Appendix A: The Tables

Every number in this book agrees with the tables on these pages. When you are drawing your map, keep this appendix open. Where a table judges, it carries the usual dots; where it only lists, it does not. All dates in the last table are approximate and say so.

## A.1 The nine seasons and their lengths

The order never changes; the starting point is the Moon's star at birth. The lengths are tradition (Parashara's 120-year timetable), not astronomy, and the tradition gives no reason for them.

| Order | Season (the courtier) | Length | Share of the 120 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ketu (the headless Sadhu) | 7 years | 5.8% |
| 2 | Venus (the Minister of Pleasure) | 20 years | 16.7% |
| 3 | Sun (the King) | 6 years | 5.0% |
| 4 | Moon (the Queen Mother) | 10 years | 8.3% |
| 5 | Mars (the Commander) | 7 years | 5.8% |
| 6 | Rahu (the smoke-headed Stranger) | 18 years | 15.0% |
| 7 | Jupiter (the Royal Priest) | 16 years | 13.3% |
| 8 | Saturn (the old Judge of Labour) | 19 years | 15.8% |
| 9 | Mercury (the clever young Prince) | 17 years | 14.2% |
| | **Total** | **120 years** | **100%** |

After Mercury the order returns to Ketu. A life that begins in, say, the Rahu season runs Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars.

To use it: find your season's row, then read across in the fixed order starting from the season's own column, adding each length to the season's start date. The months are twelfths of a year and the days thirtieths of a month, as the tradition reckons them, which is why your app's dates may differ from this arithmetic by a few days.

## A.3 The Moon's star and the first season

The balance at birth is the part of the star the Moon had still to cross, divided by 13°20′, multiplied by the lord's years.

## A.4 Season by age: a ready-reckoner for the nine starting seasons

Find the row for your first season (from A.3). Read across for the ages at which each later season begins and ends. **The table assumes you received the whole of your first season.** In real life the first season is shortened by the balance at birth, so every age in your row is reduced by the part of the first season you did not receive. Meera's row is the Mercury row minus 5.5 years (she received 11.5 of Mercury's 17); Arjun's is the Sun row minus 4.2 years; Devika's is the Venus row minus 1 year.

A quick way to read it: those who begin in Ketu spend their teens and twenties in Venus; those who begin in Venus leave it at twenty and meet Rahu in their mid-forties; those who begin in the Sun or Mercury enter Rahu or Venus in their mid-twenties; those who begin in the Moon, Mars or Rahu meet Saturn somewhere between forty and fifty; and those who begin in Jupiter or Saturn have their Saturn season early and their Venus season in their forties and fifties.

## A.5 The slow planets overhead, 2026 to 2032 (approximate)

These are the sign positions of the three planets whose weather matters most (Chapter 14), given to the nearest few months. **They are approximate.** The planets walk backwards for part of every year and cross sign boundaries more than once, so a planet near a boundary may be in either sign on a given day. Check an app for the exact date before you rely on one. Ketu is always in the sign opposite Rahu.

| Planet | Sign and approximate dates |
|---|---|
| **Saturn** (about 2½ years a sign) | Pisces until about mid-2027 · Aries from about mid-2027, with a return to Pisces from late 2027 to early 2028 · Aries again from early 2028 until about 2030 · Taurus from about 2030 |
| **Jupiter** (about a year a sign) | Gemini until about mid-2026 · Cancer to about mid-2027 · Leo to about mid-2028 · Virgo to about mid-2029 · Libra to about late 2030 · Scorpio to about late 2031 · Sagittarius through 2032 |
| **Rahu** (about 1½ years a sign, walking backwards) | Aquarius until about December 2026 · Capricorn to about mid-2028 · Sagittarius to about early 2030 · Scorpio to about mid-2031 · Libra from about mid-2031 |
| **Ketu** (opposite Rahu) | Leo until about December 2026 · Cancer to about mid-2028 · Gemini to about early 2030 · Taurus to about mid-2031 · Aries from about mid-2031 |

Saturn's seven and a half years run while Saturn crosses the sign before your Moon's sign, your Moon's sign, and the sign after. From this table: a Pisces Moon is in the middle phase until mid-2027; an Aries Moon has begun the first phase and enters the middle phase around mid-2027; a Taurus Moon begins the first phase around mid-2027 and the middle phase around 2030; an Aquarius Moon is in the last phase until mid-2027. Everything else about the weather is in Chapter 14.
