# Chapter 14: Weather Inside the Season

A season is a climate. Jaipur in a Venus season is still Jaipur: hot, dry, beautiful. But inside the climate there is weather. Some weeks it rains in the desert. Some years the monsoon does not come at all. If you know only the climate you will be surprised every afternoon; if you know only the weather you will never understand why the same rain feels like a blessing in one city and a flood in another.

The dasha system is the climate. The planets moving overhead right now are the weather (gochar, which Book 1 and most apps call transit). This chapter teaches you to read the two together, which is the only way either one makes sense. As always, every date here is approximate; your app has the exact ones.

## The slow planets are the only weather that matters for seasons

Every planet is moving overhead all the time. The Moon crosses a sign in about two and a quarter days; the Sun, Mercury and Venus take about a month each; Mars about a month and a half. These are the showers. They matter for choosing a wedding date, not for understanding a year of your life.

Three movements are slow enough to shape a year:

- **Saturn** takes about two and a half years to cross a sign, and about twenty-nine and a half years to go round the whole zodiac.
- **Jupiter** takes about a year a sign, twelve years for the round.
- **Rahu and Ketu** take about a year and a half a sign, eighteen and a half years for the round, and they move backwards through the signs, always exactly opposite each other.

> **🔍 Why?** Astronomy. The further a planet is from the Sun, the slower it orbits: Jupiter's year is twelve of ours, Saturn's nearly thirty. Rahu and Ketu are not bodies at all but the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's, and those crossing points drift slowly backwards as the Moon's orbit wobbles. Everyday parallel: on a station platform, the local trains rush through every few minutes and you stop noticing them; it is the one long-distance train that changes the shape of your afternoon.

So when I say "the weather", in this chapter I mean three things: where Saturn is, where Jupiter is, and where Rahu and Ketu are.

## Two places to stand: from the rising sign and from the Moon

A moving planet is always in some sign. That sign is some room counted from your rising sign, and also some room counted from your Moon's sign. Both counts matter, and they usually give different numbers.

Counting from the rising sign tells you **which part of life** the weather falls on: career, home, partnership. Counting from the Moon tells you **how it will feel**, because the Moon is the mind, and the tradition's oldest transit rules are all counted from the Moon.

Here are the classical good rooms from the Moon for the three slow movers. The dots judge the room, as always; a red dot is a room where that planet's transit is traditionally felt as pressure, not a sentence passed on you.

| Moving planet | Good rooms from the Moon (classical) | The rest | Felt most as pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturn | 3, 6, 11 🟢 | 🟡 | 12, 1, 2 (the seven and a half years) and 4, 8 🔴 |
| Jupiter | 2, 5, 7, 9, 11 🟢 | 🟡 | 6, 8, 12 🔴 |
| Rahu / Ketu | 3, 6, 11 🟢 (common practice, not Parashara) | 🟡 | 1, 7, 8, 12 🔴 |

> **🔍 Why?** Tradition, with a logic underneath. The lists are given by the classical authors without derivation, but you can see the shape: Saturn, the planet of work and limits, is welcome in the growing rooms (3, 6, 11) where effort pays, and heavy on the rooms of self and comfort (1, 4, 8, 12). Jupiter, the planet of gifts, is welcome where gifts land (2, 5, 9, 11) and across from the Moon (7), and uncomfortable in the hidden rooms. Everyday parallel: a strict uncle visiting is a help at exam time and a strain at a party.

## Saturn's seven and a half years, explained from the Moon

Now the transit everybody has heard of and almost everybody misunderstands: Saturn's seven and a half years (Sade Sati, which simply means "seven and a half" in Hindi).

Take your Moon's sign. Saturn, moving at two and a half years a sign, will at some point enter the sign before it. That is the first phase. Then it crosses your Moon's own sign: the second phase, the middle, the heaviest. Then the sign after: the third phase, when the weight starts lifting. Three signs, about two and a half years each, seven and a half years in all. Because Saturn's round takes about twenty-nine and a half years, this happens to you roughly once every thirty years: in the late twenties, the late fifties, and the late eighties, give or take.

![A north Indian chart with the Moon in one room and the rooms on either side shaded](../images/ch14-sadesati.svg)
*Figure 14.1: Saturn's seven and a half years. The Moon sits in one sign; Saturn crosses the sign before it, the Moon's sign, and the sign after it, about two and a half years each.*

Here is the honest framing, which I wish every WhatsApp forward included. Three of the twelve signs are "in" the seven and a half years at any moment. That is a quarter of everyone alive. Right now, as I write in late 2026, Saturn is in Pisces, so everyone with the Moon in Aquarius, Pisces or Aries is somewhere in their seven and a half years: hundreds of millions of people, having every kind of year. When Saturn enters Aries around mid-2027, the Aquarius Moons are released and the Taurus Moons begin. It is a tide, not a curse.

What it asks is real, though. Saturn over the Moon is the old Judge of Labour sitting in the Queen Mother's rooms, going through her accounts. The mind feels heavier. Things take longer. Supports you leaned on are tested: a parent ages, a job becomes work, a friendship shows its true weight. People who come out of it well are the ones who used the years to grow up in some specific way: to take responsibility for something they had been avoiding. People who come out of it badly are the ones who spent seven years waiting for it to end.

The tradition adds two smaller Saturn stretches: Saturn in the fourth sign from the Moon and in the eighth sign from the Moon, two and a half years each (Book 1 gives their Sanskrit names). Lighter than the seven and a half years, but you will feel them, mostly as a pressure on home and comfort (the fourth) or on the sense of security (the eighth).

> **📖 Story: The Judge visits the Queen Mother.** Once in a generation the old Judge of Labour came to audit the Queen Mother's household. He arrived in the room before hers and sat for two and a half years, reading. Then he moved into her own apartments and the whole palace went quiet; the lamps were trimmed, the feasts stopped, every servant's work was weighed. Then he moved to the room beyond and slowly the music returned. The Queen Mother who had kept honest accounts wept at the start and thanked him at the end: he had found three servants stealing and one old debt she could at last pay off. The one who had hidden things wept all the way through. **Rule:** Saturn's seven and a half years is an audit of the mind's household; it rewards whoever has nothing to hide and whoever is willing to start keeping accounts now.

> **💡 Did you know?** A strong Moon bears the audit better. The tradition says a Moon at its strongest place, or at home, or sitting with Jupiter, or with Jupiter looking at it, softens Saturn's weight. Arjun's Moon is at its strongest place in Taurus. His seven and a half years, beginning around mid-2027, should be a real audit but not a crushing one.

## The same weather, a friendly season and a testing season

Here is the idea that turns two half-truths into one truth. A transit does not act on its own. It acts on whatever the season has already put in the room.

Picture Saturn crossing your rising sign, room 1, which is tiring for anyone. If you are in a Jupiter season, and Jupiter is on your side, the tiredness arrives as the weight of real responsibility: a promotion that costs your evenings, a child, a house with a loan. You are tired because you are carrying something worth carrying. If you are in a Saturn season, and Saturn tests you, the same transit is the season lord itself walking over you: the tiredness has no gift inside it yet, only the task, and the task is to endure well and build slowly. Both people say "Saturn is on my rising sign and I am exhausted." Only one of them is in trouble, and even she is in the kind of trouble that builds a person.

The same is true the other way. Jupiter over your room 10 in a friendly season is the Priest blessing the work you are already doing: recognition, a good offer, a mentor. Jupiter over room 10 in a Ketu season is a blessing on a desk you are trying to leave: good news about a job you no longer want. It still counts as good weather. It just lands on a different climate.

> **🧭 Anushka's rule of thumb:** The season decides what can happen. The weather decides when it is likely, and how it feels that year. Never read a transit without first asking which season it falls into, and never let a transit frighten anyone whose season does not promise the thing they fear.

> **📖 Story: Rain on two fields.** The same monsoon fell on two farms outside the city. On the first, the Priest had spent the year sowing, and the rain brought a harvest that filled the storerooms. On the second, the headless Sadhu had spent the year letting the land rest, and the rain brought wildflowers and a flooded path. The second farmer was not cursed. He had simply not planted anything, because it was not that kind of year for him, and the rain honoured what was there. **Rule:** a transit waters whatever the season has planted; good weather on an empty field grows wildflowers, not grain.

## The two signatures: how astrologers time an event

Now the single most useful timing tool I know, and I want to label it: this is a modern Indian teaching, spread widely by the Delhi school of K.N. Rao and his students, not a line from Parashara. Practitioners call it the double transit. I call it the two signatures.

The rule: an event in some area of life tends to happen in the year when **both Saturn and Jupiter are touching that room or its lord**, by sitting in it or by looking at it, **and** the running season or sub-season promises that matter. Jupiter alone is a wish. Saturn alone is a duty. Jupiter and Saturn together, on a room the season has already opened, is a date.

"Looking at" means the gaze you met in Book 1: every planet looks at the room opposite it (the seventh from itself); Saturn also looks at the third and the tenth from itself; Jupiter also looks at the fifth and the ninth. So each of the two slow planets touches four rooms at any time: the one it sits in and the three it looks at.

> **🔍 Why?** The system's own logic, as its teachers explain it. Jupiter is the planet of permission and growth; Saturn is the planet of structure and completion. A matter needs both: the expansion that makes it possible and the solidity that makes it real. Everyday parallel: a bank cheque above a certain amount needs two signatures, the director's and the accountant's. One signature gets you a smile at the counter. Two gets you the money.

Two cautions. First, the season must promise the matter; two signatures on the marriage room in a season whose lord has nothing to do with marriage give you a year of weddings in the family, not your own. Second, Saturn's slow pace means its signature on a given room lasts about two and a half years, so the rule narrows the window to Jupiter's year within Saturn's stretch. That is useful, and it is as precise as honest astrology gets.

## The sky from 2026 to 2030, approximately

*Figure 14.2: The slow planets overhead, 2026 to 2030. Approximate: sign changes are rounded to the nearest few months, and Saturn's brief step back into Pisces is shown. Check an app for the exact dates.*

In words, and please read them with "about" in front of every date:

- **Saturn** is in Pisces until around mid-2027, then enters Aries; it steps back into Pisces for a few months from late 2027 to early 2028, then settles in Aries; it reaches Taurus from about 2030. Aries is Saturn's weakest place, so the Aries years are a slower, more irritable Saturn for everyone.
- **Jupiter** is in Gemini until about June 2026, Cancer (its strongest place) to about mid-2027, Leo to about mid-2028, Virgo to about mid-2029, and by the system's rhythm Libra and then Scorpio across 2029 to 2031.
- **Rahu** is in Aquarius until about December 2026, then Capricorn until around mid-2028, then Sagittarius. Ketu is always in the opposite sign: Leo, then Cancer, then Gemini.

These are the positions I used for everything below. If your app disagrees by a month or two, trust the app.

## Where it lands for our three lives

![A grid showing which room each transit falls in for Meera, Arjun and Devika](../images/ch14-weather-lands.svg)
*Figure 14.3: The same sky, three different rooms. Each cell gives the room from the rising sign and the count from the Moon.*

**Meera (Scorpio rising, Moon in Cancer).** Her seven and a half years ran from 2000 to 2007, inside her Ketu season, when she was a schoolgirl; her next begins when Saturn reaches Gemini in the early 2030s, inside her Moon season, which is where it belongs in this story and where we will leave it. Between now and 2030 Saturn is kind to her Moon: Pisces is the ninth sign from Cancer, Aries the tenth, Taurus the eleventh. From the rising sign, Saturn sits in her room 5 until mid-2027 and then in room 6, the room of daily work and health routines. Jupiter in Cancer from mid-2026 to mid-2027 sits on her Moon itself, in room 9: a year when faith, a teacher or a long journey comes to the mind, in the farewell sub-season of her Venus season. Then Jupiter in Leo from mid-2027 sits in her room 10, the career room, in the first year of her Sun season, and the Sun is the lord of room 10. Two signatures? In that first Sun year, Saturn sits in room 6 and Jupiter from Leo looks straight at room 6. So both slow planets touch her room 6: daily work, service, health routines, competition. Not a glamorous room. But the Sun season asks who you are when nobody is watching, and room 6 is exactly where nobody watches. I would expect her first Sun year to be about the discipline of work rather than the glory of it, with the glory coming later.

**Arjun (Cancer rising, Moon in Taurus).** This is the one to watch. Saturn enters Aries, the twelfth sign from his Moon, around mid-2027: his seven and a half years begin, inside his Rahu season, and will run through the end of Rahu and into the first years of Jupiter from December 2031. His strong Moon will help. From the rising sign, Saturn in Aries is in his room 10, the career room, and from there it looks at room 7, where his Venus sits, and at room 4, where Rahu sits. Meanwhile Jupiter in Cancer (mid-2026 to mid-2027) sits in his room 1 and looks at rooms 5, 7 and 9: Jupiter's gaze on room 7 while Rahu moves into Capricorn, his room 7, from December 2026, sitting on his Venus. Partnership is being asked about loudly through 2027, in a Rahu/Venus sub-season whose sub-lord sits in that very room. Whether it answers as a marriage or a business partner is for him and his chart to decide; the weather only says the question is on the table. Then from mid-2027 to mid-2028, Saturn sits in his room 10 and Jupiter in Leo looks at room 10: two signatures on career, in the last year of Rahu/Venus and the first months of Rahu/Sun. A career turn is very likely in that window, and it will feel like work, because Saturn is doing the signing from inside the room.

**Devika (Aries rising, Moon in Sagittarius).** Her seven and a half years are behind her: Saturn walked through Scorpio, Sagittarius and Capricorn from around late 2014 to around early 2023, which covers her Mars season and the changeover into Rahu, including the surgery of 2016 to 2018 and the property dispute of 2020 to 2021. That is what the audit looked like for her. Now Saturn in Pisces is the fourth sign from her Moon until mid-2027, one of the two smaller Saturn stretches: pressure on home and comfort, and from Pisces Saturn looks at her Moon's sign directly. From the rising sign it is in her room 12 while Jupiter in Cancer sits in her room 4, on her Sun and her own Jupiter, and looks at room 12. Two signatures on room 12 from mid-2026 to mid-2027: expenses, hospitals, foreign places, rest, letting go. In the first year of Rahu/Saturn I would tell her plainly: this is a year to take rest and health seriously and to spend deliberately, and possibly a year of a long journey. Then from mid-2027, Saturn sits in her room 1 while Jupiter in Leo sits in room 5, on her Mercury, Saturn and Rahu, and looks at room 1. Two signatures on the self. A reshaping year, inside the Rahu/Saturn sub-season, with Saturn signing from the room of the body: slower, more serious, and hers.

> **⚠️ Common mistake:** Reading a transit over a natal planet as a prediction about that planet's whole meaning. Rahu crossing Arjun's Venus does not "mean" anything about his marriage by itself. It raises the volume on Venus's matters for a year and a half. The season and the chart decide what the louder Venus actually says.

> **🪔 If you read for others:** When someone tells you they are in their seven and a half years, the first thing to say is that so is a quarter of the country. The second is to ask which season they are in. A Saturn transit in a Jupiter season and the same transit in a Saturn season need completely different conversations, and the forward they received did not know which one they were having.

## In one breath

- The season is the climate; the planets overhead now are the weather. Read them together or you will misread both.
- Only the slow movers shape a year: Saturn (two and a half years a sign), Jupiter (about a year), Rahu and Ketu (a year and a half, moving backwards).
- Count a transit from the rising sign for which part of life it touches, and from the Moon for how it feels.
- Saturn's seven and a half years is Saturn crossing the sign before the Moon, the Moon's sign and the sign after. A quarter of everyone alive is in it at any moment. It is an audit, not a curse.
- The same transit lands differently inside a friendly season and a testing season: good weather on an empty field grows wildflowers, not grain.
- Two signatures: when Saturn and Jupiter both touch a room or its lord, and the season promises that matter, that is the year to expect it. A modern teaching, widely used.
- Positions 2026 to 2030 are approximate. Check an app before you say a date out loud.
