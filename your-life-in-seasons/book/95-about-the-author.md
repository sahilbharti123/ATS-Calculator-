# About the Author

Anushka Bharti is a practising Vedic astrologer. She came to the subject through curiosity about her own life and the lives of the people she loved, found the classical books closed to her, and rebuilt the subject for herself from first principles, insisting on a reason and a story for every rule.

Her first book, *Astrology, Gently*, teaches the whole of chart reading in plain English. *Your Life in Seasons* is the second book in the series and takes one idea, the dasha system of planetary seasons, and goes deep.

She believes the most important use of astrology is not prediction but understanding: of one's own nature, of one's seasons, and of the people one loves.

If this book helped you, tell someone who is in a hard year. And if you find an error, please write; a second edition should always be better than the first.
