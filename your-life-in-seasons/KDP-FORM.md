## Description (HTML, under 4,000 characters)

```html
<b>The most common question an astrologer is asked is not "what am I like?" It is "how long will this last?"</b><br><br>
Vedic astrology has a beautiful answer. A life is not one long road. It moves in seasons. Each season is run by one of the nine planets, for a fixed number of years, in a fixed order, and the season you are born into depends on where the Moon stood in the sky the night you arrived. Astrologers call it the dasha system. This book calls it what it is: the seasons of your life.<br><br>
<i>Your Life in Seasons</i> is the second book in the <i>Astrology, Gently</i> series, and you do not need to have read the first. It takes one idea, the timing of a life, and goes deep, in plain English.<br><br>
<b>What makes this book different</b><br>
<ul>
<li><b>Plain words.</b> A mahadasha is a <i>season</i>. An antardasha is a <i>sub-season</i>. A nakshatra is <i>the Moon's star</i>. Every Sanskrit word appears once, in brackets, and then lives in the glossary, so the chapters read like English and you can still talk to any astrologer in their own language.</li>
<li><b>A reason for everything.</b> Where a rule comes from astronomy, the book says so. Where it comes from the logic of the system, it shows the logic. Where it is simply tradition, it tells you that too.</li>
<li><b>No fear.</b> A hard season is a season with a task, not a verdict. Every chapter ends with what to actually do.</li>
<li><b>A comic strip in every chapter.</b> The nine planets appear as characters, from the old Judge Saturn to the Minister of Pleasure Venus, and you, the reader, are in the strip with them.</li>
<li><b>Three whole lives, worked in full.</b> Meera, Arjun and Devika carry you from the first season to the one running today, so you see the method before you turn it on yourself.</li>
</ul>
<b>What you will learn</b><br>
How your first season is chosen and why the order never changes. What each of the nine seasons, from Ketu's seven years to Venus's twenty, tends to ask of you. How sub-seasons work and why one year inside a good season can still be hard. Where the planet sits in your chart and what that changes. The weather of transits and Saturn's seven and a half years, explained without dread. The awkward changeover years. How to live well in a hard season. And finally, step by step, how to draw your own season map with free tools.<br><br>
<b>Who this is for</b><br>
Anyone in a hard year who wants to know, kindly and honestly, what it is for and roughly how long it will hold. And anyone who loves someone in one.
```
