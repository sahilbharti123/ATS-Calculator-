<b>You bought the books everyone recommends. You opened them with real hope. And within ten pages you felt stupid.</b><br><br>
You are not stupid. Those books were written for students who already knew the sky. This one is written for everyone else.<br><br>
<i>Astrology, Gently</i> teaches Vedic astrology (Jyotish) as a language with a small grammar: nine planets, twelve signs, twelve houses, twenty-seven stars. Once you understand how the pieces combine, you never need to memorise another list, because you can derive any placement yourself.<br><br>
<b>What makes this book different</b><br>
<ul>
<li><b>A reason for everything.</b> Every rule comes with its why: the astronomy behind it, the internal logic of the system, or the classical author who stated it. Nothing is left as "the texts say so".</li>
<li><b>A story for every hard idea.</b> The planets are a royal court you will never forget: a King, a Queen Mother, a Commander, a clever young Prince, a Priest, a Minister of Pleasure, an old Judge, and two strangers at the gate. Who is whose enemy, which planets help each other, which planet serves your rising sign: you remember these as stories long after the tables fade.</li>
<li><b>Colour-coded tables and 125 diagrams.</b> Every judgement is marked good, mixed or difficult at a glance.</li>
<li><b>Lal Kitab clearly labelled.</b> Everything from that tradition sits in its own marked boxes with the source stated, so you always know which system you are using.</li>
<li><b>Three complete readings.</b> Watch the whole method applied from birth details to final advice.</li>
</ul>
<b>What you will learn</b><br>
Signs, planets and houses. Aspects and conjunctions. The 27 nakshatras. Which planets are good for your particular rising sign. House lords, yogas, divisional charts and planetary strength. Vimshottari dasha, transits and Sade Sati. Panchanga and choosing a good day. Prashna, the astrology of questions. Then the questions people actually ask: career and money, marriage and matching, health and longevity, children, education, property, travel and the spiritual path. Finally, how to sit across from someone with kindness and confidence, and how to do it ethically.<br><br>
<b>Who this is for</b><br>
Anyone who has wanted to learn Vedic astrology and given up. You need no Sanskrit and no prior knowledge. Cast your own chart in the first chapter and carry it through the book; by the end you will know it the way you know your own handwriting.<br><br>
And if you never read for anyone but yourself and the people you love, you will still understand your own life a great deal better. That is the real reason this book was written.
