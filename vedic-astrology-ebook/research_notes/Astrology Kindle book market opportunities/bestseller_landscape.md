## Key Question 1: Which titles sit at the top of the Kindle Astrology categories in the US, UK and India, and what do they have in common?

### Takeaway
The US Kindle Astrology charts are led by a mix of (a) evergreen traditionally published "complete guide" backlist (Woolfolk, Edington/Callisto, Linda Goodman, Chani Nicholas), (b) cheap or Kindle-Unlimited self-published birth-chart/ascendant guides (Mari Silva, Deborah Rossetto) and (c) adjacent "new science" titles (Human Design). The UK chart is dominated by year-ahead horoscope products and 79p single-sign ebooks from HarperCollins/Thorsons, plus novelty print; India's Kindle astrology shelf is overwhelmingly Vedic (BPHS, Lal Kitab, Phala Deepika, K.N. Rao) and heavily Hindi-language, with ranks suggesting a deep but low-velocity category.

## Key Question 3: Which sub-niches are saturated and which have thin or dated supply?

### Takeaway
Saturated: beginner Western "complete guide"/birth-chart books, sun-sign books, yearly horoscopes (now flooded with 2026 KU titles from pseudonymous publishers), and tarot-astrology crossovers. Thin or dated: Vedic astrology written for Western readers (the two mainstream entries date from 2020 and the newest is a Sept 2025 UK title), astrology for parents (a few trad print titles, no evident Kindle indie activity), astrology for career/money (dominated by niche financial-astrology trading books), astrology for men (almost nothing beyond "how to attract a X man" booklets), and dasha/timing-based planning (no English title surfaced).

## Key Question 5: Evidence on seasonality (new year, Mercury retrograde, eclipse seasons) in astrology book sales

### Takeaway
Direct Kindle sales-by-month data for astrology was not found, but publishing behaviour and category data point to a strong New Year peak (yearly horoscopes ship Aug–Dec; Body-Mind-Spirit sales were +17% YoY in January 2025 then fell in Feb–Mar), with astrologers reporting engagement spikes around eclipse seasons and Mercury retrograde rather than documented book-sales spikes.

### Cited Findings
- BookNet Canada (Body, Mind & Spirit, Q1 2025): sales up 17% YoY in January 2025, down 35% in February and down 15% in March; Divination accounted for 22% of all BMS sales Jan–Mar 2025; fastest-growing BMS sub-subjects were Ayurveda (+254%), Feng Shui (+51%) and Angels & Spirit Guides (+50%). The same summary also states Q1 sales "decreased by 42%", which is not arithmetically consistent with the monthly figures and should be verified against the original post — [BookNet Canada: Subject spotlight: Body, Mind & Spirit](https://booknetcanada.ca/?p=3786); earlier BookNet analysis of astrology/tarot/witchcraft — [Signs & Spells (2020)](https://booknetcanada.ca/blog/2020/5/11/signs-amp-spells-astrology-crystals-tarot-and-witchcraft-in-the-canadian-book-market)
- Yearly titles are released well before the year: Jessica Davidson's "Astro Journal 2026" out 15 Dec 2025; Polansky's 2026 single-sign ebooks were on NetGalley ahead of 2026; Jessica Adams' birthday book launched Oct–Nov 2024 for the Christmas market — [Davidson](https://jessicadavidson.co.uk/2025/12/15/astro-journal-2026-is-out-now/); [NetGalley](https://www.netgalley.co.uk/catalog/book/558118); [Adams](https://www.jessicaadams.com/2024/12/25/blog/two-christmas-presents-for-you/)
- UK trade press describes a "horoscope season" at year-end around which IPC relaunched Prediction magazine — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/the-ctn-guide/79875.article)
- Chani Nicholas reports noticeably greater engagement across social, blog and newsletter during eclipse season; solar eclipses pull in "peripherally-interested" people who then consume content and download apps — [TIME, 2024 solar eclipse](https://time.com/6963188/solar-eclipse-astrology-2024/)
- Google searches for "is Mercury in retrograde" have shown considerable viral growth since 2009 — [TIME, Mercury retrograde](https://time.com/5207161/mercury-retrograde-astrology-history/)
- Chinese New Year is a secondary seasonal hook: "Year of the Fire Horse 2026: A Chinese New Year Horoscope Guide" (AstroLoom) targets it — [Amazon New Age Horoscopes (Kindle)](https://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers-New-Age-Horoscopes/zgbs/digital-text/158482011)
- Publishers Weekly reported stressful periods prompting sales for spirituality titles and MBS publishers "looking skyward" (astrology) in 2020; a 2026 Winter Institute piece reports "passion purchases"/analog living trending up — [PW: Stressful Times Prompt Sales](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/religion/article/85408-stressful-times-prompt-sales-for-spirituality-titles.html); [PW: MBS Publishers Look Skyward (2020)](https://www2.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20200525/83394-mind-body-spirit-publishers-look-skyward.html); [PW: WI2026 passion purchases](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/bookselling/article/99800-wi2026-passion-purchases-analog-living-trend-up.html)
- US print market context: total print units rose <1% in 2024 (782.7M vs 778.3M) and ~0.3% in 2025, so BMS/astrology growth is category-specific rather than market-wide — [Publishers Weekly 2024](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/96842-print-book-sales-saw-a-small-sales-increase-in-2024.html); [Jane Friedman 2025](https://janefriedman.com/2025-us-book-sales-holding-steady-for-print/)
- A weak anecdotal data point: a Damascus bookstore reported horoscope book sales up 35% around New Year — [Syrian Observer](https://syrianobserver.com/?p=88527)

### Inferences
- The only robustly evidenced seasonal effect is the New Year/year-ahead cycle (release calendar Aug–Dec, January category uplift, UK "horoscope season"). Eclipse and retrograde effects are documented as attention/engagement spikes, not as book-sales spikes.
- For a Kindle launch, the data imply a Q4 release of any year-ahead or planning product and evergreen positioning for everything else.

### Gaps
- No Kindle-specific (or any ebook-specific) monthly sales data for astrology was found; BookNet data is Canadian print-plus-ebook at the broad BMS level, and Circana BookScan category breakdowns are paywalled — [Circana BookScan overview](https://www.bookweb.org/circana-bookscan-overview)
- No study linking Mercury-retrograde or eclipse dates to book sales (as opposed to search/app interest) was located.
