## Key Question 1: What do mid-list self-published spirituality/astrology authors earn per month, and from which streams?

### Takeaway
No public, niche-specific income report for a self-published astrology or mind-body-spirit author (2024–2026) surfaced in any search; the only data available is cross-genre survey data, which shows a steep power-law: roughly 44% of indie authors earn ≤$100/month, ~20% earn $500–$5,000/month, and ~13% earn >$5,000/month, with catalog size and an email list being the strongest predictors. Nonfiction authors in the ALLi data cluster in the lower-to-mid brackets.

### Cited Findings
- Written Word Media's 2025 Indie Author Survey (1,346 responses, 96% already published) found approximately 44% of respondents earn $100/month or less, ~20% earn $500–$5,000/month, ~13% earn over $5,000/month and ~8% earn over $10,000/month. All data is self-reported and the sample skews toward serious, marketing-engaged indies — [Written Word Media 2025 Indie Author Survey Results](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/); summarized at [MetaStellar](https://www.metastellar.com/nonfiction/news/results-from-written-word-medias-2025-author-survey/)
- In the same survey, ~80% of authors with 1–3 books earn under $100/month; authors with 25+ books report a median of ~$3,000/month; authors earning $5,000–$7,500/month have a median catalogue of ~30 titles — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/); [Vappingo, "How Much Do KDP Authors Actually Earn"](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/how-much-do-kdp-authors-earn/)
- Email list effect in the WWM 2025 survey: authors with a functioning email list earn ~$300/month vs ~$15/month without; average list size of authors earning >$10,000/month was 18,327 vs 902 for those under $100/month — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- ALLi 2025 Indie Author Income Survey (over 1,800 responses, 1,520 qualified): median annual income of "committed" self-published authors (>50% of working time or >50% of income from writing/publishing) was $13,500, up ~6% from $12,749 in the previous survey; 28% earned over $50,000 and almost a fifth earned six figures — [ALLi 2025 Author Income Survey](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/2025-author-income-survey/); [ALLi/Draft2Digital "Big Indie Author Data Drop" 2025 PDF](https://www.allianceindependentauthors.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Big-Indie-Author-Data-Drop-2025-UPDATE-PAGES.pdf)
- In the ALLi 2025 data, general nonfiction is ~6% of authors overall but 9–10% of those in the $100–$1,000 income brackets, i.e., nonfiction authors over-index in low-to-mid earnings — [ALLi Big Indie Author Data Drop 2025](https://www.allianceindependentauthors.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Big-Indie-Author-Data-Drop-2025-UPDATE-PAGES.pdf) (via search snippet)
- Across the broader author population, ~75% of self-published authors earn under $1,000/year from books; ~15–20% earn $1,000–$10,000/year; ~2–5% of active KDP authors reach $10,000/month — [Vappingo, KDP income](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/how-much-do-kdp-authors-earn/); [Vappingo, "$10,000 a month from KDP"](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/10000-month-kdp/)
- Public KDP income reports are a self-selected sample biased upward (often used as a credibility signal for a secondary business) and almost always report gross royalties before ad spend, which can be 20–40% of gross for authors running significant Amazon Ads — [Vappingo, "KDP Income Reports: What They Actually Tell You"](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-income-reports/)
- One estimate of typical ad burden: "authors typically spend 30–50% of net royalty on ads to stay visible" — [Titan Network, Amazon Ad Costs 2026](https://titannetwork.com/amazon-ad-costs-guide/)
- Nonfiction has a structural advantage: nonfiction books "typically have longer shelf lives and can generate steady income for years without a new release driving backlist sales"; evergreen searched topics build more stable income — [Just Publishing Advice, "Is Writing Nonfiction More Profitable Than Fiction?"](https://justpublishingadvice.com/?p=50376)
- Nonfiction authors in general (including trade-published) "often derive 40–70 percent of their income from bulk and special sales" — [Sudowrite, "How Much Do Authors Really Make? 2025"](https://sudowrite.com/blog/how-much-do-authors-really-make-2025-data-on-royalties-advances-and-income/)
- Mind-body-spirit as a category: during 2020, sales of mind-body-spirit books "soared," with specialty houses reporting notable bumps for new titles and backlist, especially in esoteric, New Age spirituality, divination, healing and self-care — [Publishers Weekly, "Stressful times prompt sales for spirituality titles"](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/religion/article/85408-stressful-times-prompt-sales-for-spirituality-titles.html)
- Astrology as a trade category: Chani Nicholas's *You Were Born for This* (HarperOne) hit #7 on PW's overall bestseller list within a week of its January 2020 release; astrology guides are "a staple of the mind-body-spirit category" — [Publishers Weekly, "Look Up, Look Inward with Astrology" (2021)](https://publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20210802/87023-look-up-look-inward-with-astrology-mind-body-spirit-books-2021.html)
- A Reddit r/selfpublish post (April 2025) titled "How did my new book perform" reported very modest initial nonfiction results with minimal earnings; another self-published author reported 80 KDP orders, 69 IngramSpark orders and 4 Draft2Digital orders over a period — [r/selfpublish thread](https://lr.us.psf.lt/r/selfpublish/comments/1k8b01o/how_did_my_new_book_perform) (page itself was blocked; figures from search snippet only)

### Inferences
- For a first book in astrology nonfiction with no list, the realistic base case from the survey data is <$100/month; a 3–5 book connected backlist plus an email list is what moves an author into the $300–$3,000/month band.
- Income stream mix for this niche is not documented anywhere I could find; the general pattern (ebook + KU dominant for indies, print a meaningful add-on for reference/workbook-style nonfiction) is an inference, not a measured finding.

### Gaps
- No astrology- or spirituality-specific KDP income report or case study for 2024–2026 was found in any search; the few niche-adjacent results (Benebell Wen's self-publishing course materials from 2016; a Sher School of Astrology Kindle launch post) could not be fetched.
- No data on the split of ebook vs KU vs print revenue for nonfiction specifically.

## Key Question 2: How much does a series / connected backlist (3–5 books) change the economics, and how do nonfiction KDP authors structure series in a niche like astrology?

### Takeaway
Survey data make catalog size the single strongest income predictor (1–3 books → ~80% under $100/mo; 25+ books → median ~$3,000/mo). In astrology, the dominant working series structures observed in the market are (a) yearly editions ("2026 Horoscope"), (b) one-book-per-zodiac-sign sets of 12, and (c) a flagship guide plus journal/workbook companion.

### Cited Findings
- Catalog size is "the strongest income predictor": 25+ books → median ~$3,000/month; 1–3 books → ~80% under $100/month — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- Authors earning $5,000–$7,500/month have a median catalogue of ~30 titles — [Vappingo, KDP income](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/how-much-do-kdp-authors-earn/)
- Nonfiction example of a short-book backlist model: S.J. Scott built a sustainable six-figure income from a catalog of short, focused habit/productivity nonfiction books — [ZonGuru, "How much do self-published authors make on Amazon"](https://www.zonguru.com/blog/how-much-do-self-published-authors-make-on-amazon)
- Observed astrology series structures (2026 editions on sale now):
  - **Per-sign annual series:** Dr Gautam DK's "Annual Horoscope 2026" series publishes one book per zodiac sign; the first two chapters are shared across all 12 editions, then each covers that sign's career, finances, health, love, marriage, children, education and spiritual growth — [Goodreads listing](https://www.goodreads.com/topic/list_book/242960485)
  - **Annual all-signs edition:** Tatiana Borsch's *Complete Horoscope 2026: Your Monthly Astrology Guide with Moon Phases and Lunar Days* is the latest in an annual series spanning over three decades — [Newsfile press release](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/277566)
  - **Trade comparables:** *The AstroTwins 2026 Horoscope: The Complete Yearly Astrology Guide for Every Zodiac Sign*; Patsy Bennett's *2026 Horoscopes Annual Calendar* (Simon & Schuster UK); a per-sign *Libra 2026* ebook sold via Hoopla/Abbey's — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/topic/list_book/243864957); [Simon & Schuster UK](https://www.simonandschuster.co.uk/books/2026-Horoscopes-Annual-Calendar/Patsy-Bennett/9781923208025); [Hoopla](https://catalog.harrisonpl.org/Hoopla/18666767)
  - **Guide + journal companion:** trade houses pair guides with journals, e.g., Adams Media's *The Astrology Journal* (Mecca Woods) alongside beginner guides like *Astrology for Life* (Castle Point) and *The Complete Book of Astrology* (St. Martin's Essentials) — [Publishers Weekly 2021](https://publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20210802/87023-look-up-look-inward-with-astrology-mind-body-spirit-books-2021.html)
- "Write to market" is applied to nonfiction via market research, and evergreen niches like self-help have high Amazon demand — [Scribecount, Authority Pub Academy](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/indie-author-courses/authority-pub-academy)
- Series pricing lever: the most effective permafree technique is making book 1 of a series free so readers pay for the rest — [Digital Pubbing, freebie strategies](https://digitalpubbing.com/freebie-strategies-for-indie-authors/)

### Inferences
- The per-sign 12-book model is attractive on KDP because it multiplies the number of ASINs (and keyword/category slots) from a largely shared manuscript — the "first two chapters common to all" structure in the Dr Gautam DK series is explicitly this. The trade-off is 12 covers and 12 listings to maintain, and annual editions expire every year (a recurring-revenue model but with yearly re-launch cost).
- A pragmatic 3–5 book architecture for a Vedic astrology author that mirrors observed market structures: (1) evergreen flagship ("Vedic Astrology for Beginners…"), (2) a workbook/journal companion (print-first, higher price), (3) a narrower "X for Y" title (e.g., relationships/career), (4) an annual predictions edition (re-launch each year), (5) optionally per-sign splits of the annual.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on per-book earnings uplift from adding book 2/3 in *nonfiction* (series read-through data is published mainly for fiction).
- No sales figures for the self-published per-sign series found.

## Key Question 3: Current norms for Kindle pricing and length in Body, Mind & Spirit nonfiction, and does KU pay off for a 400-page book?

### Takeaway
The 70% royalty band is $2.99–$9.99 (reportedly raised to $12.99 on 7 July 2026 — unverified); advice for self-published nonfiction clusters at $3.99–$9.99 with a $7.99–$9.99 "sweet spot," and 40,000–70,000 words (20,000–50,000 for narrowly focused ebooks). KENP has run ~$0.0042–$0.0050 per page in 2025–2026, so a 400-KENP book read in full earns ~$1.80–$2.00 per borrow, comparable to a $2.99 sale and below a $4.99+ sale — KU pays off only if readers finish it.

### Cited Findings
**Royalty bands**
- 70% royalty applies when the ebook is priced $2.99–$9.99 (or local equivalent) in marketplaces where 70% is offered; below $2.99 or above $9.99 is 35%; 70% also requires the book not be cheaper elsewhere online — [Vappingo, 35% vs 70%](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/amazon-kdp-royalties-35-vs-70/); [KDP help G200634560](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200634560)
- A search snippet reports that on 7 July 2026 Amazon raised the 70% ceiling from $9.99 to $12.99, "the first change since KDP launched in 2007" — [BestWriting, KDP Royalties Explained](https://bestwriting.com/kdp-royalties). *Unverified against KDP's primary page in this session; treat as provisional.*
- At the 70% tier Amazon deducts a delivery fee of ~$0.15 per MB before computing royalty; no delivery fee at 35% — [Vappingo, 35% vs 70%](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/amazon-kdp-royalties-35-vs-70/)
- For sales to customers in India, Brazil, Japan and Mexico, 70% requires KDP Select enrollment — [KDP help G200634560](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200634560)

**Pricing and length norms**
- Self-published ebooks should be priced $3.99–$9.99, with the sweet spot for nonfiction at $7.99–$9.99 — [Daniel J. Tortora, pricing self-published books](https://danieljtortora.com/blog/how-to-price-a-self-published-book)
- Self-help/how-to books typically run 20,000–70,000 words; ideal 40,000–70,000; narrowly focused self-published ebooks 20,000–50,000 — [Daniel J. Tortora, nonfiction word count](https://danieljtortora.com/blog/word-count-nonfiction-book)
- Amazon's nonfiction ebook pricing is described as "complicated" (category-dependent price perception) — [Kevin Dangoor Substack](https://kevindangoor.substack.com/p/amazon-makes-non-fiction-ebook-pricing)

**KU / KENP economics**
- KENP rate history (US): 2025 typically ~$0.00452–$0.00465/page; October 2025 $0.005007/page on a $61.2M fund (a 5-year high); April 2026 $0.00482; May 2026 ~$0.00489 on a $70.3M fund; 2025 fund ranged from $51.9M (Feb) to $59.9M (May and July) — [Kindlepreneur KENP calculator](https://kindlepreneur.com/kenp-calculator/); [Profitable.app KENP rate](https://profitable.app/kdp/stats/kenp-rate); [Written Word Media Global Fund tracker](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/kdp-global-fund-payouts); [Vappingo, KU page reads](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kindle-unlimited-page-reads/)
- KENP rate = marketplace fund ÷ total KENP read in that marketplace that month; since the November [2015] payout "payouts per country will differ based on local country factors" — [Just Publishing Advice, What is KENP](https://justpublishingadvice.com/what-is-kenp/); [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/kenp-calculator/)
- Worked example: at ~$0.0045/page a 300-KENP book read cover to cover earns ~$1.35 per read; 200 full reads/month ≈ $270 — [Vappingo, KU page reads](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kindle-unlimited-page-reads/)
- "Longer books earn more per complete read … but length only delivers income if readers actually finish"; read-through rate, not page count, determines KENP income; for long (300+ print page) books KU "can be highly lucrative," shorter books may do better on sales — [Vappingo, KU page reads](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kindle-unlimited-page-reads/); [Kindlepreneur KENP calculator](https://kindlepreneur.com/kenp-calculator/)
- KDP Select enrollment is a 90-day exclusive term; it unlocks KU, 5 free days per term (or a Countdown Deal), and the 70% tier in India/Japan/Brazil/Mexico — [Vappingo, KDP Select & KU](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-select-kindle-unlimited/); [Scribecount KDP Select guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-a-book/kdp-select-the-complete-operational-guide)

### Inferences
- Math for a 400-KENP astrology reference book: full read ≈ 400 × $0.0045–0.0050 = $1.80–$2.00. A $4.99 sale at 70% nets ~$3.40 (before delivery fee); a $2.99 sale nets ~$2.05. So KU is roughly break-even with a $2.99 sale per full read and about half a $4.99 sale. Because reference-style astrology books are often dipped into rather than read linearly, actual KENP per borrow will likely be well below the full count — KU is most attractive for linear "beginner guide" formats and least for lookup tables/ephemeris-style content.
- A reasonable launch stance for a first book: enroll in Select for the first 90 days (free days + India 70% tier + KU discovery), measure KENP vs sales, then decide.

### Gaps
- No category-specific average price for Kindle "Body, Mind & Spirit > Astrology" bestsellers was found (would require Publisher Rocket/KDSPY-type data).
- Could not verify the $12.99 ceiling change or 2026 KENP numbers directly against KDP's pages.

## Key Question 4: Which launch tactics have measurable effect for a first-time author with a small audience?

### Takeaway
The only tactic with broad survey evidence is building and emailing your own list (~20x earnings gap in the WWM 2025 survey); paid newsletter promos (BookBub Featured Deal: $353–$1,664 in Religion & Spirituality; Bargain Booksy: $25–$240) have measurable one-off spikes; Amazon Ads for books run ~$0.15–$0.60 CPC with a 25–35% ACOS norm; free days and $0.99 launches only work when paired with promo traffic. Social video has anecdotal wins concentrated in fiction.

### Cited Findings
**Email list**
- Authors with a functioning email list earn ~$300/month vs ~$15/month without; WWM concludes "the most successful book marketing is the email list … paid promos are decent but don't outperform" direct reader relationships — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)

**BookBub Featured Deals**
- Religion & Spirituality Featured Deal pricing: $353–$1,664 depending on discount price and region; Christian Nonfiction: $465–$2,331 — [BookBub Partners pricing](https://www.bookbub.com/partners/pricing)
- Requirement: book must be free or discounted at least 50%; deals can be all regions (US, UK, CA, AU), US-only, or international-only — [BookBub requirements](https://www.bookbub.com/partners/requirements); [BookBub pricing](https://www.bookbub.com/partners/pricing)
- Effect: "hundreds or even thousands of sales for featured titles and tens of thousands of downloads for free titles"; the biggest boost occurs in the first 24–48 hours and trails off ~4 days later; a genre-fiction $0.99 deal can produce 1,000–5,000+ sales in a day (fiction benchmark; nonfiction figures not given) — [Reedsy, BookBub guide](https://reedsy.com/blog/bookbub/); [Vappingo, BookBub for authors](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/bookbub-for-authors/); [BookBub support](https://support.bookbub.com/?p=117)
- BookBub's selectivity "makes it hard for emerging authors to get a promotion" — [Reedsy, Written Word Media](https://reedsy.com/blog/written-word-media-ad-platform-for-authors/)

**Other newsletter promos**
- Bargain Booksy features cost $25–$240 by genre (includes a Religion/Spirituality and a Nonfiction list); Deal-of-the-Day $95–$240; covers ebooks priced $0.99–$5.00; 230k+ readers — [Written Word Media support](https://support.writtenwordmedia.com/article/3vx9ml1zlj-how-much-does-a-bargain-booksy-feature-cost); [Self-Publishing.com review](https://selfpublishing.com/bargain-booksy-review/)
- Written Word Media overall audience 800k+ readers (600k+ on email lists), promos from $25 — [Reedsy](https://reedsy.com/blog/written-word-media-ad-platform-for-authors/)

**KDP free days / $0.99**
- Up to 5 free days per 90-day Select term; a well-promoted free run "with newsletter placements can generate thousands of downloads," while an unpromoted one "typically generates few downloads"; authors concentrate days into a 2–3 day window; benefits are rank boost, also-boughts and post-promo paid tail — [Vappingo, KDP Free Days](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-free-days-promotions/)
- Free books lower reader risk for unknown authors; bargain shoppers will try an unknown author at the right price; Gaughran's *Let's Get Visible* "price pulsing" (temporary discounting) is widely used — [Digital Pubbing](https://digitalpubbing.com/freebie-strategies-for-indie-authors/); [EditionGuard](https://www.editionguard.com/?p=1704)

**Amazon Ads (AMS)**
- Book ads (KDP Sponsored Products) run $0.15–$0.60 per click, much lower than physical goods ($0.80–$1.40) — [Titan Network, Amazon Ad Costs 2026](https://titannetwork.com/amazon-ad-costs-guide/)
- Average Amazon ACOS ~25–35% in 2025 (≈29% overall) — [eDesk, Amazon ACOS](https://www.edesk.com/blog/amazon-acos/); [Eva.guru](https://eva.guru/blog/7-proven-ways-to-lower-your-amazon-advertising-costs/)
- Bid math: with £2.80 royalty per sale and a 30% ACOS target, max profitable CPC ≈ £0.42 at 5% conversion, ≈ £0.84 at 10% — [Vappingo, ACoS for book ads](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/acos-amazon-ads-books/)
- Start with low custom bids (e.g., 11–16 cents) or 20–30% below the suggested bid and raise incrementally — [Well-Storied, Amazon ads intro](https://www.well-storied.com/blog/an-introduction-to-amazon-ads); [Vappingo, Amazon Ads for KDP](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/amazon-ads-kdp-complete-guide/)
- "At least 50 reviews" is cited as a practical threshold for an Amazon ad campaign to convert well (a practitioner heuristic, not an Amazon rule) — [BookWriter.vip, review velocity tracker](https://www.bookwriter.vip/review-velocity-tracker)

**ARC services / Goodreads**
- BookSirens: $10 per book + $2 per reader download; promoted to its community for three months; accepts nonfiction but "reader interest in nonfiction is hard to predict" (parenting/gardening do poorly; memoir/true crime do well); ~11,966 authors had used it as of May 2025 — [Self-Publishing.com, BookSirens](https://selfpublishing.com/booksirens/); [TCK Publishing BookSirens review](https://www.tckpublishing.com/booksirens-review/)
- Booksprout: $10–$30/month subscription with a larger reader base — [Medium, ARC platforms guide](https://medium.com/@scrivy/arc-review-platforms-your-book-launch-survival-guide-92d4312a9df0)
- Goodreads Giveaways: Standard $119 (up to 100 Kindle or print copies), Premium $599 with Featured placement; Kindle giveaways cost the flat fee regardless of winners, delivered automatically — [Goodreads blog](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/1108); [Author Media](https://www.authormedia.com/?p=767)

**Social video**
- #BookTok had 370 billion views and 52 million+ creations as of 2025; publicized self-published breakouts (Jeneane O'Riley, Alex Aster) are fiction — [First Writer, BookTok](https://www.firstwriter.com/articles/?GUID=4079); [BYU editing research, Dec 2025](https://editingresearch.byu.edu/2025/12/05/how-booktok-is-changing-marketing-for-publishers)
- Italian Publishers Association study: 14% of readers say social media influences book choice (22% of 18–24s); among those, 54% rely on Instagram, TikTok fourth at 26% — [First Writer](https://firstwriter.com/articles/?GUID=3981)

### Inferences
- For a small-audience first-time author the evidence-backed order of operations is: (1) start the email list before launch (reader magnet, e.g., a free birth-chart cheat-sheet), (2) seed 10–25 ARC reviews via BookSirens/Booksprout + personal network, (3) launch at $0.99–$2.99 for 5–7 days stacked with cheap Written Word Media–tier promos ($25–$240), (4) run low-bid ($0.15–$0.40) Amazon Sponsored Products on competitor-title and "astrology for beginners"-type keywords once ~15+ reviews exist, (5) apply for a BookBub Featured Deal (Religion & Spirituality, $353+) only once the book has review social proof, (6) use Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts as list-building rather than direct-sales channels.
- Nonfiction conversion from newsletter promos is likely lower than the fiction benchmarks quoted; budget on the low end of BookBub's "250–6,550 purchases" range and treat the fiction numbers as ceilings.

### Gaps
- No nonfiction- or spirituality-specific average downloads/sales for BookBub or Written Word Media promos were found (category averages are on BookBub's pricing page, which was blocked).
- No controlled data on Instagram Reels / YouTube effect for nonfiction authors.
- Freebooksy (free-book) pricing by genre for 2025 not found.

## Key Question 5: How does Amazon's AI-content disclosure work in 2025–2026, and is there evidence it affects ranking or reader trust?

### Takeaway
Since September 2023 KDP requires disclosure of AI-*generated* text, images or translations (even if heavily edited) but not AI-*assisted* work (brainstorming, outlining, editing, fact-checking); the disclosure is internal to Amazon and not shown to shoppers, so it has no documented effect on ranking, royalty or categories. Enforcement for non-disclosure (title removal, account action) is reported to have intensified in 2025–2026.

### Cited Findings
- Disclosure required if any portion of the book was AI-generated: text (even if substantially edited), cover/interior images, or translations; not required for AI brainstorming, outlines, titles, or AI used to edit/refine/fact-check your own writing — [Scribecount, KDP AI Content Policy compliance guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide); [KDPBuilder, AI disclosure rules](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/kdp-ai-disclosure-rules)
- "The disclosure checkbox is not visible to readers and is internal Amazon records only, and does not affect royalty rate, pricing, search ranking, or categories" — [Scribecount compliance guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- Skipping a required disclosure is treated as a policy violation, not a paperwork oversight; Amazon can remove a title without prior warning — [Univers Studio, KDP AI policy 2026](https://univers.studio/blog/kdp-ai-content-policy-2026/)
- Enforcement "significantly ramped up in 2025 and has continued into 2026, with authors having books removed and accounts suspended for non-disclosure" — [Writeo, "Amazon Is Actually Enforcing Its AI Disclosure Rule Now"](https://writeo.app/news/amazon-kdp-ai-disclosure-enforcement-2026)
- Alongside the AI policy, Amazon capped new-title uploads at three per day per account (Authors Guild coverage of the September 2023 changes) — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/)
- Related: KDP's own help covers content guidelines on what can and cannot be published — [Vappingo, KDP Content Guidelines](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-content-guidelines/)

### Inferences
- For an astrology author using AI tools for research, outlining and line-editing of self-written text, no disclosure is required; if any chapter text, chart illustrations or the cover is AI-generated, tick the disclosure — there is no visibility penalty for doing so, but a real removal risk for not doing so.
- Reader-trust effects are therefore moot at the listing level (readers cannot see the flag); trust risk comes instead from visible quality signals (reviews mentioning "AI-sounding" prose) and from off-Amazon exposure.

### Gaps
- No empirical study of ranking or sales differences between disclosed and undisclosed titles was found (and given the flag is non-public, none is likely to exist).
- Could not read KDP's primary policy page or the Authors Guild article directly to confirm exact current wording.

## Key Question 6: Cheapest credible path to paperback, hardcover and audiobook editions — and are they worth it in this niche?

### Takeaway
KDP print-on-demand is zero-upfront: a 300-page black-ink US paperback costs ~$4.45 to print and the same hardcover ~$9.25, but KDP has no print facility in India, so Indian paperback buyers face slow imports (use Pothi/Notion Press locally). Audio with no upfront cost is either Virtual Voice (free AI narration, $3.99–$14.99 price band, 40% royalty, US marketplace only) or ACX royalty share (50/50 with narrator, 7-year Audible exclusive); ACX moved to a 50% exclusive / 30% non-exclusive "member value" model for new titles from 26 May 2026 (reported, unverified).

### Cited Findings
**Print**
- KDP printing cost = fixed cost + per-page cost, varying by marketplace, ink and trim — [KDP help GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H)
- US black-ink paperback: $2.15 fixed for 24–108 pages; $2.15 + $0.013/page above 108 pages (one source); another worked example gives a 300-page 5.5×8.5 black-ink paperback at ~$4.45 ($0.85 fixed + $0.012/page) and the same hardcover at ~$9.25 ($5.65 fixed + $0.012/page); hardcover 75–108 pages: $6.80 flat (US) / £5.23 (UK). Color paperback ≈ $6.00 fixed + $0.065/page — [Vappingo, KDP Print Royalties](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-print-royalties/); [Vappingo, KDP Hardcover](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11963); [Scribecount, hardcover](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-hardcover-amazon-kdp); [Ghostwriting LLC](https://ghostwritingllc.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-print-a-paperback-book-on-amazon-kdp/). *Note: the two paperback fixed-cost figures ($2.15 vs $0.85) conflict; KDP's own table could not be read to resolve it.*
- Hardcover vs paperback trade-off and formatting requirements are covered by Scribecount and Vappingo (hardcover "roughly double" the print cost) — [Scribecount hardcover guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-on-amazon/publishing-hardcover-amazon-kdp)
- India: "KDP has no print centre in India. A KDP paperback prints abroad and reaches Indian buyers only as a slow, expensive import"; recommendation is to use a local POD (Pothi, Notion Press) for Indian print and keep KDP for Kindle — [KDP India, how to self-publish a paperback](https://kdpindia.com/how-to-self-publish-a-paperback/); [Goodreads blog on POD costs for Indian authors](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/26115569-how-much-does-print-on-demand-or-pod-really-cost-for-indian-authors)
- Professionally "published" self-publishing packages in India run ₹20,000–₹75,000 (not needed for KDP DIY) — [OrangeBooks, self-publishing cost in India](https://www.orangebooks.in/blog/self-publishing-cost-in-india)

**Audio — Virtual Voice**
- KDP pays a 40% royalty for Virtual Voice audiobooks sold on Amazon and Audible; price band locked to $3.99–$14.99 — [KDP help GWHSFAK69P2537YR](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GWHSFAK69P2537YR); [Narration Box, Virtual Voice vs custom AI](https://narrationbox.com/blog/audible-virtual-voice-vs-custom-ai-narration)
- Eligibility: live Kindle ebook (live ≥7 days), table of contents, English primary language, no existing audiobook, under ~240k words; distribution reaches Audible, Amazon, Alexa and Amazon Music Unlimited; "only available for books to be distributed in the US marketplace at this time" — [KDP help GJSXT4GZLP4PL62B](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJSXT4GZLP4PL62B); [GetResponse, Virtual Voice guide](https://www.getresponse.com/blog/virtual-voice-audible-audiobooks)
- Scale: beta began late 2023 for select US self-published authors; 40,000+ Virtual Voice titles on Audible by mid-2024; Amazon has since expanded to select publishers — [Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/amazon-expands-audibles-ai-virtual-voice-narrators-to-broaden-access-to-audiobooks/); [Good e-Reader](https://goodereader.com/blog/e-book-news/kindle-direct-publishing-launches-ebooks-to-audiobooks-using-ai-voices)

**Audio — ACX / Findaway**
- ACX royalty share: no upfront payment; 50/50 split of royalties with the narrator for a 7-year Audible-exclusive term; royalty-share titles cannot convert to non-exclusive (pay-for-production titles can after 90 days) — [Vappingo, KDP and Audiobook Publishing](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-audiobook-publishing/); [Scribecount, ACX and Audible](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/publishing-wide/acx-audible)
- Historical ACX rates: 40% exclusive / 25% non-exclusive — [Daniel J. Tortora, ACX royalties](https://danieljtortora.com/blog/acx-audiobook-royalties-get-paid)
- Reported change: from 26 May 2026 all newly claimed titles are on Audible's new model paying 50% (exclusive) / 30% (non-exclusive) of "Member Value" (listener subscription fee + extra credits, allocated across what they listened to) — [Narration Box, voice cloning & ACX royalty share](https://narrationbox.com/blog/voice-cloning-acx-royalty-share-indie-authors); [Audible podcast "KB's SelfPub Empire" bonus episode](https://www.audible.co.uk/podcast/KBs-SelfPubEmpire/episodes/B0CCR56NT5). *Unverified against ACX's own announcement in this session.*
- Wide audio: Findaway Voices distributes to Spotify (ACX does not); non-Spotify wide distribution has transitioned from Findaway Voices to "Voices by INaudio" — [Vappingo, audiobook options](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-audiobook-publishing/)
- UK/non-US ACX access and tax considerations are documented separately — [Publishing.co.uk, ACX for UK authors](https://publishing.co.uk/guides/audiobook-acx-uk-authors/)

### Inferences
- Zero-cash path: Kindle (KDP) → KDP paperback for US/UK (~$4.45 print cost on 300 pages lets a $14.99–$16.99 list price clear a few dollars at 60% royalty minus print) → KDP hardcover only if a "gift/reference" positioning justifies $24.99+ → Virtual Voice for US audio (free, fast, 40%) if the ebook is on Amazon.com; use ACX royalty share only if a human narrator matters for the niche's "voice" (spiritual content often does) and you accept 7-year exclusivity.
- Virtual Voice's US-only distribution and the uncertain eligibility for India-based accounts is a material constraint for this author; it was not possible to confirm whether non-US KDP accounts can enable it for their US-marketplace listing.
- Worth it? Print is likely worth it for workbook/journal companions and reference tables (astrology readers annotate); audio is lower priority for chart-heavy content but reasonable for a narrative "beginner's guide."

### Gaps
- Could not read KDP's print cost table to reconcile the conflicting paperback fixed-cost figures or confirm UK/India figures.
- Whether KDP accounts registered in India can use Virtual Voice (even for the US store) is unconfirmed.
- No data on audiobook sales for spirituality/astrology nonfiction specifically.

## Supplementary: Review velocity, cover/title conventions, and India-specific KDP points

### Takeaway
There is no official Amazon review threshold for ranking; practitioner heuristics cite 25/50/100 as promo-site minimums and ~50 reviews as the point where ads convert well, with recent review velocity and a ≥4.5-star average mattering more than totals for new releases. For India, KDP pays in rupees via direct deposit (7–10 business days after payment date), the India 70% band is ₹99–₹449 (reportedly ₹99–₹599 from July 2026) and requires Select, GST is deducted before royalty, and KU India costs ₹199/month (implying a lower India KENP rate, which Amazon does not publish).

### Cited Findings
**Reviews**
- Thresholds like 25, 50 and 100 reviews are "commonly cited promo minimums, not official Amazon rules"; ~50 reviews is cited as the level at which Amazon ad campaigns succeed — [BookWriter.vip review velocity tracker](https://www.bookwriter.vip/review-velocity-tracker)
- Claim that Amazon "requires at least 100 reviews to list books in search results for your topic" appears in the same practitioner source; this is *not* corroborated by Amazon and should be treated as a marketing heuristic — [BookWriter.vip](https://www.bookwriter.vip/review-velocity-tracker)
- For new products/books, review velocity and strong ratings weigh more than total count; recent review activity is read as momentum; practitioners target ≥4.5 stars and a steady drip rather than a big stale total — [eComEngine, review velocity (Feb 2026)](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-review-velocity); [eComEngine, reviews and BSR (Sep 2025)](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/reviews-impact-bestseller-rank); [Seller Labs](https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/review-velocity-vs-review-count-what-matters-more/)
- Policy: authors may give free ARC copies, but a review cannot be required or conditioned on the copy or rating; paying for reviews, review swaps/groups and family reviews are prohibited (consistent since Amazon's 2016 incentivized-review ban, with the book ARC exception) — [Kindlepreneur podcast E36 on Amazon review policy](https://kindlepreneur.com/e36-amazon-book-review-policies-can-we-give-a-book-for-free-for-reviews/); [Vappingo, ARC readers & launch team](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/arc-readers-launch-team/); [BookWriter.vip policy checker](https://www.bookwriter.vip/amazon-review-policy-checker)

**Title/cover conventions (nonfiction, spirituality)**
- Nonfiction titles use a short main title plus a descriptive, keyword-bearing subtitle after a colon (e.g., *Holistic Tarot: An Integrative Approach to Using Tarot for Personal Growth*); best keywords should be worked into the title/subtitle where natural — [Benebell Wen, self-publishing logistics workbook](https://benebellwen.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/module-4-workbook-h-self-publishing-logistics.pdf)
- Spiritual self-help cover briefs on design marketplaces specify light/energy fields, sacred geometry, cosmic/mystical elements conveying transformation; "expansive, sacred, mystical, empowering" tone — [DesignCrowd brief, Soul Alchemist](https://www.designcrowd.com/bookcover-design/contest/4098133); [99designs spiritual cover inspiration](https://99designs.com/inspiration/designs/spiritual)
- Observed 2026 astrology title formula: "[Sign/Complete] Horoscope 2026: [The Complete Yearly Astrology Guide for Every Zodiac Sign / Your Monthly Astrology Guide with Moon Phases and Lunar Days]" — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/topic/list_book/243864957); [Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/277566)

**India-specific KDP**
- India 70% band: previously ₹99–₹399, then ₹99–₹449 inclusive of GST; a search snippet reports an update to ₹99–₹599 effective 7 July 2026 (*unverified*); KDP deducts GST from the list price before calculating royalty; India 70% requires KDP Select — [KDP help, Setting List Prices (G202169970)](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202169970); [KDP help G200634560](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200634560); [KDP India, publishing a Kindle ebook](https://kdpindia.com/how-to-publish-a-kindle-ebook/)
- Indian authors can set India-specific prices and receive royalties in rupees; KU India launched at ₹199 (~$3.00)/month — [Pratham Books on Kindle India launch](https://prathambooks.org/amazon-launches-kindle-store-for-india/amp/); [KDP help G6BZ4M9PVJ8YSCW8](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G6BZ4M9PVJ8YSCW8)
- Payments: direct deposit (EFT) usually has no minimum threshold and is the recommended method; for banks in India, direct deposits can take 7–10 business days after the payment date; some Indian banks require extra documentation to release international electronic payments; royalties are paid monthly ~60 days after the month of sale; wire and check have minimum thresholds and fees — [KDP help GJD7DFFX6X88AK46](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46); [KDP help GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ); [KDP help G4EQZQ73RYEX8RQQ](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4EQZQ73RYEX8RQQ)
- KENP payouts "per country will differ based on local country factors" — the India rate is set from India's share of the fund and is only visible in the author's own KDP reports — [Just Publishing Advice](https://justpublishingadvice.com/what-is-kenp/)

### Inferences
- A sensible first-book review target is 15–25 verified reviews in the first 30 days (from ARC + launch list), then sustained velocity of a few per month, rather than chasing 50–100 before advertising.
- For the India market, an English astrology ebook priced ₹199–₹299 (inside the 70% band, Select-enrolled) is the practical sweet spot; expect India KENP per page to be materially below the US ~$0.0045–0.005 given the ₹199 subscription price, and expect India print to be out of KDP's reach (use Pothi/Notion Press or skip).

### Gaps
- The India KENP rate in rupees/paise for 2025–2026 is not published by Amazon and did not surface in any search.
- No source quantified typical English-language Kindle ebook price points in India for nonfiction.
- Could not confirm the ₹599 band change or the $12.99 US ceiling change against KDP's primary pricing page.
