# Appendix B: Glossary (Sanskrit to Plain English)

Every technical word used in this book, in alphabetical order, with the plain-English meaning first and the chapter that treats it in brackets. Alternative spellings you will meet in other books and apps are given after the term. When a definition here seems to disagree with something you read elsewhere, remember that spellings and even meanings drift between schools; Appendix A holds the numbers this book uses.

Before the alphabet, the cast. Every story in this book uses the same nine characters, and the glossary is easier to read if you have them in mind.

> **📖 Story: The court in one paragraph.** A King (Sun) rules a kingdom of twelve districts (the signs) from a haveli of twelve rooms (the houses). His Queen Mother (Moon) runs the household and its moods. His Commander (Mars) guards the borders and loses his temper. His clever young Prince and secretary (Mercury) writes the letters and counts the money. Guru-ji, the Royal Priest and Minister (Jupiter), advises, blesses and teaches. Shukracharya, the Minister of Arts and Pleasure (Venus), keeps the court beautiful and the King distracted. Old Shani-ji, once a servant and now the Judge of Labour (Saturn), moves slowly and forgets nothing. At the gate stand a Smoke-headed Stranger (Rahu), hungry for everything, and a Headless Sadhu (Ketu), hungry for nothing. Royal Party: King, Queen Mother, Commander, Priest. People's Party: Prince, Minister of Pleasure, Judge. The streets of the districts are the nakshatras; the railway timetable that says when each courtier's turn comes is the dasha. **Rule:** Planet = who; sign = how (which district); house = where (which room); nakshatra = which street; dasha = when.

|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | The King (Raja) | 🔴 Mild malefic | Royal |

| Mars | The Commander (Senapati) | 🔴 Malefic | Royal |

Colour dots throughout the book: 🟢 benefic, friend or good; 🔴 malefic, enemy or difficult; 🟡 neutral or mixed. In the chart diagrams the planets keep fixed colours: the Sun orange, the Moon grey, Mars red, Mercury green, Jupiter gold, Venus pink, Saturn navy, Rahu and Ketu purple.

## A

- **Abhijit**: A 28th "star" used only for choosing auspicious times: the last quarter of Uttara Ashadha plus the first 4° of Shravana. Not used in dashas. (Ch 16)
- **Adhi yoga**: Benefic planets in the 6th, 7th and 8th houses counted from the Moon; a combination for leadership and prosperity. (Ch 10)
- **Amala yoga**: A single benefic planet alone in the 10th house from the Lagna or the Moon; a spotless reputation. (Ch 10)
- **Amanta / Purnimanta**: Two ways of ending the lunar month: at the new moon (Amanta, used in the south and west of India) or at the full moon (Purnimanta, used in the north). The same festival can fall in differently named months. (Ch 16)
- **Amatyakaraka (AmK)**: In Jaimini's method, the planet with the second-highest degree in any sign; the "minister", read for career. (Ch 15)
- **Amavasya**: The new-moon day, when the Sun and Moon share the same degree; the last day of the dark fortnight. (Ch 16)
- **Andha graha**: "Blind planet": a Lal Kitab term for a planet placed where it cannot act on its own matters. Rules vary by edition. (Ch 22)
- **Antardasha (Bhukti)**: A sub-period inside a major planetary period; each Mahadasha is divided into nine Antardashas in fixed proportion. (Ch 13)
- **Apoklima**: The 3rd, 6th, 9th and 12th houses, traditionally the weaker "cadent" positions (the 9th excepted). (Ch 4)
- **Arudha (Arudha Lagna, AL)**: In Jaimini's method, the image or reflection of a house: count from a house to its lord, then the same distance again. Shows how the world *sees* you rather than what you are. (Ch 15)
- **Ashtakavarga**: A point-scoring system giving every sign a score of 0–8 for each planet, based on where the seven planets and Lagna stand; used to judge transits and strength. (Ch 12)
- **Ashtakoota**: The eight-part, 36-point compatibility test for marriage, comparing the two Moons. (Ch 19)
- **Ashtama Shani**: Saturn transiting the 8th sign from the natal Moon, about 2½ years of heaviness. (Ch 14)
- **Ashtottari dasha**: A 108-year conditional dasha system used by some schools when Rahu is in a kendra or trikona from the Lagna lord. (Ch 15)
- **Asta (combustion)**: A planet so close to the Sun (within a set number of degrees) that it is "burnt" and loses strength. Orbs are in Appendix A.2. (Ch 3, 12)
- **Atmakaraka (AK)**: In Jaimini's method, the planet with the highest degree in its sign; the "soul planet", the chart's chief significator. (Ch 15)
- **Avastha**: The "state" of a planet. Three families: **Baladi** (by degree: infant, youth, adult, old, dead), **Jagradadi** (awake, dreaming, asleep, by dignity) and **Deeptadi** (nine moods such as radiant, delighted, tormented, by placement). (Ch 12)
- **Ayanamsa**: The angular gap between the Western (tropical) zodiac and the star-based (sidereal) zodiac, about 24° today. Indian practice uses the Lahiri value. (Ch 1)
- **Ayurdaya**: Longevity assessment. Three classical bands: **Alpayu** (short, to about 32), **Madhyayu** (middle, to about 64) and **Purnayu** (full, beyond). Judged with caution and never spoken to a client as a date. (Ch 20)

## B

- **Badhaka (Badhakesh)**: The "obstructing" house and its lord: the 11th for movable signs, the 9th for fixed, the 7th for dual. Used for hidden obstacles. (Ch 9)
- **Balarishta**: Combinations indicating danger to health in early childhood; read with restraint and cancellation rules. (Ch 20)
- **Beeja mantra**: The "seed" sound-formula of a planet, such as *Om Hraam Hreem Hraum Sah Suryaya Namah* for the Sun. (Ch 23)
- **Bhakoot**: The seventh test in Ashtakoota (7 points): the distance between the two Moon signs, read for family welfare and finances. (Ch 19)
- **Bhava**: A house: one of twelve areas of life counted from the Lagna. (Ch 4)
- **Bhava Chalit**: A chart in which planets are placed by house cusps (house midpoints and boundaries) rather than by whole signs, so a planet near a boundary may shift house. (Ch 5)
- **Bhava Madhya (cusp)**: The midpoint or most powerful degree of a house in cusp-based systems. (Ch 5)
- **Bhavat Bhavam**: "House from house": the nth house from the nth house is a second indicator of that matter (the 4th from the 4th, the 7th, also shows the mother). (Ch 4)
- **Bindu**: A point or dot; in Ashtakavarga, one unit of the score. (Ch 12)
- **BPHS**: *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*, the foundational Sanskrit text attributed to the sage Parashara; the backbone of this book. (Ch 1)
- **Brihat Jataka**: Varahamihira's classic text (6th century CE, Ujjain); compact and authoritative. (Ch 1)
- **Budha-Aditya yoga**: Sun and Mercury together (Mercury not combust): intelligence and articulate speech. (Ch 10)

## C

- **Chandra Bala**: The Moon's transit strength for a given person on a given day, judged by the transiting Moon's house from the natal Moon; used in choosing times. (Ch 16)
- **Chandra Lagna**: The Moon's sign treated as the 1st house; the chart read "from the Moon". (Ch 5)
- **Chandra-Mangala yoga**: Moon and Mars together or opposite: drive and earning capacity. (Ch 10)
- **Chandrashtama**: The roughly 2¼ days each month when the transiting Moon is in the 8th sign from the natal Moon; a low-energy day, avoided for beginnings. (Ch 16)
- **Chara / Sthira / Dwiswabhava**: The three sign qualities: movable (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn), fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) and dual (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces). (Ch 2)
- **Chara dasha**: Jaimini's sign-based dasha system, in which signs rather than planets rule periods. (Ch 15)
- **Cusp**: See Bhava Madhya.

## D

- **Daan (dana)**: Donation; giving away a planet's items to pacify it. (Ch 23)
- **Darakaraka (DK)**: In Jaimini's method, the planet with the lowest degree; the spouse significator. (Ch 15)
- **Daridra yoga**: Combinations for poverty, typically the 11th lord in a dusthana or malefics afflicting the wealth houses; read with the whole chart. (Ch 10)
- **Dasamsa (D10)**: The tenth divisional chart, each sign split into ten parts of 3°; read for career and public life. (Ch 11)
- **Dasha**: A planetary period: the system by which the chart's promises are timed. (Ch 13)
- **Dhaiya**: Saturn's 2½-year transit through the 4th or 8th sign from the natal Moon (see Kantaka and Ashtama Shani). (Ch 14)
- **Dhana yoga**: Wealth combinations: connections between the lords of the 2nd, 5th, 9th, 11th and the Lagna. (Ch 10, 18)
- **Dharmi graha**: "Righteous planet": a Lal Kitab term for a planet that, by placement, does no harm and should not be "punished" with a remedy. Rules vary by edition. (Ch 22)
- **Dig Bala**: Directional strength: Jupiter and Mercury strongest in the 1st, Moon and Venus in the 4th, Saturn in the 7th, Sun and Mars in the 10th. (Ch 12)
- **Disha Shool**: "Direction thorn": the direction traditionally avoided for starting a journey on each weekday. (Ch 16)
- **Dispositor**: The lord of the sign a planet sits in; the planet's "landlord". A chain of dispositors shows who ultimately controls a placement. (Ch 9)
- **Drekkana (D3)**: The third divisional chart, each sign in three parts of 10°; siblings, courage, and the manner of death. (Ch 11)
- **Drishti**: Aspect: the "glance" one planet casts on a house or planet at fixed distances. (Ch 6)
- **Dusthana (Trik)**: The difficult houses 6, 8 and 12: struggle, hidden matters, loss. (Ch 4)

## E

- **Ecliptic**: The Sun's apparent yearly path across the sky; the zodiac belt is centred on it. (Ch 1)
- **Eclipse**: The Sun or Moon darkened when the Moon is near Rahu or Ketu at new or full moon; astrologically a sensitising event. (Ch 3)
- **Ekadashi**: The eleventh lunar day of each fortnight, a traditional fasting day for Vishnu. (Ch 23)
- **Exaltation / Debilitation degrees**: The exact degree of a planet's peak (Uccha) and trough (Neecha) within its exaltation and debilitation signs; listed in Appendix A.2. (Ch 3)

## G

- **Gaja Kesari yoga**: Jupiter in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Moon; fame, wisdom, protection. (Ch 10)
- **Gana**: The sixth Ashtakoota test (6 points): each nakshatra is Deva (godly), Manushya (human) or Rakshasa (fierce); temperaments are compared. (Ch 7, 19)
- **Gandanta**: The "knot" junctions where a water sign meets a fire sign (end of Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces; start of Leo, Sagittarius, Aries), each 3°20′ either side; sensitive zones for the Moon and Lagna. (Ch 7)
- **Gandmool (Gand Mool nakshatra)**: The six nakshatras owned by Mercury and Ketu (Ashwini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati) that contain the Gandanta zones; a Moon here traditionally calls for a shanti ritual. (Ch 7)
- **Gochar (Gochara)**: Transit: the current positions of the planets moving over the birth chart. (Ch 14)
- **Graha**: "That which grasps": one of the nine planets: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu. (Ch 3)
- **Graha Maitri**: The fifth Ashtakoota test (5 points): friendship between the lords of the two Moon signs. Also the general word for planetary friendship. (Ch 3, 19)
- **Graha Yuddha**: Planetary war: two true planets (not Sun, Moon or nodes) within one degree of each other; the "loser" is weakened. (Ch 12)
- **Guru Chandala yoga**: Jupiter joined with Rahu (some include Ketu); tests of ethics and unconventional wisdom. (Ch 10)

## H

- **Hora**: (1) A planetary hour, one of 24 in a day. (2) The second divisional chart (D2), each sign split into two halves ruled by the Sun and Moon; read for wealth. (3) "Hora Shastra": the predictive branch of Jyotisha. (Ch 11, 16)
- **Hora Lagna (HL)**: A special ascendant in Jaimini's method, advancing one sign every 2½ hours from sunrise; read for wealth. (Ch 15)
- **Hora Shastra**: The branch of Jyotisha dealing with birth charts and prediction, as opposed to calendar and omen lore. (Ch 1)

## I

- **Indu Lagna**: A wealth ascendant computed from the lords of the 9th houses from the Lagna and the Moon. (Ch 18)
- **Ishrafa**: In Tajaka, a separating aspect: the faster planet has already passed the slower; the matter is over. (Ch 15)
- **Ithasala**: In Tajaka, an applying aspect: the faster planet moves toward the slower; the matter will come about. (Ch 15)

## J

- **Jaimini**: A parallel school of astrology attributed to the sage Jaimini, using sign aspects, karakas by degree and sign-based dashas. Appears in this book only where marked. (Ch 15)
- **Janma Patrika**: See Kundali.
- **Japa**: Repetition of a mantra, usually counted on a mala of 108 beads. (Ch 23)
- **Jataka**: A birth chart, or the branch of astrology dealing with birth charts. (Ch 1)
- **Jataka Parijata**: Vaidyanatha Dikshita's classical compendium (c. 15th century). (Ch 1)
- **Jyotisha (Jyotish)**: "The science of light": Indian astrology and astronomy, one of the six limbs of the Veda. (Ch 1)

## K

- **Kala Chakra dasha**: A nakshatra-based dasha system with its own sequence of signs and periods; specialised and rarely used by beginners. (Ch 15)
- **Kala Purusha**: The "cosmic man": the zodiac laid over a human body, Aries at the head to Pisces at the feet; the basis of medical astrology. (Ch 2, 20)
- **Kala Sarpa yoga**: All seven planets hemmed on one side of the Rahu–Ketu axis; a fated, karmic life pattern. Popular teaching, not in BPHS. (Ch 10)
- **Kantaka Shani**: Saturn transiting the 4th (some say also the 7th or 10th) from the Moon or Lagna; the "thorn" period. (Ch 14)
- **Karaka**: A significator: the planet that naturally stands for a matter (Venus for marriage, Jupiter for children). (Ch 3, 4)
- **Karakamsa**: In Jaimini's method, the Navamsa sign occupied by the Atmakaraka; read for the soul's direction and career. (Ch 15)
- **Karakatwa**: The list of things a planet signifies; its portfolio. (Ch 3)
- **Karana**: Half a lunar day; one of the five limbs of the Panchanga, with eleven kinds. (Ch 16)
- **Karma (Prarabdha / Sanchita / Kriyamana)**: Action and its results. **Sanchita** is the whole store of past karma; **Prarabdha** the portion ripened for this life (what the chart shows); **Kriyamana** what you do now (which the chart cannot show). (Ch 1)
- **Kemadruma yoga**: No planet (other than the Sun, Rahu or Ketu) in the 2nd or 12th from the Moon; loneliness, cancelled by many conditions. (Ch 10)
- **Kendra**: The angular houses 1, 4, 7 and 10; the pillars of the chart. (Ch 4)
- **KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati)**: A modern system (K. S. Krishnamurti, 20th century) using the nakshatra sub-lord of cusps and planets for precise event prediction. (Ch 17)
- **Krishna paksha**: The dark fortnight, from full moon to new moon. (Ch 16)
- **Krura / Saumya graha**: Cruel and gentle planets: the natural malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, waning Moon, afflicted Mercury) and benefics (Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, unafflicted Mercury). (Ch 3)
- **Kuja dosha (Mangal dosha, Manglik)**: Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from the Lagna (also checked from the Moon and Venus); friction in marriage, with many cancellations. (Ch 10, 19)
- **Kundali (Janma Patrika, Kundli)**: The birth chart: the map of the sky at the moment and place of birth. (Ch 1)

## L

- **Lagna**: The rising sign, or ascendant: the sign on the eastern horizon at birth, which becomes the 1st house. (Ch 1, 5)
- **Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa**: The ayanamsa value adopted by the Indian Calendar Reform Committee in 1955 and used by most Indian astrologers. (Ch 1)
- **Lakshmi yoga**: The 9th lord strong in a kendra, own or exalted sign, with a strong Lagna lord; wealth and grace. (Ch 10)
- **Lal Kitab**: "The Red Book": a distinct remedy-centred system by Pt. Roop Chand Joshi (five Urdu volumes, 1939–1952), using a fixed-house chart. Appears only in marked boxes and in Chapter 22. (Ch 22)

## M

- **Mahadasha**: A major planetary period in Vimshottari (6 to 20 years long). (Ch 13)
- **Mangal Nek / Mangal Bad**: Lal Kitab's benefic and malefic forms of Mars. (Ch 22)
- **Maraka**: A "killer" house or planet: the 2nd and 7th houses and their lords, whose periods can bring health crises. Spoken of only with great care. (Ch 4, 20)
- **Moolatrikona**: A planet's "root-triangle" portion of a sign, stronger than its own sign but weaker than exaltation; degree ranges in Appendix A.2. (Ch 3)
- **Moorti Nirnaya**: Judging the quality of a transit (gold, silver, copper, iron) by where the transiting Moon stands from the natal Moon at the moment a planet enters a new sign. (Ch 14)
- **Mudda dasha**: The planetary periods within a Tajaka annual chart, dividing the year. (Ch 15)
- **Muhurta**: Electional astrology: choosing an auspicious time to begin something. (Ch 16)
- **Muntha**: In Tajaka, a point that advances one sign per year from the birth Lagna; its house in the annual chart colours the year. (Ch 15)

## N

- **Nadi**: The eighth Ashtakoota test (8 points): the nakshatras' three "pulses" (Adi, Madhya, Antya); same nadi is the main objection. (Ch 19)
- **Naisargika / Tatkalika / Panchadha Maitri**: Natural friendship (fixed), temporary friendship (by position in this chart), and the five-fold combination of the two. (Ch 3)
- **Nakshatra**: One of 27 lunar mansions of 13°20′ each; the Moon's nakshatra at birth starts the Vimshottari dasha. (Ch 7)
- **Navagraha**: The nine planets as a group, and their worship. (Ch 23)
- **Navamsa (D9)**: The ninth divisional chart, each sign split into nine parts of 3°20′; marriage, dharma and the inner strength of planets. The most important varga. (Ch 11)
- **Neecha**: Debilitation: the sign where a planet is weakest. (Ch 3)
- **Neecha Bhanga**: Cancellation of debilitation by specific conditions (for example the sign lord in a kendra from the Moon); may give late strength. (Ch 10)
- **Nirayana**: Sidereal, star-fixed: the zodiac used in Jyotisha. (Ch 1)
- **Node**: Rahu (north) and Ketu (south): the two points where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's path. (Ch 3)

## P

- **Pada**: One quarter of a nakshatra, 3°20′; each pada corresponds to one Navamsa sign. (Ch 7)
- **Pakka ghar**: A planet's "permanent house" in Lal Kitab. (Ch 22)
- **Paksha**: A lunar fortnight: bright (Shukla) or dark (Krishna). (Ch 16)
- **Panapara**: The 2nd, 5th, 8th and 11th houses; medium strength. (Ch 4)
- **Panch Mahapurusha yogas**: Five "great person" combinations: a planet in its own or exalted sign in a kendra: **Ruchaka** (Mars), **Bhadra** (Mercury), **Hamsa** (Jupiter), **Malavya** (Venus), **Shasha** (Saturn). (Ch 10)
- **Panchanga**: The five-limbed Hindu almanac: tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga, karana. (Ch 16)
- **Parivartana**: Exchange: two planets each sitting in the other's sign; the two houses fuse. (Ch 9, 10)
- **Phaladeepika**: Mantreswara's classical text (c. 13th–16th century), rich in yogas and results. (Ch 1)
- **Pitru dosha**: An affliction of the ancestral line, read chiefly from the Sun, the 9th house and Rahu–Ketu with the Sun; a Parashari-tradition concept distinct from Lal Kitab's *pitru rin*. (Ch 21)
- **Pitta / Kapha / Vata**: The three Ayurvedic constitutions (fire, water-earth, air), associated with planets and signs in medical astrology. (Ch 20)
- **Prarabdha**: See Karma.
- **Prashna**: Horary astrology: answering a question from the chart of the moment it is asked. (Ch 17)
- **Prashna Marga**: A Kerala text on horary and remedial astrology (17th century). (Ch 17)
- **Pratyantar dasha**: The third level of Vimshottari sub-period, inside an Antardasha. (Ch 13)
- **Pravrajya yoga**: Combinations for renunciation, typically four or more planets in one sign or a strong Saturn influence on the 10th; the monk's chart. (Ch 10, 21)
- **Precession**: The slow backward drift of the equinox point against the stars (about 1° in 72 years), which creates the ayanamsa. (Ch 1)
- **Purnima**: The full-moon day. (Ch 16)

## R

- **Rahu Kaal**: A roughly 1½-hour slot each day, ruled by Rahu, avoided for beginnings. (Ch 16)
- **Raja yoga**: Combinations for rise in status, classically the lords of a kendra and a trikona joined, in mutual aspect or exchanging signs. (Ch 10)
- **Rashi**: A sign of the zodiac: one of twelve 30° divisions. (Ch 2)
- **Ratna**: Gemstone, as a remedy. (Ch 23)
- **Rin**: "Debt": Lal Kitab's family-level karmic debts with collective remedies. (Ch 22)
- **Rudraksha**: Beads of the *Elaeocarpus* tree, sacred to Shiva, classified by faces (mukhi) and matched to planets. (Ch 23)
- **Ruling planets**: In KP, the planets ruling the moment of judgement (day lord, Moon's sign and star lords, Lagna's sign and star lords), used to time events. (Ch 17)

## S

- **Sade Sati**: Saturn's roughly 7½-year transit through the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon. (Ch 14)
- **Saka calendar**: India's official civil-era calendar, beginning in 78 CE; the year printed on government almanacs. (Ch 16)
- **Sambandha**: Relationship between two planets: by conjunction, mutual aspect, exchange, or one aspecting the other's sign. (Ch 6, 9)
- **Samhita**: The branch of Jyotisha dealing with omens, weather, and the fortunes of nations, as opposed to individual charts. (Ch 1)
- **Sandhi**: A junction: the last degrees of a sign or house and the first of the next, where a planet is unstable. (Ch 5)
- **Sankalpa**: The spoken intention at the start of a remedy or ritual. (Ch 23)
- **Saptamsa (D7)**: The seventh divisional chart, each sign in seven parts; children. (Ch 11)
- **Saravali**: Kalyana Varma's classical text (c. 8th–10th century). (Ch 1)
- **Saraswati yoga**: Jupiter, Venus and Mercury in kendras, trikonas or the 2nd, with Jupiter strong; learning and the arts. (Ch 10)
- **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)**: The total Ashtakavarga score of each sign, all planets combined (out of 337); above 28 is comfortable, below 25 weak. (Ch 12)
- **Sayana**: Tropical, equinox-based: the zodiac used in Western astrology. (Ch 1)
- **Shadbala**: The six-fold strength calculation for planets (positional, directional, temporal, motional, natural, aspectual), expressed in units called rupas. (Ch 12)
- **Shakata yoga**: Jupiter in the 6th, 8th or 12th from the Moon; ups and downs in fortune. (Ch 10)
- **Shanti**: Pacification: a ritual or practice to quiet a disturbing planetary influence. (Ch 23)
- **Shashtiamsa (D60)**: The sixtieth divisional chart, each sign in sixty parts of half a degree; past-life karma, and the chart that separates twins. (Ch 11)
- **Shodasavarga**: The set of sixteen divisional charts. (Ch 11)
- **Shukla paksha**: The bright fortnight, from new moon to full moon. (Ch 16)
- **Sidereal / Tropical**: Star-fixed zodiac (Jyotisha) versus equinox-fixed zodiac (Western). (Ch 1)
- **Soya grah / Soya ghar**: Lal Kitab's "sleeping planet" and "sleeping house": dormant until woken by a transit or related planet. (Ch 22)
- **Sripati (Shripati) system**: A house system that uses unequal houses with midpoints as cusps; the basis of Bhava Chalit charts in most Indian software. (Ch 5)
- **Stellium**: A modern word for three or more planets in one sign or house. (Ch 5)
- **Sub-lord**: In KP, the lord of the sub-division of a nakshatra (each nakshatra is split into nine unequal parts in Vimshottari proportion); the deciding factor in a KP judgement. (Ch 17)
- **Surya Lagna**: The Sun's sign treated as the 1st house; the chart read "from the Sun". (Ch 5)
- **Swakshetra**: A planet in its own sign. (Ch 3)

## T

- **Tajaka (Tajika)**: The Persian-influenced system of annual charts (Varshaphal), with its own aspects and year-lords. Appears in this book only where marked. (Ch 15)
- **Tara Bala**: The strength of a day's nakshatra counted from one's birth nakshatra, in nine groups; the third Ashtakoota test also uses it. (Ch 7, 16, 19)
- **Tattva**: Element: fire, earth, air, water (and space); each sign belongs to one. (Ch 2)
- **Teva**: The Punjabi/Hindi word for a birth chart, especially in Lal Kitab usage. (Ch 22)
- **Tithi**: A lunar day, defined by each 12° of separation between Moon and Sun; thirty in a lunar month. (Ch 16)
- **Trikona**: The trinal houses 1, 5 and 9; merit, fortune and wisdom. (Ch 4)
- **Trimsamsa (D30)**: The thirtieth divisional chart, each sign in five unequal parts of 5°, 5°, 8°, 7°, 5°; misfortune, character and disease. (Ch 11)

## U

- **Uccha**: Exaltation: the sign where a planet is strongest. (Ch 3)
- **Ujjain meridian**: The reference longitude of classical Indian astronomy (roughly 75°47′ E), through Ujjain; the Indian "Greenwich". (Ch 16)
- **Upachaya**: The growing houses 3, 6, 10 and 11, which improve with time and where malefics do well. (Ch 4)
- **Upapada (UL)**: In Jaimini's method, the Arudha of the 12th house; read for marriage. (Ch 15)
- **Upa-ratna**: A substitute gemstone (garnet for ruby, moonstone for pearl, and so on). (Ch 23)
- **Upay (Upaya)**: Remedy. *Upay* is the Hindi/Lal Kitab form; *upaya* the Sanskrit. (Ch 22, 23)
- **Uttara Kalamrita**: Kalidasa's (not the poet's) classical text, valued for its detailed lists of significations. (Ch 1)

## V

- **Vakri**: Retrograde: a planet appearing to move backwards against the stars. Rahu and Ketu are always retrograde. (Ch 3)
- **Vara**: The weekday, each ruled by a planet: Sunday–Sun, Monday–Moon, Tuesday–Mars, Wednesday–Mercury, Thursday–Jupiter, Friday–Venus, Saturday–Saturn. (Ch 16)
- **Vargottama**: A planet in the same sign in both the birth chart (D1) and the Navamsa (D9); extra strength and steadiness. (Ch 11)
- **Varna**: The first Ashtakoota test (1 point): the "class" of the Moon sign, read for spiritual compatibility and ego. (Ch 19)
- **Varshaphal / Varshphal**: The annual chart: in Tajaka, the chart of the Sun's return to its birth degree each year; in Lal Kitab, the 35-year cycle of year-lords. (Ch 15, 22)
- **Varshesha**: In Tajaka, the lord of the year, chosen from five candidates by strength. (Ch 15)
- **Vashya**: The second Ashtakoota test (2 points): mutual attraction and influence between the two Moon signs. (Ch 19)
- **Vedha**: Obstruction of a good transit by another planet in a specified position; used in transit judgement. (Ch 14)
- **Vimshottari**: The 120-year dasha system based on the Moon's nakshatra; the standard timing tool. (Ch 13)
- **Viparita Raja yoga**: "Reversed" royal combinations: the 6th, 8th or 12th lord placed in another of those houses: **Harsha** (6th lord), **Sarala** (8th lord), **Vimala** (12th lord); gains from others' losses, resilience. (Ch 10)
- **Vish yoga**: Saturn and the Moon together: emotional heaviness, discipline learned the hard way. (Ch 10)
- **Vrata**: A vow, usually a weekday fast or restriction, kept as a remedy. (Ch 23)

## W

- **Whole-sign houses**: The house system in which each sign is exactly one house, beginning with the Lagna sign as the 1st; the default in Jyotisha and in this book. (Ch 5)

## Y

- **Yantra**: A geometric diagram of a planet or deity, engraved or drawn, used as a focus for worship. (Ch 23)
- **Yoga**: (1) A planetary combination with a named result (Raja yoga, Gaja Kesari). (2) In the Panchanga, one of 27 divisions of the combined longitude of Sun and Moon. (Ch 10, 16)
- **Yogakaraka**: For certain Lagnas, a single planet that owns both a kendra and a trikona and therefore gives Raja yoga on its own (Mars for Cancer and Leo, Saturn for Taurus and Libra, Venus for Capricorn and Aquarius). (Ch 8)
- **Yogini dasha**: A 36-year dasha system with eight "yogini" periods, popular in the Himalayan north and Bengal. (Ch 15)
- **Yoni**: The fourth Ashtakoota test (4 points): each nakshatra has an animal symbol; instinctive compatibility. (Ch 19)
- **Yuti**: Conjunction: two or more planets in the same sign or house. (Ch 6)
