# About the Author

Anushka Bharti is a practising astrologer. She came to Jyotish the way many people do, through curiosity about her own life and the lives of the people she loved, and she stayed because the subject would not let her go.

Her first years of study were frustrating. The classical texts assumed knowledge she did not have, and the modern courses handed her lists to memorise without the reasons behind them. Rather than give up, she rebuilt the subject for herself from first principles, insisting on a reason and a story for every rule. This book is the result of that rebuilding, written for the reader she once was.

She reads charts for people in the Parashari tradition, uses Lal Kitab as a clearly separate lens for remedies, and believes that the most important use of astrology is not prediction but understanding: of one's own nature, of one's seasons, and of the people one loves.

*Kundali Made Simple* is her first book.

If this book helped you, the kindest thing you can do is tell someone else who is struggling with the same closed books. And if you find an error, please write; a second edition should be better than the first.
