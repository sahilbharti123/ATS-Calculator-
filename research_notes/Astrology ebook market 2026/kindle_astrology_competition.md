# Competitive Landscape for Self-Published Astrology Ebooks on Amazon Kindle (2025-2026), with Underserved Sub-Niches

Method note: the main evidence base is a live scrape of Amazon.com (US) pages on **3 October 2026**: three Kindle bestseller lists (Paid and Free), Kindle New Releases, the print "Astrology" bestseller list, 38 product pages (for BSR, publisher, KU, page count and date) and 20 Kindle keyword searches. Amazon.co.uk and Amazon.in were also checked. Each bestseller page returned 30 of its 50 items. Items #31-50 and #81-100 load by JavaScript and were not captured, so "top 60 of 100" was actually observed per list. Amazon numbers (BSR, ranks, prices, ratings) change by the hour and are only a snapshot. A "KU=True" flag means the listing showed Kindle Unlimited ($0.00 to KU readers). A blank Kindle "Publisher" field is treated as *likely* self-published. That is an inference, because KDP ebooks often show no publisher or only an author imprint.

## 1. Amazon Kindle astrology categories and what the current bestsellers look like

### Takeaway
Amazon's Kindle astrology tree is very shallow. It has one parent, Kindle eBooks > Religion & Spirituality > New Age > **Astrology**, and only two child nodes: **Eastern Astrology** and **Horoscopes**. There are no browse nodes for sun signs, birth charts, compatibility, Vedic or transits. The lists are low-volume: #2 in Kindle Astrology was only about #35k in the whole Kindle Store, and #5 in Eastern Astrology was about #279k. Traditional-publisher backlist dominates the top 10, often at promotional $1.99-$3.99 prices. Long, KU-enrolled indie books (220-640 pages) hold several top-30 slots. The lists are also full of tarot and witchcraft titles that authors have placed in astrology categories.

### Inferences
- The two small child nodes, Horoscopes and especially **Eastern Astrology**, are easy to place in. In Eastern Astrology a title at about BSR 280k (a few sales a week) can sit at #5. A new indie title with modest launch sales can earn a "#1 New Release" or "top 10" badge. These badges are cosmetic, though. Real income needs sustained BSR under about 100k, which in this snapshot only top-10 titles achieved.
- The Kindle astrology lists are shaped by **traditional-publisher price promotions**. Nicholas, Spiller, Arroyo, Faulkner, Sparkly Kat and Wagner were all at $1.99 while their paperbacks were $13-18. An indie at $7.99-$12.99 competes against discounted classics with thousands of reviews.
- Indie winners in 2024-2026 share a profile: long (220-640 pp), deep, "soul" or psychological framing, KU-enrolled, issued in several paperback and hardcover formats, and part of a numbered series (Durefelt's "Astrology for the Soul Series Book 1/2", Hind Mohsin's "Astrology of the Soul Series").
- Tarot and witchcraft books sit inside the astrology node, so the category's top spots cover all of "divination". That shrinks the room for pure astrology titles but suggests crossover hooks (astrology + tarot, astrology + witchcraft) sell.

## 2. Which sub-niches look underserved and which look saturated

### Takeaway
The most saturated areas are **per-sign 2027 yearly horoscopes**, **Chinese-zodiac 2027 annuals** and **generic 12-sign "everything about [sign]" or "[sign] in love" series**. Several high-volume publishers released full 12-sign (and 144-combination) sets in Aug-Sep 2026, nearly all with zero reviews and BSRs of roughly 350k-430k. There are also mass beginners and Vedic entry titles (more than 3,000 search results each). The niches with **low supply and some proven demand** are:
- Saturn return (only 81 Kindle results, yet several titles with 60-145 ratings)
- astrocartography and relocation (128 results, plus a 2025 DK title)
- astrology for parents and children (238 results, one 74-rating leader)
- career and money astrology (511 results, but the top results mostly have 0-5 ratings)
- psychologically framed self-care or neurodivergence astrology
- advanced technique manuals (horary, electional, evolutionary, traditional), where readers pay $13-37

Outer-planet transit books (Pluto in Aquarius, Neptune in Aries) are scarce but show little demand.

## 3. How flooded astrology is with AI-generated or low-quality books, and how Amazon's 2026 limits and disclosure rules affect a 12-book series

### Takeaway
No published study measures AI content in Amazon's astrology categories specifically. Originality.ai's August 2026 study of Religion & Spirituality found 63% of 2026 releases likely AI-written, with the closest category, Witchcraft, at 78%. Directly observed 2026 astrology listings show clear content-mill patterns: 12-sign and 144-combination sets, 13+ numbered volumes in months, 300+ catalogue entries, and near-zero reviews. That AI is involved is an inference from these patterns. KDP's rules since September 21, 2026 allow **two new titles per format per week**. KDP also requires disclosure of AI-*generated* content and bans content "excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books". Both directly constrain a templated 12-sign series.

**KDP title limit**
- Since **September 21, 2026**, KDP allows **two new titles per book format per week**: two Kindle eBooks, two paperbacks and two hardcovers, each with its own allowance. The allowance resets every Sunday at 12:00 AM UTC.
  - The count applies to title *creation* (clicking "+ Create"), so new pre-orders, translations and large-print editions all count. Updating existing titles (keywords, categories, price, files) does not.
  - Amazon's guidance no longer advertises an exception path for high-volume publishers.
  - Authors can stage pre-orders (up to 10 active at once, scheduled up to 18 months ahead) to coordinate release dates.
  
  — [Vappingo: KDP publishing limit 2026](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-publishing-limit/) (citing the KDP "Create a Book" help page and a KDP Community announcement, checked Sep 24, 2026)
- ALLi (Sep 24, 2026) confirms the two-per-format-per-week limit and the Sunday reset. It says the cap is down from "ten titles weekly in 2025". — [ALLi / Self-Publishing Advice](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/?p=241887). Conflict: the Authors Guild reported the earlier cap as **3 new titles per day**, introduced in **September 2023** — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/). The 2025 "ten per week" figure is unverified.

**AI disclosure and KDP content rules**
- KDP requires disclosure of **AI-generated** content, meaning text, images or translations created by an AI tool even if substantially edited afterwards. Disclosure applies when publishing or republishing. **AI-assisted** content (your own work refined, edited or brainstormed with AI) does not require disclosure. The author remains responsible for guideline and IP compliance. — [KDP Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) (fetched)
- Review times for books with disclosed AI content reportedly rose from 24-48 hours to 3-5 business days in many cases. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-publishing-limit/) (search snippet only; not verified in the fetched text)
- KDP's "disappointing content" rules prohibit:
  - "Content that is not significantly different from content in another book"
  - "Content that is excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books"
  - "Content that is freely available on the web"
  - content "too short"
  - "Bonus content that appears before a book's primary content"
  
  — [KDP Guide to Kindle Content Quality](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200952510) (fetched)

### Inferences
- **Effect on a 12-sign series**:
  - Ebook creation takes at least **6 weeks** (12 ÷ 2 per week). Paperbacks and hardcovers run in parallel on their own allowances. An all-signs omnibus, translations or a large-print edition each use another slot.
  - For 2027 annuals, the prime selling window is Oct-Jan. Starting title creation on Oct 4, 2026 means the 12th ebook could not be created before about the week of Nov 8-14, 2026, so the later signs lose a third or more of the season. A 2028 annual set should start title creation by about mid-August 2027 and use pre-orders.
  - The cap does *not* restrict a single all-12-signs annual. That needs one slot per format and concentrates reviews and ranking on one ASIN, which is how the strongest indie annual (Sasson) operates.
- **Templating risk**: if the 12 books share most of their text (a common "change the sign name" template), they risk the "excessively reused … across books" and "not significantly different" rules. Titles could be blocked or the account warned, and this matters more now that each slot is scarce.
- **Disclosure economics**: AI-assisted drafting with genuinely author-written interpretation avoids the disclosure requirement. Disclosed AI-generated text adds review delay and risk of reader backlash.
- **Visible flooding in 2026 is highest in** per-sign and per-animal annuals, sign-based personality and love series, combinatorial "planet in sign/house" references and rapid-fire numbered Vedic series. Human-authored differentiation (an expert voice, original chart examples, a real biography and author platform) stands out more because these mills average 0 reviews.

### Gaps

- Whether Amazon shows AI disclosure to shoppers could not be confirmed. No fetched source states it either way.
- Sales of mill catalogues are unknown beyond the BSR snapshots (about 350k-430k).
- The 14,419-book genre-fiction study's original source was not located.

## 5. Pricing and format norms

### Takeaway
Kindle prices fall into tiers:

| Tier | Typical Kindle price |
|---|---|
| Commodity mills and short guides | $0.99-$5.99 (most in KU) |
| Credible indie series | $7.99-$12.99 |
| Publisher backlist | Often promoted at $1.99-$3.99 |
| Advanced technical manuals | $13-$37 |

Indie titles that rank are long (220-640 pages). **Print matters disproportionately**: the print Astrology top 30 is full of gift and reference books, oracle decks, calendars and workbooks. Planners and annuals sell in print, not ebook. Successful indies offer 3-6 formats. Audiobooks lead some print lists for narrative and spiritual astrology. Chart-heavy ebooks pay a real delivery-fee cost at the 70% royalty.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Price positioning**: $6.99-$9.99 Kindle (inside the 70% band, a commonly cited $2.99-$9.99 window not verified in this thread) for a deep 200-400-page indie title, with a $14.99-$19.99 paperback. Reserve $0.99-$2.99 for launch promos or series starters. Advanced technique books can go above $9.99 at 35% royalty, or pair a $9.99 ebook with a $25-35 print edition.
- **Chart-heavy ebooks**: a 17 MB file costs about $2.57 per US sale in delivery (17.1 × $0.15) at 70%. Compress images, use vector or SVG where supported, or keep reflowable text with a few key chart wheels. Detailed tables of aspects and ephemerides are better in print or as downloadable PDFs.
- **Print is likely essential** for gift-type titles (sign gift books, Saturn-return gifts, parent/baby, planners, workbooks, yearly annuals). Low-content journals and planners belong in print only.
- **Audiobooks** are viable for narrative, psychological or "spiritual memoir" astrology (Silverman, Gallagher, Gregory-style). They suit reference and chart-reading books poorly. Virtual Voice lowers cost, but its effect on astrology buyers is unknown.

## 6. Amazon content policies and legal considerations for astrology books

### Takeaway
No KDP rule specific to astrology was found. The general content-quality, misleading-content and AI rules apply, and so do health-claim cautions for medical or mental-health astrology. Key IP points:
- Astro.com lets users publish chart drawings they created themselves from their own data, but horoscope *texts* are copyrighted and commercial redistribution of its data is barred.
- The Swiss Ephemeris software is under AGPL or a commercial licence.
- Several frameworks carry trademarks: Astro*Carto*Graphy, Moonology™, Jovian Archive Human Design, Quantum Human Design™.

Zodiac sign names themselves appear freely in thousands of titles.

### Cited Findings
- **KDP prohibits** "descriptive content meant to mislead customers or that doesn't accurately represent the content of the book" and "content that's typically disappointing to customers". It also bars undifferentiated public-domain versions when a free one exists. — [KDP Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) (fetched)
  - This matters for public-domain astrology classics such as Ptolemy's *Tetrabiblos*, Lilly and Manly P. Hall. Several appear in the top 100: *Tetrabiblos* #7 in Eastern Astrology at $14.48; Manly P. Hall #71 in Horoscopes at $0.99. — [Amazon Kindle Best Sellers: Eastern Astrology](https://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers-Kindle-Store-Eastern-Astrology/zgbs/digital-text/6361643011); [Amazon Kindle Best Sellers: New Age Horoscopes, page 2](https://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers-Kindle-Store-New-Age-Horoscopes/zgbs/digital-text/158482011/ref=zg_bs_pg_2_digital-text?_encoding=UTF8&pg=2)
- **Health claims**: claims about drugs, health products and similar items require substantiation. — Amazon/KDP help text as surfaced in search for [KDP help topic G4WB7VPPEAREHAAD](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4WB7VPPEAREHAAD) (search snippet only; page not fetched, and it may be Amazon Ads policy rather than KDP content policy)

Claims that readings are truthful or accurate are likely to be problematic. — [ASA: Psychics, spiritualists, fortune tellers, astrologers and clairvoyants](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/psychics-spiritualists-fortune-tellers-astrologers-and-clairvoyants.html) (search snippet). ### Inferences
- **Disclaimers**: include a front-matter "for entertainment, self-reflection and educational purposes; not a substitute for medical, psychological, financial or legal advice" statement. This matters most for medical, mental-health and financial astrology and for any "predictions". Avoid guaranteed-outcome language in titles and descriptions ("will find love", "cure"), which could fall under KDP's misleading-content rules or ad policies.
- **Chart images**: generating wheels yourself, in astro.com (your own data) or in licensed software (Solar Fire, Astro Gold and others; their licences were not checked here), is the safest route. Do not paste Astrodienst interpretive texts. Planetary positions are astronomical facts. Generally, factual data is not copyrightable, but specific formatted ephemeris tables may be protected. Generate your own tables, or license them, rather than copying a published ephemeris.
- **Naming**: use "astrocartography" or "relocation astrology" rather than "Astro*Carto*Graphy". Use "Human Design" descriptively only, not in a branded way. Avoid "Moonology" in titles. Do not imply endorsement by famous astrologers, and do not use their names as keywords or in subtitles.
- The "excessively reused … across books" rule (section 3) is the main legal and policy risk for 12-sign series, alongside AI disclosure.
