Scope note: founder is a first-time, full-time, risk-tolerant seller/Instagram-creator with no coding, ₹3,00,000 total (setup + stock + marketing + reserve), city = one of the 8 metros (unknown). Source dates are given in brackets. "Search snippet" = figure seen in a search-result summary where the page itself was not opened or returned 403; treat as weaker. Most rupee figures in this space come from vendor/SaaS/consultancy blogs (Petpooja, DineOpen, OKCredit, IncorpX, Truffle Nation, etc.) that have commercial motives; they are directional, not audited. ## 1. Unit economics by model: setup cost, monthly fixed cost, margin, typical revenue for a new operator, break-even

### Takeaway
Only asset-light, home- or doorstep-based models fit both ₹3L and a 2–3 week start: home tiffin / office-meal subscription, healthy/protein meal prep, home bakery, doorstep car wash, home deep-cleaning, event/balloon decoration, photo-booth rental, asset-light laundry pickup, and flea-market/pop-up selling. Rented cloud kitchens (₹3–15L, 6–12 month break-even), metro-station kiosks (10-month rent deposit), food trucks (₹15–50k licences, 15–30+ working days) and mobile pet-grooming vans (₹6–8.5L) fail one or both constraints.

## 2. Aggregator economics: Swiggy/Zomato commissions, fees, onboarding requirements and timeline; ONDC and alternatives

### Takeaway
For a new single-outlet brand, Swiggy/Zomato take ~22–30% commission plus 18% GST on that commission, and in practice also expect restaurant-funded discounts (~15%) and ads (₹1,000+/week), leaving roughly half the order value before food cost; onboarding needs FSSAI + PAN + bank + (per most guides) GSTIN and takes ~5–10 business days after documents (up to ~3 weeks). Low-commission alternatives exist (ONDC 3–10%; Rapido's Ownly flat ₹25/₹50 per order in parts of Bengaluru; Swiggy's budget "Toing"), but their order volume is small, so they cannot be relied on for week-3 revenue.

### Cited Findings
- Single-outlet/small restaurants pay 25–27% commission on both platforms; 10+ outlet chains negotiate 18–22% [2026] — [Restaurant Coach](https://restaurantcoach.in/zomato-swiggy-commission-rates-2026/)
- Independent restaurants report 22–30% per order vs ~8–9% for large chains (citing NDTV Profit 2025); 18% GST on commission; restaurant-funded discounts ~15% of order value; "minimum ₹1,000 per week" ads to stay visible; reports of unauthorised ad deductions [Jun 2026] — [DineHere](https://dinehere.ai/in/blog/how-much-swiggy-zomato-cost-indian-restaurants)
- Illustrative ₹1,000 order: commission (24%) −₹240, GST on commission −₹43, discount −₹150, packaging −₹30, ads −₹50 → ~₹487 to restaurant before food/ops costs [Jun 2026] — [DineHere](https://dinehere.ai/in/blog/how-much-swiggy-zomato-cost-indian-restaurants)
- Case: Scoopful (Bengaluru) ₹17,000 sales → ₹5,500 commission + ₹10,000 ads → ₹1,500 left [2026] — [DineHere](https://dinehere.ai/in/blog/how-much-swiggy-zomato-cost-indian-restaurants)
- Reported 2026 base commissions: Zomato 18–28%, Swiggy 17–25%; effective deduction 25–35%; Zomato long-distance surcharge up to ₹40 (6+ km); a "platform fee" hike in March 2026 to ₹14.90 (Zomato) / ₹17.58 (Swiggy, 24 Mar) — [MenuManager](https://menumanager.in/zomato-platform-fee-hike-2026/). Online delivery share cited as rising from 11% (FY26) to 18% (FY31) (search snippet; page not opened) — [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/food/indias-food-services-sector-boom-faces-scaling-bottleneck)

### Inferences
- (estimate) Earliest realistic first aggregator order if starting 3 Oct 2026: FSSAI basic (7–15 days) and GST (7 working days) in parallel → Swiggy/Zomato onboarding 5–10 business days → ~day 18–30. So aggregator-first plans miss or barely hit the 3-week target; direct WhatsApp/Instagram/society sales can start earlier.
- (estimate) At ~₹487 net per ₹1,000 order and 30–35% food cost, a ₹300-AOV aggregator order leaves roughly ₹40–₹55 contribution before rent and labour — consistent with the "70 orders → ₹1,000–₹3,000" figure. A content-led founder should treat aggregators as a discovery/overflow channel and push repeat customers to direct ordering (WhatsApp/own link) or subscriptions.
- (estimate) For a home tiffin/subscription brand, aggregators are optional; Ownly/ONDC are worth listing on where available because they are cheap, but should not be in the revenue forecast.

## 3. Licences and registrations: what is needed, cost, and how long they actually take

### Takeaway
A home-based food business needs only FSSAI Basic Registration (₹100/yr, turnover ≤₹12L; legally decided within 7 days, typically issued in 7–15 working days, with a "deemed" right to start if no decision); Shop & Establishment is near-instant in Maharashtra, 1–3 days in Delhi, 7–15 days in Karnataka; GST is voluntary below thresholds but takes 7 working days (30 if physical verification) and is reportedly needed for aggregator listing. Street carts are the licensing problem: new vendors effectively cannot get a Certificate of Vending outside periodic town-vending surveys, and food trucks need 15–30+ working days and ₹15–50k of NOCs.

### Cited Findings
- FSSAI Basic Registration: turnover up to ₹12 lakh; ₹100/year; covers petty retailers, hawkers, temporary stalls, home-based units; typically issued in 7–15 working days. State Licence: ₹12L–₹20 crore turnover; ₹2,000/year (some sources ₹2,000–₹5,000); 30–60 days — [Tax2Win](https://tax2win.in/guide/fssai-registration-vs-license); [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/business-guides/fssai-license-fees-cost-of-food-business-registration/)
- FSS Licensing Regulations: registering authority must grant/reject or order inspection within 7 days of application; if inspection ordered, grant within 30 days; if no grant/denial/inspection within 7 days or no decision within 30 days, the petty food business "may start its business" (must comply with later improvement notices) — [FSSAI Licensing Regulations compendium](https://foodregulatory.fssai.gov.in/All%20Docs/Safe%20Food%20Practices/FSS_Licensing_Regulations.pdf)
- Shop & Establishment: Maharashtra instant/within 48 hours, free for <10 employees (one-time, no renewal); Delhi 1–3 days, ₹100–₹500; Karnataka 7–15 days, ₹250–₹1,000 (via Seva Sindhu); registration due within 60 days (MH) / 90 days (Delhi) of starting [2026] — [IncorpX state-wise guide](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/shop-and-establishment-license-state-wise-guide)
- GST: approval within 7 working days if documents in order; Aadhaar authentication; up to 30 days where physical verification is triggered (no Aadhaar auth, risk-flagged, or officer's discretion) — [CBIC Rule 9](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter3/rule9_v1.00.html); [StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/time-limit-increased-for-grant-of-gst-registration-from-3-to-7-working-days-93581.html). Thresholds: services ₹20L (mandatory for car-wash-type services only above ₹20L) — [OKCredit](https://okcredit.in/blog/how-to-start-car-wash-business-in-india/); ₹20L for fast-food services but "mandatory for platform onboarding" — [Agile Regulatory](https://www.agileregulatory.com/blogs/how-to-apply-for-zomato-restaurant-partner-registration-in-2026-)
- Car wash licences: municipal trade licence, Shop & Establishment, free Udyam registration, State Pollution Control Board consent (Orange category); GST only above ₹20L [Aug 2026] — [OKCredit](https://okcredit.in/blog/how-to-start-car-wash-business-in-india/)
- Delhi: police "Eating House Registration" requirement scrapped by LG order of 19 June 2025 (MHA withdrew the 2023 regulations); Delhi government moving to drop MCD health trade licence for eateries [Jul 2025] — [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/mha-withdraws-the-delhi-eating-houses-registration-regulations-2023-with-immediate-effect/); [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/19/des82-dl-lg-restaurants-license.html)
- Cloud kitchen licence list: FSSAI (basic 7–10 days), trade licence, fire NOC (no costs/timelines given) [Feb 2026] — [DineOpen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/cloud-kitchen-guide-2026)
- Food trucks: FSSAI, RTO-cleared commercial vehicle, municipal trade licence, fire NOC, GST above threshold, plus Town Vending Committee certificate if parking in a vending zone; ₹15,000–₹50,000 in year 1; 15–30 working days in parallel; advise 45–60 days lead — [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/guide/how-to-get-food-truck-license-india); [Petpooja](https://blog.petpooja.com/growth-scaling/open-food-truck-business-in-india/)
- Street vending: under the Street Vendors Act 2014, Town Vending Committees survey existing vendors (at least once every 5 years) and issue Certificates of Vending to those identified in the survey; vendors are protected from eviction until survey/certification completes — [Wikipedia: Street Vendors Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Vendors_Act,_2014); [CCS](https://ccs.in/street-vendors-act-2014-allocating-certificates-vending)
- Mumbai: BMC is issuing QR codes to its ~10,500 licensed hawkers (by end-April 2026) to tackle illegal vending; Bombay HC has said "the entire city" is encroached by unauthorised hawkers and directed BMC and police to remove unlicensed ones; post-election BMC demolition drives — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/bmc-to-roll-out-qr-codes-for-10500-licensed-hawkers-by-april-end-to-tackle-illegal-vending); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/bombay-high-court/entire-mumbai-has-been-encroached-by-hawkers-state-cannot-express-helplessness-bombay-high-court-pulls-up-state-govt-mumbai-police-275024); [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/mumbai-news-post-polls-bmc-steps-up-action-against-unauthorised-extensions-and-hawkers-activists-flag-patchy-enforcement)

### Inferences
- (estimate) Practical day-by-day for a home food brand: Day 1 apply FSSAI Basic (FoSCoS) + Udyam (free, online) + open current account; Day 1–3 apply GST only if going on Swiggy/Zomato; Day 8+ start direct sales (legally permissible if no inspection/decision communicated within 7 days, and most basic registrations arrive in 7–15 working days).
- (estimate) Service businesses run from home/doorstep (car wash, cleaning, décor, photo booth) can start invoicing on day 1 with Udyam; Shop & Establishment/trade licence matters once there is premises or staff (state rules differ), and local society/RWA permission is the real gate for doorstep car wash.
- (estimate) A new chai/momo cart is effectively operating outside the formal licence system in most metros (no new Certificates of Vending outside surveys); legality comes from either (a) private land with owner permission (outside a shop, office campus, society gate), (b) event/flea-market slots, or (c) informal tolerance — the root of harassment/eviction risk.

### Gaps
- Municipal trade-licence fees and processing times for BBMP/GHMC/GCC/PMC/KMC/AMC not collected (vary by city and trade).
- No official data on how many Certificates of Vending were issued to new vendors in 2025–26 in any metro.
- Whether Mumbai/Bengaluru/Hyderabad still require a separate police eating-house licence for small eateries was not verified (only Delhi's 2025 abolition found).

## 4. Failure rates and why (cloud kitchens, carts, franchises)

### Takeaway
Cloud kitchens are the best-documented failure case: 25–30% close in year one and ~half of metro kitchens were loss-making (NRAI 2023), driven by aggregator dependence, commissions + ads exceeding food cost, oversized menus and rating collapse. Carts fail mainly through eviction/illegality; franchise "opportunities" under ₹3–10L are a recurring fraud vector in 2025–26.

### Cited Findings
- 25–30% of Indian cloud kitchens close within the first year (BBFT, 2024); "nearly half" of cloud kitchens in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru were running at a loss (NRAI survey, 2023); cloud-kitchen segment USD 1.24bn in 2025 → USD 3.69bn by 2034 (IMARC) [May 2026] — [Petpooja](https://blog.petpooja.com/operations-workflows/why-cloud-kitchens-shutting-down/)
- Five failure causes: single-channel aggregator dependency, oversized menus (35+ items, food cost >40%), rating collapse → heavy discounting, multi-brand complexity, discovering losses only at month-end [May 2026] — [Petpooja](https://blog.petpooja.com/operations-workflows/why-cloud-kitchens-shutting-down/)
- Owners "paying more in commissions than they spend on ingredients"; Rebel Foods FY25 net loss ₹336.6 cr on ₹1,617 cr revenue (search snippet; page 403) [Feb 2026] — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/02/indian-cloud-kitchen-boom)
- Gurugram founder (Reddit "SquishyWaffle"): started at 12 orders/day × ₹250 from a 180 sq ft basement → 200/day in 6 months → 78 kitchens, 14,000 orders/day, $5M raised; then commissions rose to 32%, wastage 8%→18%, marketing ₹12–15/order, ₹80L monthly burn, 30% kitchens loss-making; "I should have stopped at 8–10 kitchens" [Jan 2025] — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/dont-start-a-cloud-kitchen-gurugram-startup-founder-shares-how-scaling-too-fast-almost-destroyed-their-business-462290-2025-01-28)
- CloudKitchens (Travis Kalanick) India arm Kitchen Plus exited in 2022/23 after ~3 years amid higher-than-expected rents/utilities [Feb 2023] — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2023/02/travis-kalanicks-cloud-kitchen-business-makes-a-silent-exit)
- Bombay HC on hawkers / BMC drives (see Q3) — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/bombay-high-court/bombay-high-court-illegal-hawkers-bmc-273421)
- Franchise frauds: Marakkar Biryani promoter arrested for cheating 240+ people across 5 states of >₹25 cr; investors paid ₹5.18–10L each, only 21 shops opened [Jul 2025] — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!state/rs-dot-25-crore-biryani-shop-franchise-scam-absconding-businessman-arrested-enn25070705006); fake Tata Zudio franchise, Surat man lost ₹31L via fake "registration/NOC" fees [Jul 2025] — [DeshGujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2025/07/26/surat-man-duped-of-%E2%82%B931-lakh-in-fake-tata-zudio-franchise-scam-2-held/); fake Domino's franchise, ₹2.65L lost to fake "Jubilant FoodWorks" manager [Apr 2026] — [The Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/4/12/man-duped-of-rs-265-lakh-in-pretext-of-franchise-scam-.html); fake fertiliser franchise website, ₹3.78L; gang linked to ~500 crimes nationally [Aug 2026] — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/adilabad-five-held-for-duping-man-of-rs-3-78-lakh-in-fake-fertiliser-franchise-scam); 2020 SC plea by 38 petitioners vs Westland Trade Pvt Ltd brands (Hyper Mart, Big Mart, Louies Salon, Midnight Cafe, "Franchisee World") — [Bar & Bench](https://barandbench.com/news/litigation/plea-in-supreme-court-seeks-cbi-ed-sfio-probe-into-indias-biggest-franchisee-scam)

### Inferences
- (estimate) Highest-risk models for this founder, in order: rented multi-brand cloud kitchen (capital + 6–12 month break-even + aggregator margin squeeze) > paid franchise under ₹3L (fraud + royalty drag) > street cart on public land (eviction) > rented kiosk (fixed rent vs uncertain footfall). Lowest-risk: pre-sold subscriptions (tiffin, car wash) and event services paid in advance, because cash comes before cost.
- (estimate) Common failure thread across food models is "volume bought with discounts/ads" — a creator with organic Instagram reach can partly substitute for paid platform ads, which is the main lever on the 25–35% deduction problem.

### Gaps
- No credible closure-rate data for tiffin services, home bakeries, car-wash, cleaning or décor businesses in India.
- The BBFT 2024 and NRAI 2023 primary reports were not accessed directly (cited via Petpooja).
- No reliable data on frequency of cart confiscation/fines or informal "hafta" payments in specific metros (only court/BMC reporting for Mumbai).

## 5. Fit with a sales-strong, on-camera Instagram founder

### Takeaway
Models where the product is visual, repeat-purchase, and sold to a dense local catchment (societies, offices, gyms) benefit most from Reels + door-to-door selling: home tiffin/protein meal subscriptions, home bakery/dessert drops, event/balloon décor and photo booths (inherently visual, and every event is content), and doorstep car-wash subscriptions sold society-by-society. Aggregator-led cloud kitchens benefit least because platform ranking is bought with discounts and ads, not creator content.

### Cited Findings
- Oye! Momo (South Delhi, founded 2025): started with ₹2,000 of raw material and a borrowed steamer; ₹200 of Instagram ads produced ~₹1 lakh of orders; now a B2B momo supplier at ₹56L revenue — [Startuppedia](https://startuppedia.in/startup-stories/once-worked-at-theka-coffee-25-yo-built-oye-momo-to-supply-fresh-momos-to-restaurants-hits-rs-56l-11810304)

- Home bakers growing via Instagram + word of mouth to ₹60,000/month in 6 months and ₹1.2L/month by month 12 (course-provider testimonials; bias) — [Truffle Nation](https://trufflenation.com/blog/how-to-start-home-bakery/)
- Selling direct vs app: a ₹1,800 bakery order nets ~₹420 via a 25%-commission app vs ~₹808 via own store at 3.5% fee; WhatsApp order handling costs 2–3 hours/day and loses 20–30% of enquiries [Jul 2026] — [Cakesify](https://www.cakesify.com/blog/is-home-baking-profitable-in-india-an-analysis-for-aspiring-sellers/)
- Doorstep car wash is "a natural fit for subscription selling inside apartment societies" — [OKCredit](https://okcredit.in/blog/how-to-start-car-wash-business-in-india/)
- Platform visibility requires paid ads (₹1,000+/week) and discounts (~15%) — [DineHere](https://dinehere.ai/in/blog/how-much-swiggy-zomato-cost-indian-restaurants); Ownly ranks by ratings, not paid promotion — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/06/223-rapido-food-delivery-no-commissions-customer-data-access/)

### Inferences
- (estimate) Ranking for this founder (fit × feasibility in 3 weeks × ₹3L): (1) office/PG tiffin or protein-meal subscription in one micro-market — Reels of daily menus, founder-face "what I cooked for 40 people today", direct WhatsApp subscriptions; (2) event décor + photo-booth combo for Oct–Feb festival/wedding season — every gig is portfolio content; (3) doorstep car-wash subscriptions in 3–5 large societies — pure sales game; (4) home bakery/dessert drops (Diwali hampers) — strong content but low early revenue unless founder can bake; (5) pop-up/flea stalls as a test-and-sample channel for any of the food products.
- (estimate) Sales skill matters most where you sell to gatekeepers: RWA/society committees (car wash, cleaning, laundry), office admin/HR (bulk lunch), gym owners (protein meals), event planners/wedding photographers (décor, photo booth referrals).

## 7. Franchises under ₹3 lakh: real or traps?

### Takeaway
Genuine sub-₹3L franchises are few (kiosk/cart momos, laundry collection points); most well-known chai/QSR franchises need ₹10L+, and the sub-₹10L franchise space is a documented hunting ground for fraud (fake Zudio/Domino's/biryani/fertiliser franchises in 2025–26). For this founder, a franchise mainly converts scarce capital into a fee plus royalty without solving the real constraint (customers).

### Cited Findings
- Momomia momo franchise ~₹2–3L (listicle claim; "≈10% profit per year") — [Petpooja food franchise list](https://blog.petpooja.com/growth-scaling/food-franchise-business-in-india/); Chai Sutta Bar needs ₹10–12L — [same](https://blog.petpooja.com/growth-scaling/food-franchise-business-in-india/)
- Laundry brands offer "collection centre" franchises where the brand processes orders; Dhobilite plans from ₹3L to ₹50L — [Dhobilite](https://www.dhobilite.com/blog/dhobilite-franchise); [Fabrico](https://fabrico.in/blog/small-franchise-business)
- Fraud cases 2025–26: Marakkar Biryani (₹25 cr+, 240+ victims, ₹5.18–10L each); fake Zudio (₹31L); fake Domino's (₹2.65L); fake fertiliser franchise (₹3.78L; gang tied to ~500 crimes) — see Q4 sources: [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!state/rs-dot-25-crore-biryani-shop-franchise-scam-absconding-businessman-arrested-enn25070705006); [DeshGujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2025/07/26/surat-man-duped-of-%E2%82%B931-lakh-in-fake-tata-zudio-franchise-scam-2-held/); [The Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/4/12/man-duped-of-rs-265-lakh-in-pretext-of-franchise-scam-.html); [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/adilabad-five-held-for-duping-man-of-rs-3-78-lakh-in-fake-fertiliser-franchise-scam)
- Marakkar Biryani promoted franchises heavily on social media and used TV actors in ads; franchisees who did open received little support — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!state/rs-dot-25-crore-biryani-shop-franchise-scam-absconding-businessman-arrested-enn25070705006)
- Typical scam mechanics: fake websites, social-media ads, staged "registration fee / NOC / agreement" payments in instalments, impersonation of brand managers — [DeshGujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2025/07/26/surat-man-duped-of-%E2%82%B931-lakh-in-fake-tata-zudio-franchise-scam-2-held/); [The Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2026/4/12/man-duped-of-rs-265-lakh-in-pretext-of-franchise-scam-.html)
- Advice: review royalty, territory exclusivity, renewal/termination and guaranteed support before signing — [Fabrico](https://fabrico.in/blog/small-franchise-business)

### Inferences
- (estimate) Red flags: big brands (Zudio, Domino's, Amul, Tata) contacted via WhatsApp/Instagram ads; payments to individuals or non-brand accounts; "limited slots" urgency; promised fixed monthly returns; no visit to an operating franchisee. Real large brands do not sell franchises through cold DMs.
- (estimate) If a franchise is considered at all, the defensible version is a low-fee supply tie-up (e.g., buying frozen momos/sauces wholesale or a laundry brand's processing backend) rather than an upfront franchise fee — this keeps most of ₹3L for marketing and reserve.

### Gaps
- No verified data on the unit economics or survival of specific sub-₹3L franchise brands (Momomia, Wow! Momo kiosks, laundry collection points); listicle claims only.
- I did not find a source on whether India has any franchise-specific disclosure law (my understanding is it does not, and franchise disputes fall under general contract/consumer law). This is unsourced, so check it before stating it in the report.
