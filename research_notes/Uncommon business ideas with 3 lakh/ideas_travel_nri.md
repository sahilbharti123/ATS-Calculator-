# Travel, hospitality and NRI/diaspora-visitor business ideas proven abroad (2023–2026) but absent or rare in India (0–3 players), for a ₹3 lakh, ~2-month metro launch (research date: 3 Oct 2026)

*Method note: competitor checks are 1–3 web searches per idea plus page fetches. The session's web-search budget ran out mid-way, so some counts rest on a single search and should be re-checked by hand on Instagram, Google Maps and JustDial before committing. "[EST]" marks my estimates; everything else is sourced. Currency assumption for conversions [EST]: US$1 ≈ ₹88, £1 ≈ ₹118. Data older than 2024 is labelled **(older)**.*

## Q0. Demand context: how big are the NRI-visitor, wedding-season, student-abroad and outbound-winter markets?

### Takeaway
Every space on the brief has large, recent, sourced demand. There are 3.4–3.5 crore overseas Indians, including about 1.96 crore PIOs (foreign citizens of Indian origin). CAIT counts 46 lakh weddings in the 2025 Nov–Dec window. About 18.8 lakh Indian students are abroad. Family interest in winter trips abroad rose 30% year on year for Dec 2025–Feb 2026. The December–January NRI homecoming coincides with peak wedding season and the North-Indian school winter break, which is the launch window for this founder.

### Cited Findings
- **Overseas Indians:** about 35.4 million in 2025–26 (15.85M NRIs and 19.57M PIOs), per MEA figures summarised by search. The MEA series shows 343.6 lakh in 2025, up from 161.8 lakh in 2012. — [MEA – Population of Overseas Indians](https://www.mea.gov.in/population-of-overseas-indians.htm); [Factly/Dataful](https://factly.in/saudi-arabia-us-canada-and-gulf-nations-lead-growth-in-overseas-indian-population)
- **Year-end NRI return:** around 35 lakh overseas Indians returning over the year-end period pushed up airfares. The biggest traffic increases were on India–UAE routes. **(older: Dec 2023 article; via search summary)** — [Business Today, 29 Dec 2023](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/latest/trends/story/popular-tourist-destinations-in-india-swamped-as-year-end-tourism-explodes-411315-2023-12-29); [BW Hotelier](https://bwhotelier.com/article/a-seasonal-travel-surge-in-india-539742)
- **Wedding season 2025:** CAIT estimated **46 lakh weddings between 1 Nov and 14 Dec 2025** and ₹6.5 lakh crore of spending, with Delhi alone at 4.8 lakh weddings. — [CAIT press release](https://cait.in/wedding-season-2025-to-generate-₹6-5-lakh-crore-business-from-46-lakh-weddings-across-india-cait-delhi-alone-to-witness-₹1-8-lakh-crore-trade-from-4-8-lakh-weddings-indian/) (URL as cited in sibling notes file `ideas_hobbies_genz_retail.md`; not re-fetched by me)
  - **Conflict:** Skift reports the same CAIT data as "460,000 weddings" and $72.3B. This looks like a units error, since 46 lakh = 4.6 million. Skift also says 1 in 4 Indian weddings is now a destination wedding (90% of them domestic) and that hotel rates surge up to 40% in peak wedding weeks. — [Skift, 10 Dec 2025](https://skift.com/2025/12/10/indias-72-billion-wedding-season-is-reshaping-travel-again/)
- **Students abroad (MEA data tabled in Parliament, Dec 2025):** 1.882 million Indian students abroad in 153 countries. Of these, 1.254M are in higher education (down 76,000, or 5.7%, from the 2024 peak of 1.33M) and 628,305 are at school level. Higher-education numbers by country: Canada 427,085, US 255,000+, UK 173,190, Australia 138,579, Germany 59,000. — [The PIE News, 22 Dec 2025](https://thepienews.com/outbound-indian-university-enrolments-fall-after-three-year-rise/)
- **Outbound winter travel:** Agoda saw a 30% year-on-year rise in Indian family searches for international winter stays (check-ins Dec 2025–Feb 2026, searched Sep–Oct 2025). Growth by destination: South Korea +226%, China +105%, Austria +82%, Kazakhstan +68%, Taiwan +44%, Germany +43%, Japan +41%, Finland +33%. — [The Week / Agoda release, 13 Nov 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/11/13/dcm73-agoda.html)
- Japan received a record **315,100 Indian visitors in 2025 (+35.2%)**. — [India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=211713). Kazakhstan was the 4th most-searched international destination for Indians in 2025 (Niyo). — [India Outbound](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=210307)
- **Transit:** Delhi airport international-to-international transfers rose from 388,000 (FY2023) to 1.34 million (FY2025), up 244%. — [Infrastructure Today, 3 Sep 2025](https://infrastructuretoday.co.in/delhi-airport-becomes-asias-fastest-growing-transit-hub-after-244-surge-in-international-transfers/)

### Inferences
- The founder's ~8-week runway lands squarely on the **Dec 2026–Jan 2027 NRI and wedding peak**. Ideas that can sell before mid-November and deliver from mid-December have a natural first season. Ideas with a 30-day-plus booking lead time (rail saloon) or long sales cycles (reunions, roots tours) will mostly earn in Jan–Mar 2027 or the following December.
- The PIO count (about 1.96 crore foreign citizens of Indian origin) is the under-served "diaspora visitor" pool. Most NRI services in India target Indian-passport NRIs and their elderly parents (a sector covered by another researcher).

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 count of NRIs/OCIs who visit India in Dec–Jan was found; the 35 lakh figure is from Dec 2023.
- No city-wise split of NRI arrivals was found, so city choice for each idea is reasoned, not sourced.

## Q1. Idea 1 — "Wedding Kids' Club": mobile crèche and nannies for multi-day Indian weddings (UK wedding-crèche model)

### Takeaway
UK weddings routinely book mobile crèches run by vetted nannies, including for Indian weddings. India has 46 lakh weddings a season, NRI guests who fly in with toddlers, and sangeets running to 1 a.m. My searches found **0 dedicated Indian wedding-crèche brands**: a wedding blog says providers exist but names none, and "Mobile Creches" is an unrelated NGO. This is the fastest-to-cash idea here (3–5 weeks) and fits a sell-to-planners founder. Its main risk is child-safety liability.

### Cited Findings
- **Abroad, what it is:** a wedding crèche is "a specialist service which offers a flexible solution for childcare on your wedding day". Experienced wedding nannies and nursery nurses run age-appropriate games, crafts and films; staff hold enhanced DBS checks and paediatric first-aid qualifications. — [Event Childcare (UK)](https://eventchildcare.net/childcare-at-weddings)
- **Abroad, named operators:** Swindon Child Carers (mobile wedding crèches since 1988, ages 6 weeks–8 years) — [Guides for Brides](https://www.guidesforbrides.co.uk/wedding-entertainment/wiltshire/swindon-child-carers-ltd/71992). Gilly's Helping Hands (since 2011, Devon/Cornwall/Somerset) — [Bridebook](https://app-proxy-dev-1.bridebook.com/uk/wedding-entertainment/gilly-s-helping-hands-childcare-barnstaple-devon-csR8Bx3xga). Other listed operators: Events Creche (Northamptonshire), Teepees Treats & Events, Cherub Event Minders, Willow and Wander — [Guides for Brides](https://guidesforbrides.co.uk/wedding-entertainment/northamptonshire/events-creche/166231); [Bridebook](https://bridebook.com/uk/wedding-entertainment/cherub-event-minders-wigan-greater-manchester-csqgOm4a8p). Dizzy Ducks offers a mobile wedding crèche — [Nursery World](https://nurseryworld.co.uk/news/article/dizzy-ducks-offers-mobile-wedding-creche)
- **Indian-wedding proof abroad:** Tinies Crèches worked with Payal Events (Manchester) to provide childcare for the whole of day four of a multi-day Indian wedding. — [Tinies case study](https://tinies-old-static-luvt8.kinsta.page/www.tinies.com/event-childcare/event-childcare-what-others-think/case-study-bespoke-package-for-a-wedding.html)
- **Abroad, market size (older):** the UK wedding-crèche sector was valued at £4 million, and childcare.co.uk reported "a 168 per cent increase in demand for wedding crèche services over the last two years". **(older: Telegraph, June 2018)** — [Telegraph via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/rise-wedding-cr-che-three-073858176.html)
- **India demand:** 46 lakh weddings (1 Nov–14 Dec 2025) — [CAIT](https://cait.in/wedding-season-2025-to-generate-₹6-5-lakh-crore-business-from-46-lakh-weddings-across-india-cait-delhi-alone-to-witness-₹1-8-lakh-crore-trade-from-4-8-lakh-weddings-indian/). 1 in 4 is a destination wedding — [Skift](https://skift.com/2025/12/10/indias-72-billion-wedding-season-is-reshaping-travel-again/)
- **India competitors:** BollywoodShaadis advises hiring "mobile crèche service providers that will happily come to your wedding", but names no company, city or price. — [BollywoodShaadis](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/amp-articles/special-creche-for-children-at-the-wedding-716). A search for wedding crèche or nanny services in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru returned only the NGO Mobile Creches (founded 1969; crèches for construction-site children), which is not a competitor. — [Wikipedia: Mobile nurseries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_nurseries). **Honest count: 0 dedicated brands found** (search-limited).

### Inferences
- **How it runs without founder skills:** the founder sells to wedding planners, venues (5-star banquet and destination resorts), decorators and NRI families, using Reels of "parents actually dancing at the sangeet". Delivery comes from a roster of 6–10 freelance caregivers: pre-school/Montessori teachers (free in the late-Dec winter break in North India), trained nannies from agencies, and a lead supervisor with paediatric first aid. Activity kits are pre-packed per age band.
- **Startup cost [EST], about ₹3,00,000 including reserve:**

| Item | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Play and activity kit (foam mats, 2 play tents/teepees, craft and colouring supplies, Montessori toys, books, projector for movies, ID wristbands, walkie-talkies, baby-change kit) | 80,000 |
| Branded T-shirts, standee, signage | 15,000 |
| Paediatric first-aid/CPR course for roster (6–8 people) | 15,000 |
| Staff police verification and a lawyer-drafted safeguarding policy, parent consent and indemnity forms | 15,000 |
| Public/event liability insurance (availability for this use unverified) | 15,000 |
| Simple website and booking form; photo and video shoot at a pilot event | 20,000 |
| Launch marketing (Reels ads, planner meet-ups, 2 free showcase sessions) | 40,000 |
| Working capital and reserve | 1,00,000 |

- **Time to launch [EST]:** 3–5 weeks (kit, roster, two showcase weddings by mid-November). This catches the Dec–Feb peak and the April–May season.
- **Pricing and unit economics [EST]:** ₹18,000–25,000 per 5-hour evening for up to 12 kids (1 lead plus 3 carers), ₹1,500 per extra child, and ₹60,000–1,20,000 for a 3-day wedding package. Direct cost per evening is about ₹13,000 (4 carers × ₹2,500, consumables ₹1,500, transport ₹1,500), giving a gross margin of about 30–45%. At 15 evenings in the Dec–Feb peak, contribution is about ₹1–1.5 lakh. Planner referral fees (10%) come out of this.
- **Regulation and risks:** I found no India-specific licence for event childcare (gap). Child-safety duties under POCSO norms and venue rules still apply, so police-verified staff, 1:3 or 1:4 ratios for under-5s, sign-in/sign-out with photo ID, no-photo policies and an incident log are essential. Key risks are injury or lost-child liability, one bad review ending the business, seasonality, and planners bundling a cheap "kids' zone" (tattoo artist plus game stall). Differentiate as *certified childcare*, not entertainment.
- **City fit:** strongest in Delhi NCR (4.8 lakh weddings), Mumbai, Ahmedabad (Gujarati NRI weddings) and Hyderabad; destination-wedding hubs (Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa) can be served on travel packages.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 traction figures for UK or US wedding crèche firms; the £4M market figure is from 2018.
- Indian liability-insurance products for event childcare, and any state-level licensing, were not researched.
- Instagram-only Indian "kids' zone" vendors were not exhaustively searched; some may call themselves crèches.

## Q2. Idea 2 — "Desi Roots December Camp": 1–2-week heritage day-camps for NRI/OCI kids visiting their grandparents (Heritage Camps / diaspora culture-camp model)

### Takeaway
Abroad, diaspora and adoptee families pay for structured heritage camps; a US nonprofit even runs an Indian/Nepalese heritage camp. In India, NRI families arrive in December with kids aged 5–14 who are bored at grandparents' homes. I found only summer or local-kid culture camps and **no December day-camp product in a metro for visiting NRI kids (0–2 adjacent players)**. Timing is perfect for a ~20 Dec 2026 start, and partners (teachers, artists, guides) deliver everything.

### Cited Findings
- **Abroad:** Heritage Camps for Adoptive Families (Denver nonprofit) runs an **Indian/Nepalese Heritage Camp** for adoptees with Indian or Nepalese birth heritage and their families. It includes dance, music, rangoli, mehndi, cooking demos and cricket, with separate workshops for kids, teens and parents; the 2026 edition was 11–14 June at YMCA Snow Mountain Ranch, Colorado. — [heritagecamps.org](https://www.heritagecamps.org/?p=1334)
- **Abroad (diaspora side):** an Udupi-inspired culture camp in the US "fosters heritage pride among NRI kids" (Aug 2025). — [News Karnataka](https://newskarnataka.com/udupi/udupi-inspired-culture-camp-in-us-fosters-heritage-pride-among-nri-kids/07082025/). Singapore's Indian Heritage Centre runs year-end (Nov–Dec) school-holiday programmes on Indian performing arts. — [The New Age Parents](https://thenewageparents.com/indian-heritage-centre-year-end-school-holidays/)
- **Diaspora-youth heritage programmes are an established format abroad** (e.g., Punjab's earlier "Connect with your Roots" programme for 16–22-year-olds of Punjabi origin). — [CGI Vancouver PDF](https://www.cgivancouver.gov.in/public_files/assets/pdf/Connect-with-your-Roots_jan03.pdf) **(older; date not shown)**; [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/government-to-play-host-to-punjabi-origin-elderly-nris-visiting-state-421141/amp)
- **India competitors found:**
  - Jnana Prabodhini, Harali (Solapur, Maharashtra): a summer camp for children visiting from abroad — [Maayboli post](https://www.maayboli.com/node/34674) **(older; date unclear; rural and summer, not a metro December camp)**
  - Hare Krishna Movement, Hyderabad: a 21-day culture camp (Bharatanatyam, Gita shlokas, rangoli, crafts) for local school students in summer — [Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/hare-krishna-launches-21-day-culture-camp-for-kids-1874296)
  - An unnamed "enrichment program for NRI children aged 10–15" appeared in search summaries but could not be attributed.
  - **Honest count: 0 metro December camps for visiting NRI kids found; 1–2 adjacent.**
- **Demand pool:** 15.85M NRIs and 19.57M PIOs — [MEA](https://www.mea.gov.in/population-of-overseas-indians.htm); 628,305 Indian children at school level abroad — [The PIE News](https://thepienews.com/outbound-indian-university-enrolments-fall-after-three-year-rise/)

### Inferences
- **How it runs:** the founder sells via NRI parent WhatsApp and Facebook groups (Bay Area, NJ, Toronto, London, Dubai and Singapore Indian associations), Instagram Reels and school alumni networks. Partners deliver: a venue (a CBSE school or activity centre on winter break, or a club), freelance instructors (Bollywood or folk dance, tabla, block-printing or Madhubani, mother-tongue storytelling, kite-making for Uttarayan in Ahmedabad), a heritage-walk guide, a farm or village day-trip operator, and a bus. The founder is host, MC and content-maker.
- **Startup cost [EST] for one 2-week cohort of 25 kids (weekdays, 9:30–2:30):** venue ₹50,000; instructors ₹70,000; 2 field trips (bus plus entry) ₹30,000; materials and T-shirts ₹25,000; snacks ₹25,000; first-aid and insurance ₹10,000; marketing ₹40,000. That is about ₹2.5 lakh, largely funded by pre-sold seats, with a ₹50,000 reserve.
- **Pricing [EST]:** ₹15,000–20,000 per child for 2 weeks (about $170–230, cheap by US camp standards) and ₹1,500 per drop-in day. At 25 kids × ₹18,000 = ₹4.5 lakh, contribution is about ₹2 lakh per cohort; break-even is about 14 kids. Two cohorts (20 Dec–2 Jan and 3–14 Jan) are possible.
- **Time to launch:** 6–8 weeks, which is tight. Book the venue and instructors by end-October and open pre-sales by 1 November, since NRI families plan December by early November.
- **Regulation and risks:** child safeguarding (police-verified staff, ratios, pickup authorisation); Delhi NCR winter air quality may force indoor-only days (unsourced risk); NRI arrival dates are staggered by weddings, so drop-in day passes are needed; one-season business, possibly extended to a June edition for Gulf NRIs (Gulf schools break in summer).
- **City fit:** Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi-adjacent and Delhi NCR have heavy NRI December return flows (inference from the Gulf and US diaspora mix; not sourced by city).

### Gaps
- No sourced pricing for Indian kids' holiday camps or for diaspora heritage camps (HCAF fees not shown).
- No data on how many NRI children visit India in December.
- Birthright Israel-style participant numbers could not be retrieved (search budget exhausted).

## Q3. Idea 3 — "Roots Journeys": ancestral-village tracing and guided homecoming trips for the diaspora, especially PIO and girmitiya descendants (Ancestry/heritage-travel model)

### Takeaway
"Roots travel" is a top 2025 travel trend abroad (Skift/Marriott: 43–45% of 18–34-year-olds have taken a heritage trip). India has about 1.96 crore PIOs, including descendants of indentured labourers (girmitiyas) in Fiji, Trinidad, Guyana, Suriname, Mauritius and South Africa. Supply in India is a few government schemes and **1 private operator found (Metrolink Tours)**. The ticket is high and the work can be delivered by partner genealogists and guides. Sales cycles are long, so expect Jan–Mar 2027 trips, not December.

### Cited Findings
- **Abroad trend:** Skift's research with Marriott Bonvoy found 43% of 18–24-year-olds and 45% of 25–34-year-olds had taken a heritage trip, citing that "it was important to know where their family came from". — [Skift, 25 Nov 2024](https://skift.com/2024/11/25/new-report-marriott-bonvoys-ticket-to-travel-redefining-traveler-priorities-for-2025/)
- Search data reported "ancestry travel" up 1,618% and "genealogy tours" up 6,671% in 12 months (via search summary; source page returned 403, so unverified). — [Business Traveller](https://www.businesstraveller.com/features/travel-trends-back-to-the-roots/). Irish ancestry tours are a named heritage-travel trend. — [Travel Market Report](https://travelmarketreport.com/destinations/articles/ireland-ancestry-tours-trend-heritage-travel)
- **India and the diaspora:** girmitiyas were indentured Indians taken to Trinidad and Tobago, Fiji, Mauritius, Guyana, Suriname and South Africa. — [LAEX](https://laex.in/prelims-fact-sheet/girmitiya-heritage-trinidad-diaspora/). Descendants make pilgrimages to Kolkata's Hooghly depots (Garden Reach, archives, memorials). — [Indian Newslink (NZ)](https://indiannewslink.co.nz/the-hooghly-river-remains-a-silent-witness-to-girmitya-history); [La Source, Jun 2025](https://thelasource.com/selected/2025/06/10/echoes-of-home-connecting-with-girmitiya-history/)
- **India supply, government:** the "Discover your Roots" scheme (Ministry of Overseas Indian Affairs directive; UP) had located the native places of "at least 20 people", for a **USD 150 fee** plus documents such as an old letter or immigration receipt. — [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/india/up-wooing-expatriates-discover-your-2572860) **(older; page 403 on fetch, date unknown, likely pre-2024)**. Punjab runs NRI heritage tours for over-60s and earlier ran "Connect with your Roots" for 16–22-year-olds. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/government-to-play-host-to-punjabi-origin-elderly-nris-visiting-state-421141/amp)
- **India supply, private:** "Metrolink Tours is a pioneer in organizing customized heritage tours for NRIs", with ancestral home and temple visits, DNA-test coordination, record retrieval and translation, and private rituals. — [NRI Globe](https://nriglobe.com/entertainment/reconnect-with-your-roots-a-guide-to-heritage-tours-for-nris/) (via search summary; page did not load on fetch, city unknown). An academic or community "GIRMITIYA project" does archival and oral-history mapping (not a commercial tour). — [UoH Herald](https://herald.uohyd.ac.in/?p=38880)
- **Honest count:** 1 private operator (Metrolink Tours) plus 2–3 government or academic programmes.

### Inferences
- **How it runs:** the founder runs Instagram/TikTok-style Reels ("I found my great-great-grandfather's village") targeted at Fiji, Trinidad, Guyana, Mauritius, South Africa, UK-Gujarati/East-African and Canadian-Punjabi diaspora, and partners with diaspora cultural associations and consulates' diaspora days. Partners deliver: a freelance genealogist or archive researcher (emigration passes, depot registers, land records), a local fixer and translator in the ancestral district (Bihar, eastern UP, Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Gujarat, Punjab), a driver and guide, and a videographer to film the homecoming (a premium add-on).
- **Startup cost [EST]:** website and diaspora-specific landing pages ₹25,000; retainer for 3 pilot research cases ₹45,000; one sample "roots film" ₹30,000; diaspora-geo-targeted ads ₹50,000; GST registration, contracts and payment gateway ₹15,000. Total about ₹1.65 lakh, plus ₹1.35 lakh reserve.
- **Pricing [EST]:** research report ₹25,000–45,000 (about $300–500, against the government's $150 fee); 4–6-day guided roots journey ₹1.5–3 lakh per family excluding flights; roots film add-on ₹40,000–80,000. Gross margin 25–35%, so 3–4 journeys plus 8–10 research reports a season would earn ₹4–6 lakh gross profit.
- **Time to launch:** 3–4 weeks to open research orders. First trips are likely Jan–Mar 2027 (the Caribbean and Fiji diaspora need 2–4 months to plan).
- **Regulation and risks:** GST and tour-operator registration (Ministry of Tourism approval is optional, but it adds credibility). Archive access rules and success rates are uncertain (some records are lost), so sell research first with a "no-trace partial refund" clause. Payments are cross-border. There is also an emotional-expectations risk. **City fit:** Kolkata (depot history, archives) and Chennai (Madras depot) give a real edge; Delhi or Lucknow suit the eastern-UP and Bihar ancestry belt.

### Gaps
- Metrolink Tours' location, pricing and volume are unverified.
- No figures were found for how many diaspora members visit India on roots trips.
- The success rates of archive tracing in Indian state archives are unknown.

## Q4. Idea 4 — "Batch Reunion Co.": turnkey school and college reunions timed to the NRI December homecoming (US reunion-planner model)

### Takeaway
The US has a decades-old reunion-planning industry (classmate-finding, venue, DJ, memory book, sold per head). In India, December is already "reunion month" because NRIs are home; institutions such as BIT Mesra reserve 1–20 December for alumni meets. But reunions are organised by alumni offices or volunteer committees, and I found **0 private reunion-planning companies**. Capex is very low and the founder's selling and Reels skills fit. Abroad evidence is mostly old or estimated, and lead times are long.

### Cited Findings
- **Abroad (older):** reunion-planning firms have operated since 1982. In 1989, 10–15 million Americans attended family, military or school reunions, and planners charged $35–40 per head (over $50 in big cities) for invitations, name tags, hall, DJ, meal, photos and a "memory book". **(older: 1989)** — [Christian Science Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/1989/0613/preu.html)
- **Abroad (estimates):** National Reunion Services and similar firms are in an estimated $5–25M revenue band — [Owler](https://www.owler.com/company/gradtrac/competitors). ReunionPlanner.com has an estimated revenue of about $4.4M — [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/reunionplanner-com-revenue). (Both are third-party estimates, not audited.) Armed Forces Reunions Inc. is cited as the largest US military-reunion company. — [Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/military-reunions-sustain-norfolk-meeting-planning-firm/)
- **India, December demand:** BIT Mesra designates 1–20 December for organising alumni meets. — [BIT Mesra](https://bitmesra.ac.in/Other-Department-Pages/content/1/536/579). IIT Delhi's alumni office runs batch reunions such as a 20–22 Dec 2024 Pearl Reunion. — [IIT Delhi Alumni](https://alumni.iitd.ac.in/alumni-reunions). Woodstock School recommends at least 9 months of planning. — [Woodstock School](https://www.woodstockschool.in/alumni/can-we-help-you/planning-an-event-or-reunion/). One school reunion had 50 attendees, 30 of them flying in from the US, Canada and elsewhere. — [Mylapore Times, Dec 2018](https://mylaporetimes.com/2018/12/reunion-of-p-s-senior-1993-batch-on-dec-27) **(older)**
- **India competitors:** search found "no specific dedicated private companies specializing exclusively in school alumni reunion planning"; reunions are run by institutions or alumni committees (search summary of results above). **Honest count: 0.**

### Inferences
- **How it runs:** the founder pitches batch WhatsApp-group admins ("25 years since Class of 2001; we do everything, you just show up"). Partners deliver: hotel or club banquet, DJ or band, photographer and videographer, a "memory book" or yearbook designer and printer, and a classmate-tracer freelancer (LinkedIn and Facebook search). The founder runs ticketing, the RSVP sheet and Reels of "then vs now".
- **Startup cost [EST]:** brand, website and RSVP or ticket tool ₹20,000; memory-book template design ₹15,000; a sample event or shoot ₹25,000; ads ₹30,000. Total about ₹90,000; keep about ₹2 lakh as reserve and venue advances (collect 50% from attendees upfront).
- **Pricing [EST]:** ₹3,500–6,000 per head all-in (venue, F&B, DJ, photos, memory book) with a ₹800–1,200 per head margin. An 80-person reunion gives about ₹65,000–95,000 gross profit. Alternative: a 12–15% planning fee on the committee's budget.
- **Time to launch:** 2–3 weeks to start selling. For December 2026, target small (30–60 person) batch dinners booked by mid-November. Larger 25th and 50th reunions should be pre-sold for Dec 2027.
- **Risks:** committees may prefer DIY, collections can be slow, venue minimum guarantees bring no-show risk, and the market is seasonal. Abroad proof is old (1989) or third-party estimated, so this is the weakest "proven abroad" case in the list.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 data on the US or UK reunion-planning industry was retrieved.
- No Indian data on the number of school or college reunions held each December.

## Q5. Idea 5 — "Saloon Journeys": curated private rail-saloon trips built on IRCTC's saloon/coach charter (luxury-rail boom model)

### Takeaway
Luxury and slow rail is booming abroad (Railbookers +68% luxury-rail bookings in 2024; new Belmond trains in 2025). Indian Railways lets anyone charter a heritage-style **saloon car** (living room, attached bath, kitchenette, valet) attached to normal Mail/Express trains via the IRCTC FTR portal, but almost nobody packages it as a consumer experience. I found **0–1 private curators** (an IRCTC-affiliated luxury-train site). Capex is near zero because customers fund the charter. Lead time is at least 30 days and Railways availability is the main risk.

### Cited Findings
- **Abroad:** "Railbookers reported a 68% rise in bookings for luxury rail journeys in 2024 compared with the previous year". 2025 launches include Belmond's L'Observatoire (March) and Britannic Explorer (July). — [Luxe Beat](https://luxebeatmag.com/?p=866693); [Travel Market Report](https://travelmarketreport.com/niche-luxury/articles/rail-trends-to-look-out-for-in-2025). LVMH is pushing into luxury travel through Belmond. — [Skift, 16 Dec 2024](https://skift.com/2024/12/16/lvmhs-new-luxury-travel-push-with-belmond/)

Saloons "can be attached in all Mail/Express trains without any restriction", and trains can be chartered for marriage parties and religious tours. Booking is via the FTR portal 30 days to 6 months ahead, with a **₹50,000 security deposit per coach** payable within 6 days. — [IRCTC Tourism blog, Apr 2019](http://blog.irctctourism.com/charter-coach-train-or-saloon-cars-with-irctc/) **(older)**; [IRCTC charter page](https://irctctourism.com/charter)
- **India pricing:** "total booking cost reaches approximately Rs. 2 lakh for short tours and can go over Rs. 3 lakh" (search summary; not verified). Northern Railway confirmed a ₹3.08 lakh saloon charter for a puja trip. — [Railway News](https://railwaynews.net/northern-railway-confirms-rs-3-08-lakh-saloon-puja-charter.html); see also [Udayavani](https://english.udayavani.com/article/now-luxury-on-railway-saloons/282209)
- **India competitors:** IRCTC itself (direct FTR booking, plus its own luxury trains) and Indianluxurytrains.com, which blogs and sells luxury-train and saloon products. — [Indian Luxury Trains blog](https://www.indianluxurytrains.com/blog/irctc-launches-luxury-railway-saloon). No curated consumer "saloon experience" brand was found. **Honest count: 0–1 curators, plus IRCTC.**

### Inferences
- **How it runs:** the founder designs 3–4 signature routes (e.g., Delhi–Kalka plus the Shimla toy train; Mumbai–Goa Konkan; Howrah–New Jalpaiguri for Darjeeling; Chennai–Madurai). The founder sells to NRI multi-generation families, wedding parties ("baraat by saloon"), milestone birthdays and anniversaries, and corporate leadership offsites. IRCTC and Railways run the train; partners provide an on-board caterer (if permitted), décor, a photographer, destination hotel and guides. Reels of the vintage interiors carry the marketing.
- **Startup cost [EST]:** route recce and one shoot ₹40,000; deck, website and legal terms ₹30,000; ads and outreach ₹40,000. Total about ₹1.1 lakh, plus ₹1.9 lakh reserve. The ₹50,000 deposit and charter fees are collected from the client before booking.
- **Pricing [EST]:** ₹4.5–6 lakh per 2-day package for 8–10 guests (charter plus food, décor, photography and destination stay), with a 15–25% margin of about ₹70,000–1.2 lakh per trip.
- **Time to launch:** 3–4 weeks to sell. First departures need at least 30 days of lead time, so mid-January 2027 at the earliest; plan Dec 2026 bookings now for Jan–Mar.
- **Regulation and risks:** whether a third party may resell or package saloon charters is unverified (gap); limited saloon availability and Railways feasibility review; food-service rules on board; cancellation and refund terms set by Railways; heavy reliance on one supplier. Wow-factor content is high, so it also works as a lead magnet.

### Gaps
- Current (2025–26) saloon tariffs, minimum charges and the actual number of saloons available for charter.
- Whether IRCTC permits travel agents to book FTR saloons on behalf of clients, and the commission terms.
