## Q1. How big and how real is the demand (NRIs, elderly living alone, fraud exposure, willingness to pay)?

### Takeaway
The pool of buyers is large: about 15.9 million NRIs, and roughly 149 million Indians aged 60+, of whom about 26% live alone or only with a spouse (LASI, 2017-18). Fraud against seniors is a fast-growing, front-page problem. India lost ₹22,845 crore to cyber fraud in 2024, and the Supreme Court has said "digital arrest" victims, mostly elderly, lost over ₹3,000 crore. NRIs already pay roughly $25 per hospital visit and $999 a year for care plans. No official dataset counts NRI parents who live alone.

### Cited Findings
**Diaspora size**
- MEA estimates about 35.4 million overseas Indians in 2025–26: 15.85 million NRIs (Indian passport holders) and 19.57 million PIOs. The US has the largest Indian-origin population, followed by the UAE, Malaysia, Canada and Saudi Arabia. — [MEA, Population of Overseas Indians](https://www.mea.gov.in/population-of-overseas-indians) (page returned 403 when fetched directly; figures are from a search summary of the MEA page)
- Overseas Indians grew from about 161.8 lakh in 2012 to 343.6 lakh in 2025. NRIs grew by over 70% and PIOs by about 45% over the period. — [Factly](https://factly.in/saudi-arabia-us-canada-and-gulf-nations-lead-growth-in-overseas-indian-population). Note: 343.6 lakh (Factly, 2025) and 35.4 million (2025–26) differ slightly, probably because they come from different MEA updates.

## Q2. How does a non-technical founder collect money from children abroad, and what legal, trust and safety rules apply across all concepts?

### Takeaway
Razorpay supports international cards at up to 3% plus 18% GST on the fee, with no setup fee. PayPal is simpler but costs about 7–9% all-in. The main trust levers are ID and police-verified partners, a same-companion promise, and live WhatsApp updates; all the sourced Indian companion firms use some version of them. The DPDP Rules' core consent and security duties start on 14 May 2027, so a December 2026 launch should build consent forms from day one.

### Cited Findings
**Payment rails**
- Razorpay charges a 2% platform fee on domestic payments (including UPI) and "up to 3%" on international cards. There is no setup fee or annual maintenance charge, and 18% GST applies to the fee. — [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)
- Razorpay's MoneySaver Export Account for bank transfers costs about 1% + GST with zero FX markup. — [Xflow/Skydo comparisons](https://www.skydo.com/compare/razorpay-pricing) (secondary; these are competitor sites)
- PayPal India charges 4.40% plus a fixed fee ($0.30 for USD) to receive international commercial payments, plus a 3–4% FX markup. The effective cost is about 7–9% (≈8.25%). — [Skydo, PayPal vs Razorpay](https://www.skydo.com/compare/paypal-vs-razorpay); [Xflow](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/razorpay-vs-paypal) (secondary; competitors to PayPal)
- Presenza already prices in USD, GBP and AED, takes payment after the visit, and books over WhatsApp. That shows multi-currency, pay-after-visit is workable for small operators. — [Presenza](https://presenza.co.in/nri-families)

**Tax and registration** (from the sibling notes file)
- The GST registration threshold for services is ₹20 lakh. Inter-state supply of services is exempt from compulsory registration below that figure. — [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/gst-limit-registration-threshold-india/); [GSTZen](https://gstzen.in/a/integrated-tax-notification-102017-seeks-to-exempt-persons-making-inter-state-supplies-of-taxable-services-from-registration-under-section-232.html)

**Data protection (DPDP)**
- The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on 13–14 November 2025 and come into force in three phases:

  | Phase | Date | What starts |
  |---|---|---|
  | I | 13 Nov 2025 | Rules 1–2 and 17–21 (definitions and Data Protection Board provisions) |
  | II | 13 Nov 2026 | Consent-manager registration |
  | III | 14 May 2027 | Core duties for data fiduciaries: consent, security safeguards, breach reporting, retention and erasure; also protections for vulnerable persons |

  Sources: [Bitraser](https://www.bitraser.com/article/dpdp-rules-2025.php); [PIB document](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fcentre-notifies-dpdp-rules-implementation-planned-in-phases-spread-over-12-18-months-3798465)

**Vetting practices of existing players**
- Kurie: Aadhaar verification, police background check, training in elderly communication and hospital navigation, and a 24-hour replacement guarantee. — [Kurie](https://kurie.in/)
- Accompany (Chennai): background and reference checks, ID verification, interviews, and orientation in senior care, sensitivity and safety. — [Accompany](https://www.accompanyservices.in/)
- NRI Nestham: a "5-step selection process including background checks, reference verification, and skill assessments." — [NRI Nestham](https://nrinestham.com/)
- Papa (US): criminal background checks, driving-record reviews, and training in "empathy, cultural competency, and humility." — [KFF Health News, Mar 2022](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/medicare-advantage-plans-senior-companions-profits/)

**Cautionary precedents**
- Aaji Care (India, 2017) found untrained caregivers administering medicine improperly and had to bring in trained-staff requirements. Companions straying into medical tasks is a real liability risk. — [Scroll, 2017 (older)](https://scroll.in/article/832304/lonely-seniors-citizens-in-india-are-hiring-grandchildren-for-solace-and-help)
- KFF Health News reported criticism that Papa's in-home visits helped Medicare Advantage plans with risk-coding, a business-model reputation risk. — [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/medicare-advantage-plans-senior-companions-profits/)

### Inferences
- A service done in India for parents but paid for by children abroad is probably not an "export of service" for GST. Place of supply for services performed on persons in India is generally India. A CA should confirm this. Below ₹20 lakh turnover it is moot.
- Trust package that every concept here should copy:
  - Aadhaar plus police verification for every partner.
  - A same-person-every-visit promise.
  - A geo-tagged photo or video report on WhatsApp to the child abroad.
  - A written "no medical tasks, no handling money or OTPs" rule.
  - A signed consent form from the parent (DPDP-ready, since parents' health and financial data is sensitive).
- Price in INR and USD on separate pages: USD for Razorpay international cards, INR or UPI for siblings and children in other Indian cities.

### Gaps
- Per-check cost of private background verification in India (for example OnGrid, AuthBridge, IDfy) could not be verified; the OnGrid pricing page returned 404. An unverified working estimate is ₹300–1,500 per person per check type, and police verification through state police portals is often free or nominal.
- Public-liability or professional-indemnity insurance quotes for a small Indian companion or service business were not found.
- Stripe India's current onboarding status (reportedly invite-only) was not verified.

## Q3. Concept: companionship marketplace ("grandkids on demand", Papa-style vetted young companions)

### Takeaway
The format is proven at scale in the US: Papa is a SoftBank-backed unicorn with 25,000+ "Pals" and 65+ Medicare Advantage plan customers. In India it has 2–3 dedicated players, none clearly dominant:
- GoodFellows (Mumbai, Ratan Tata-backed, 2022; 2026 status unclear)
- Accompany (Chennai)
- Happy Folks Club (Bengaluru, volunteer)

The large care platforms bundle companionship, so the format is "niche but not empty". It fits the founder's sales skills, but delivery quality and safety risk are high.

### Cited Findings
**Papa (US)**
- Founded 2017 in Miami. It pairs college and nursing students, and other "Pals", with seniors for companionship, transport, errands and tech help. Its $150 million Series D was led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and made it a unicorn. — [Refresh Miami](https://www.refreshmiami.com/to-scale-the-power-of-human-connection-papa-raises-150m-series-d-and-we-have-a-new-unicorn/); [Venture Capital Journal](https://www.venturecapitaljournal.com/papa-snaps-up-150m-series-d/)
- Pals were paid up to $15/hour (2020). Papa is sold mainly as a benefit through health plans and employers. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/papa-raises-18-million-funding/)
- As of March 2022: 65+ Medicare Advantage plans, 25,000+ Pals with an average age in the mid-30s, and a projected 1 million+ companionship hours in 2022. Plans pay Papa a per-member monthly fee. — [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/medicare-advantage-plans-senior-companions-profits/)
- Getlatka estimates 2024 revenue at $148.1 million and says Papa reached it "without raising any venture capital". That claim contradicts Papa's known funding, so treat the revenue figure as unreliable. — [Getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/papa.com)

**Indian players**
- **GoodFellows** (Mumbai, launched 2022 by Shantanu Naidu, backed by Ratan Tata). It hires young graduates as companions, typically for 3 visits a week of up to 4 hours. It charged ₹5,000/month after a free month and planned expansion to Pune, Chennai and Bengaluru. — [The Week, 2022](https://www.theweek.in/leisure/society/2022/08/17/ratan-tata-unveils-start-up-goodfellows-to-senior-citizens-make-friends.amp.html); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/ratan-tata-backs-startup-that-staves-off-senior-loneliness-1.89952240)
  - 2026 status unverified: goodfellows.in did not resolve (DNS error) when fetched on 3 October 2026, which may mean a different domain or that the service has wound down.
- **Accompany** (Chennai, since 2021). Its "Sakhas/Sakhis" do doctor visits, temple visits, outings, home companionship and 1:1 or group yatras. Pricing is hourly and not published. It explicitly accepts bookings from children "living abroad or in another city". — [Accompany](https://www.accompanyservices.in/)
- **Happy Folks Club** (Bengaluru, run by teenagers). Its "Dosti" programme pairs seniors with young volunteers, for a symbolic ₹1 fee. — [Samarth, Jul 2026](https://care.samarth.community/long-distance-care-giving/blog-wellbeing-senior-citizen-activities-social-groups-bangalore/); [Vivan Angels/search summary](https://vivanangels.com/blog/top-5-parent-caregiver-services-to-trust-in-india)
- **Ashvasan Foundation** (Bengaluru) offers volunteer companion services. — [Samarth, Jul 2026](https://care.samarth.community/long-distance-care-giving/blog-wellbeing-senior-citizen-activities-social-groups-bangalore/)
- **LegUp** (Bengaluru, founded May 2024) is a platform for verified caregivers (elder and baby care) and planned to reach Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune and Delhi by end-2025. — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/leg_up)
- Generalists that bundle companionship: Samarth (outings, community events, hobby engagement), Emoha, Anvayaa. — [Samarth](https://care.samarth.community/all-cities/elder-care-services-in-delhi/); [Vivan Angels](https://vivanangels.com/blog/top-5-parent-caregiver-services-to-trust-in-india)
- Older precedent (2017): "grandchildren for hire" services were already running:

  | Provider | City |
  |---|---|
  | Aaji Care | — |
  | The Family Member | Ahmedabad |
  | Samvedna | Gurugram |
  | First Seniors | Delhi |
  | Maya Care | Pune |

  Prices ran from ₹150 per 1.5-hour visit to ₹20,000/month, and a young part-time visitor earned about ₹4,000/month. — [Scroll, 2017 (older)](https://scroll.in/article/832304/lonely-seniors-citizens-in-india-are-hiring-grandchildren-for-solace-and-help)

### Inferences
- Honest count for the exact format (paid, vetted young companions booked by remote children) in Indian metros: 2–3 dedicated players (GoodFellows if active, Accompany, plus volunteer Happy Folks Club), plus 3+ generalists. Several 2017-era players appear to have faded, so the format has struggled to monetise.
- How it would run (estimates):
  - Recruit psychology, social-work or nursing students and young retirees.
  - Pay ₹200–350/hour.
  - Charge ₹500–800/hour, or ₹4,000–8,000/month for weekly visits ($59–99 for NRIs).
  - Gross margin 35–50% before travel and replacement costs.
- Why it is not bigger yet (inference):
  - Indian parents resist "paying a stranger to visit".
  - Monthly churn is high.
  - Unlike the US, there is no insurer or health-plan payer.
  - Safety incidents are reputational landmines.
- Downgrade it to "medium" fit. It is better as an add-on to a narrower hook, such as scam-proofing, tech setup or festivals, than as a standalone.

### Gaps
- GoodFellows' current (2025–26) status, pricing and city footprint could not be confirmed.
- No Indian data was found on churn or repeat rates for companion subscriptions.
- An Instagram and Justdial sweep for small city-level "companion for elderly" operators was not completed (search cap).

## Q5. Concept: digital-safety and anti-scam coaching for seniors ("scam-proofing")

### Takeaway
This has the strongest demand signal (₹22,845 crore lost in 2024; 72% of surveyed seniors want workshops). Funded US analogues exist (Carefull, Scamnetic, Jortty), and Candoo Tech sells scam training. In India, most supply is free and one-to-many (police, HelpAge, NGOs) or bundled into apps (Khyaal) and online classes (Social Paathshala). Two formats are open:
- A paid, in-home 1:1 "scam-proof your parents" visit bought by NRI children, with device lock-down, family code word and mock-call drills.
- A paid B2B workshop for RWAs and employers.

For that exact format I found 0–1 players (Elderra.io, unverified).

### Cited Findings
**Abroad (US and Australia)**

## Q14. Overall comparison: which concepts fit (1–3 competitors, ₹3 lakh, about 2 months, sales- and Reels-led founder)?

### Takeaway
