# Book and Ebook Market 2025-2026: Market Size, Formats, Fiction vs Nonfiction, and Structural Shifts (for a first-time self-published ebook author)

Research date: October 3, 2026. All figures US unless stated. "Units" figures come from Circana BookScan, which tracks **print only** (point-of-sale print, not ebook or KU). "Revenue" figures come from AAP StatShot, which **covers only reporting publishers and leaves out most KDP/self-published revenue**. Neither source sees most indie ebook or Kindle Unlimited activity. Keep this in mind for every comparison below.

## 1. Latest US and global figures for print, ebook and audiobook sales (2025 and 2026 YTD)

### Takeaway
US print units were flat in 2025 (762.4M, +0.3%) and in H1 2026 (-0.3%). AAP publisher revenue rose 2.5% to $33.4B in 2025, with ebooks at $2.2B (+3.2%) and digital audio at $2.5B (+5.3%). Audio is now the bigger and faster-growing digital format. In H1 2026, trade ebooks fell 3.0% while trade digital audio rose 12.6%. Reader surveys still show print reaching the most people (64% of adults), then ebooks (31%), then audiobooks (26%).

### Inferences
- The market a first-time author enters is flat in print, with modest growth in publisher-reported digital. Audio is now the growth format. Publisher-reported ebook revenue is shrinking slightly in 2026 (-3.0% H1). That may reflect share moving to KU and indie titles rather than less ebook reading, since KU payouts grew about 11-13% over the same periods (see section 4).
- In AAP data, audiobooks ($2.5B) passed ebooks ($2.2B) for publishers in 2025. For an indie author, ebook plus KU is still the cheapest entry point, and AI narration (Spotify/ElevenLabs, section 6) lowers the cost of adding audio.
- Ebook readership skews young and college-educated (Pew). That matters for genre and marketing-channel choices.

### Gaps
- No public 2025 or 2026 total for the whole US ebook market including self-published titles. BookStat/Publishers Lunch figures are paywalled.
- Circana does not publish ebook or audio unit totals comparable to BookScan print.
- I found no reliable measured global audiobook revenue for 2025. The often-quoted "26.2% CAGR" comes from unnamed market-research firms via aggregators and should not be relied on.

## 2. Adult fiction vs adult nonfiction unit trends 2024-2026, with category winners and losers

### Takeaway
Adult fiction grew in print each year (about +5% in 2024, +1% in 2025, +5.7% in H1 2026). Adult nonfiction fell each year (-0.5% in 2024, -1.5% in 2025, -5.8% in H1 2026). Nonfiction is still the largest print segment. Its growth pockets were religion and self-help in 2025, but self-help fell 24.3% in H1 2026 once *The Let Them Theory* cooled. Biography/memoir and business were consistent decliners.

### Cited Findings
- 2024: adult fiction up nearly 5% vs 2023. Adult nonfiction down 0.5%. — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html) (search summary of article)
- 2024: adult nonfiction lost 1.3M units, with biography posting the steepest losses. Religion/Bibles and "family" (led by gift journals) grew. — [infoDOCKET on Circana 2024 results, Jan 22, 2025](https://www.infodocket.com/2025/01/22/circana-bookscan-book-sales-2024-results-a-year-of-fantasy-thriller-and-romance-data-and-highlights-top-selling-books-2024/)
- 2025 adult fiction +1%. Romance +3.9% to about 44M units. Fantasy -8.7% to 24.1M. Science fiction +22.1% to just over 6M (largest gain among adult fiction categories). Graphic novels +9.2% to 25.9M. Suspense/thriller -2.2% to 25.5M. — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)
- 2025 adult nonfiction -1.5% and still the largest segment by a wide margin. Religion +5.4% to 67M units. Self-help +14.7% (driven by *Let Them Theory*). Biography/memoir -7%. Business/economics -3.9%. — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)
- 2025 children's and YA: juvenile fiction +1.6%, juvenile nonfiction +3.6%, YA fiction -1.8%, YA nonfiction +3.8%. — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)
- H1 2026 vs H1 2025 (thousands of units): adult fiction 100,311 vs 94,869 (+5.7%). Adult nonfiction 122,332 vs 129,883 (-5.8%). Children's fiction +5.3%. Children's nonfiction +4.0%. YA fiction 11,074 vs 14,877 (-25.6%). YA nonfiction -7.4%. — [PW, Jul 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html)
- H1 2026 subcategories: self-help -24.3% (comping against Mel Robbins), general nonfiction -11.1%, graphic novels +36.2%, games/activities/hobbies +18.4%. YA supernatural/horror was the only YA fiction gainer. Top H1 2026 titles: *Theo of Golden* (Allen Levi) 1.198M, *Project Hail Mary* (Andy Weir) 829K, *The Let Them Theory* 566K, *The Correspondent* (Virginia Evans) 503K, *Oh, the Places You'll Go!* 482K. — [PW, Jul 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html)
- AAP revenue view for 2025: adult fiction $7.1B (+3.9%), adult nonfiction $6.0B (+1.3%), children's/YA fiction $5.4B (+0.9%), religious presses $2.2B (+9.3%). — [AAP StatShot Annual, Aug 10, 2026](https://publishers.org/news/aap-statshot-annual-report-publishing-revenues-totaled-33-4-billion-for-calendar-year-2025/). **Conflict:** an aggregator lists AAP 2025 adult nonfiction as $5.93B (-4%) — [selfpublishing.com](https://selfpublishing.com/publishing-by-the-numbers/). The primary AAP release says +1.3%, so prefer that. The -4% may be an older monthly cut.
- A secondary aggregator claims romance print sales of 51M units over the "latest 12 months" (+24% YTD 2025), citing Circana. This conflicts with PW's year-end figure of about 44M (+3.9%) and should be treated as unreliable. — [selfpublishing.com](https://selfpublishing.com/publishing-by-the-numbers/) vs [PW](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)

### Inferences
- Momentum is in fiction, especially romance, sci-fi (2025) and graphic novels (2025-26). Fantasy, after the romantasy peak of 2023-24, fell 8.7% in 2025. That suggests it is saturating rather than growing, though it is still large at 24M units.
- Nonfiction demand is concentrated in a few blockbusters (Robbins) and in religion. Without a breakout, self-help and general nonfiction fall sharply, as H1 2026 shows. Combined with AI saturation (section 5), a debut indie nonfiction ebook in self-help or "how-to" faces shrinking demand and maximum supply-side competition.
- The two-year rise of *Theo of Golden*, which began self-published and grew by word of mouth, shows breakout is still possible outside traditional channels. That is anecdotal and not a base rate.

### Gaps
- Kristen McLean (Circana) commentary for 2025 year-end and H1 2026 did not come through in fetched text.
- No ebook-specific fiction vs nonfiction split for 2025-26 was found. Circana and PW data are print only.
- Full PW category tables (cooking, health, history, true crime, etc.) were not accessible.

## 3. Indie/self-published share of ebook sales, and which genres dominate

### Takeaway
No publicly verifiable 2025 BookStat figure was found. Widely repeated aggregator claims put the indie share at about 30-34% of US ebook revenue or units, up to 51% of units, but they cite no source. The strongest hard evidence of indie scale is KU payouts to self-publishers: $711.3M in 2025. Romance is the dominant indie genre by far, followed by fantasy, sci-fi, thriller and mystery.

### Cited Findings
- Self-published share of US ebook units in 2025: 51%, and of revenue: 34%+. KDP share of Amazon ebooks: 31%. **No underlying source is named.** — [selfpublishing.com "Publishing by the Numbers 2026"](https://selfpublishing.com/publishing-by-the-numbers/); repeated by [bestwriting.com](https://bestwriting.com/ebook-statistics). Another site claims "30-40% of U.S. ebook unit sales, particularly in fiction," also unsourced. — [publishingstate.com, Jun 27, 2025](https://publishingstate.com/assessing-the-american-ebook-market-in-2025/2025/) (low-reliability source; its AAP figures are internally inconsistent)
- Older benchmark (Feb 2016, Author Earnings / Data Guy): indie authors accounted for about 45% of Amazon ebook unit sales and about a quarter of US consumer ebook dollars. Big Five had less than 25% of Amazon ebook purchases. — [ALLi, Feb 12, 2016](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/indie-authors-now-top-us-digital-book-sales/)
- BookStat (successor to Author Earnings) estimates sales from Amazon sales ranks and other sources, across all publisher types. Publishers Lunch now distributes BookStat data, but behind a paywall. — [Jane Friedman, "Why Book Sales Figures Are So Hard to Interpret"](https://janefriedman.com/why-book-sales-figures-are-so-hard-to-interpret-and-complete-sales-figures-nearly-impossible-to-find/); [Jane Friedman, "Publishers Lunch is now sharing Bookstat data"](https://janefriedman.com/publishers-lunch-is-now-sharing-bookstat-data/)
- KU payouts to self-publishers: 2020 $379.8M, 2021 $450.1M, 2022 $511.4M, 2023 $578.6M, 2024 $639.6M, **2025 $711.3M** (+11.2%). Cumulative Jan 2020 to Feb 2026: $2.58B. — [TNPS, Mar 19, 2026](https://thenewpublishingstandard.com/2026/03/19/kindle-unlimited-payout-2026-four-million-books-ebook-growth/)
- Written Word Media 2025 Indie Author Survey (1,346 responses, skewed to serious and prolific authors). Respondent genres: romance 21%, fantasy 14%, sci-fi 8%, thriller 8%, mystery 7%. Romance is about 44% of $10K+/month earners. Paranormal romance is about 11% of top earners vs 4% of all respondents. Cozy mystery is overrepresented above $500/month. 83% name Amazon as their top revenue source. 38% have all books in KDP Select and 30% are fully wide. — [Written Word Media, 2025 Indie Author Survey Results](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- WWM 2025 income: 44% earn $100/month or less. 20% earn $500-$5,000/month. 13% earn over $5,000/month and 8% over $10,000/month. About 80% of authors with 1-3 books earn under $100/month. Authors with 25+ books report a median of about $3,000/month. Average marketing spend is $636/month overall, about $4,500/month for $10K+ earners and about $81/month for under-$100 earners. Average email list is 902 subscribers for under-$100 earners and 18,327 for $10K+ earners. — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/); [bestwriting.com summary](https://bestwriting.com/how-much-do-authors-make)
- WWM 2025 formats: 98% publish ebooks, 97% paperback, 51% audiobooks, 36% hardback. — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- Older data (about 2016-2018, Author Earnings era): romance was about 45% of all ebooks sold or fully read on Amazon, including KU. 89% of romance sales were ebooks. Romance was the most-read KU genre of KU's first decade. — [IndieReader, Dec 2018](https://indiereader.com/2018/12/why-your-niche-category-can-make-a-big-difference-in-your-sales-romance-edition/); [The Bookseller, KU most-read books of the decade](https://thebookseller.com/news/amazon-kindle-unlimited-reveals-most-read-books-of-the-past-decade)

### Inferences
- Indie ebooks are a large share of the US ebook market, likely a third to a half of units. The exact 2025 number is not publicly verifiable, so the report should present it as a range and label it unverified.
- For a first-time author, WWM data say income tracks catalog size, series, email list and ad spend. A single debut ebook will most likely earn under $100/month. Genre fiction (romance, especially subgenres such as paranormal, and cozy mystery) gives the best odds. Nonfiction barely appears among top indie earners.

### Gaps
- No verifiable 2025-26 BookStat/Data Guy share figures (paywalled at Publishers Lunch).
- Kristine Kathryn Rusch commentary was not located within budget.
- No 2025-26 genre split of indie ebook revenue from a named data provider was found.

## 4. Kindle Unlimited: subscribers, KDP Select Global Fund, per-page rate, genres

### Takeaway
The KDP Select Global Fund grew from about $52M/month (early 2024) to $62-68M/month in 2026. That is about $700M for 2025 by monthly sum, or $711.3M per TNPS. The per-page rate has crept up but not kept pace with the fund: about $0.00435 average in 2024, $0.00444 in 2025 and $0.00466 for Jan-Aug 2026, ranging $0.0042-$0.0052. Amazon publishes no subscriber count. Romance has dominated KU for a decade.

Its headline KU share figures are behind a paywall. — [K-lytics](https://k-lytics.com/). Older data puts romance at about 45% of Amazon ebook sales and full KU reads. — [IndieReader, 2018](https://indiereader.com/2018/12/why-your-niche-category-can-make-a-big-difference-in-your-sales-romance-edition/)

### Inferences
- Calculated from WWM data: the Jan-Aug 2026 fund totals $518.2M, +12.6% vs $460.3M in Jan-Aug 2025. The mean per-page rate is about $0.00466 in 2026 vs $0.00444 in 2025 (+5%) and about $0.00435 in 2024 (excluding the Dec anomaly). Page reads are therefore also growing, by roughly 7%.
- Rule of thumb for a debut author: 100,000 KENP pages read is about $466 at the 2026 average rate. A 300-KENP novel read in full earns about $1.40, roughly comparable to a 70% royalty on a $1.99-$2.99 ebook sale.
- KU strongly favors long, bingeable genre-fiction series (romance subgenres, cozy mystery, LitRPG/progression fantasy). That is consistent with WWM's top-earner genre data. Short nonfiction gets few page reads.

### Gaps
- No official or credible KU subscriber count for 2025-26.
- No 2025-26 public K-lytics or BookStat breakdown of KU page reads by genre.
- KDP All-Star bonus amounts for 2025-26 were not verified.

## 5. Amazon's response to AI-generated books, and the impact on nonfiction

### Takeaway
Amazon has steadily tightened volume caps: 3 titles/day (Sept 2023), then 10/week (about 2025), then **2 new titles per format per week from Sept 21, 2026**. It has required disclosure of AI-generated text, images and translations since Sept 2023, and enforcement has reportedly escalated to the account level. AI saturation is heaviest in nonfiction niches. Originality.ai found 82% of 2025 "Herbal Remedies" titles and 77% of late-2025 "Success" self-help titles were likely AI-written. Kobo rejected 45% of 2025 submissions, mostly as AI slop.

### Cited Findings
- Sept 2023: KDP introduced required disclosure of AI-generated text, images and translations, and capped new titles at 3 per day. Amazon said "very few publishers will be impacted" and that the limit "may be adjusted." — [Authors Guild, Sep 27, 2023 update](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/)
- AI-generated vs AI-assisted: per KDP guidance as summarized by compliance guides, you must disclose AI-generated text, images (including covers and coloring pages) and translations. AI-assisted use (brainstorming, grammar, editing human text, improving human-made images) does not need disclosure. AI content is permitted if disclosed. Non-disclosure can lead to book blocking or removal and, if repeated, account termination. — [univers.studio KDP AI policy 2026](https://univers.studio/blog/ai-generated-content-kdp/); [builtwritten.com](https://www.builtwritten.com/blog/disclose-ai-in-your-book-kdp-policy-2026) (secondary guides; KDP's own content-guidelines page was not fetched)
- Volume caps: the limit was cut to 10 per week about a year before Sept 2026. **From Monday Sept 21, 2026, it is two new titles per week per format** (ebook, paperback, hardcover, so up to 6 new title records per week). Amazon's stated reason: to "help maintain a positive publishing experience for all authors." — [ALLi podcast, Sept 2026](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/podcast-amazon-cuts-kdp-uploads-to-two-titles-a-week/); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-publishing-limit/); [Author Media](https://authormedia.com/amazon-cracks-down-on-ai-authors). The allowance resets Sundays at 12:00 AM UTC and does not affect already-published titles. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-publishing-limit/). Official notice: [KDP Community, "Update on KDP Title Creation Limits"](https://www.kdpcommunity.com/s/article/KDP-Title-Creation-Limits-Update?language=en_US) (page did not render for fetch)
- Forbes (Bill Rosenblatt, Sep 30, 2026) covered the cut as "Amazon slashes indie book author upload limits" in the context of AI slop. — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/billrosenblatt/2026/09/30/amazon-slashes-indie-book-author-upload-limits-heres-why-the-music-industry-cant-do-the-same/) (403 on fetch; headline only)
- Low-confidence claims, unverified: "over 40% of new KDP titles in Q1 2026 involved AI assistance," attributed to "Amazon's own data," and "monthly new e-book releases on KDP nearly tripled between 2022 and 2025." — [AI Policy Desk](https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/amazon-kdp-ai-generated-content-surge-enforcement-2026). No primary Amazon source was found, so do not present these as fact.
- Herbal Remedies: Originality.ai scanned 558 paperbacks published Jan 1 to Sep 30, 2025. 82% were likely at least partly AI-written, and 96% had some likely AI content in summary, contents or author bio. It found fictional authors and dangerous foraging advice. — [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/blog/likely-ai-herbal-remedies-books-study); [Publishers Lunch, Nov 2025](https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2025/11/the-slippery-slop-of-ai-herbal-remedy-books-on-amazon/)
- Self-help "Success": of 844 books published Aug 31 to Nov 28, 2025, 77% were likely AI-written and 90% had some likely AI elements on their Amazon pages. 29 "repeat" authors (under 4% of authors) published 101 books (12% of the set). — [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/blog/likely-ai-success-self-help-book-study)
- Kobo: Kobo Writing Life rejected 45% of submitted titles in 2025, "hundreds of thousands." CEO Michael Tamblyn attributes more than 80% of rejections to manifestly AI-generated, poor-quality books (CONTEC Buenos Aires, April 2026). Kobo has not published totals or its methodology, and Tamblyn concedes detection tools misfire in both directions. — [The Next Web, Jun 26, 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/kobo-rejects-45-percent-self-published-books-ai)

### Inferences
- The two-per-format weekly cap will not constrain a first-time author. It mainly throttles mass AI publishers, and over time it may reduce clutter in nonfiction categories.
- The most AI-saturated categories are low-barrier nonfiction: herbal and health remedies, self-help/success, foraging, low-content and activity books. In those, a human debut author competes with very cheap volume, and reader trust in unknown authors is falling. Credentials, a real author bio, and genuine expertise or "look inside" quality are becoming differentiators.
- Combined with Circana's H1 2026 data (self-help -24.3%, general nonfiction -11.1%), nonfiction faces falling demand and a supply glut at the same time.

### Gaps
- No Amazon-published count of AI-disclosed titles or removals.
- No category-level sales-impact study isolating AI books' effect on human authors' nonfiction revenue.
- KDP's own help page on AI content was not fetched directly. The AI-generated vs AI-assisted wording comes from secondary guides.

## 6. Other 2025-2026 structural changes: Vella, royalties, platforms, direct sales

### Takeaway
Amazon has closed Kindle Vella (Feb 26, 2025). It cut print royalties on books under $9.99 to 50% (June 10, 2025) and launched free AI translation (Kindle Translate, Nov 2025). Spotify has become a serious indie audio channel: 1M Audiobooks+ subscribers and free ElevenLabs AI narration for US indie authors in 2026. Direct sales via BookFunnel/Shopify are becoming mainstream: 30% of indies already sell direct and 30% more plan to.

### Cited Findings
- Kindle Vella: Amazon said in Oct 2024 that it "hasn't caught on as we'd hoped." No new stories after Oct 28, 2024. The last day to publish episodes or buy tokens was Dec 4, 2024. Shutdown was Feb 26, 2025. Readers kept purchased episodes, and leftover tokens were refunded as Amazon gift cards. — [GeekWire, Oct 2024](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/amazon-to-shut-down-kindle-vella-serial-book-platform-saying-it-hasnt-caught-on-as-wed-hoped/); [selfpublishing.com](https://selfpublishing.com/kindle-vella-shutdown/); [KDP Help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GUJHP3574Z3DLPZD)
- KDP print royalty cut: from June 10, 2025, paperbacks and hardcovers priced under $9.99 USD (under €9.99, £7.99, CA$13.99, AU$13.99, ¥1000) earn 50% minus print cost instead of 60%. This was described as the first such cut in nearly 20 years. Ebook royalty tiers (35%/70%) were not reported as changed. — [KDP Help, print royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201834330); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-print-royalties/); [Indie Author Blueprint](https://indieauthorblueprint.substack.com/p/how-the-new-kdp-royalty-rules-affect)
- Kindle Translate: announced Nov 6, 2025 as a beta for select KDP authors. It is free, covers English↔Spanish and German→English, and delivers translations in days with automated accuracy checks and optional author preview. Amazon says fewer than 5% of Amazon.com titles exist in more than one language. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/amazon-launches-kindle-translate-for-independent-authors/); [Slator](https://slator.com/kindle-translate-amazon-create-ai-translated-ebooks-72-hours/); [iPhone in Canada, Nov 7, 2025](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2025/11/07/amazon-kindle-translate-ai/)
- Spotify (Investor Day, May 21, 2026): more than 700,000 audiobook titles in 22 markets. Listening hours +60% YoY. Nearly half of audiobook listeners started within the past year. Audiobooks+ add-on has 1M subscribers and about $100M annualized recurring revenue. Spotify for Authors expanded to 10 more languages. An ElevenLabs-powered audiobook creation tool entered invite-only beta in June 2026, English-only, with no exclusivity. — [Spotify Newsroom, May 21, 2026](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-05-21/investor-day-audiobook-features-updates/); [TechCrunch, May 21, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/21/spotify-launches-an-elevenlabs-powered-audiobook-creation-tool/); [The Bookseller](https://www.thebookseller.com/news/spotify-rolls-out-elevenlabs-audiobook-tools-in-next-chapter-of-books-strategy)
- The Spotify tool later opened to all US indie authors. It is free for up to 5 titles, converts EPUB to audio, and the audio can be distributed anywhere. — [ALLi, "Spotify Opens AI Audiobook Creation Tools"](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/spotify-opens-ai-audiobook-creation-tools/); [selfpub substack](https://selfpub.substack.com/p/spotify-free-ai-audiobook-production-authors)
- Feb 2025: ElevenLabs partnered with Findaway Voices (Spotify) to distribute ElevenLabs-narrated audiobooks wide. — [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/indie-author-courses/elevenlabs) (secondary)
- Kobo: rejected 45% of self-pub submissions in 2025 (see section 5), so it is a more curated but still open channel. — [TNW, Jun 26, 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/kobo-rejects-45-percent-self-published-books-ai)
- Direct sales: 30% of WWM 2025 respondents already sell direct and another 30% plan to within 12 months. About half of $10K+/month earners sell direct. Direct margins can reach about 95% minus processing vs a 70% maximum on Amazon. — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/); [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/direct-sales/bookfunnel-for-direct-sales). In 2025 BookFunnel added Fourthwall, its first merchant-of-record partner, alongside Shopify, Payhip, Gumroad, Thrivecart and WooCommerce. — [BookFunnel blog, 2025](https://blog.bookfunnel.com/2025/announcing-fourthwall-integration)

### Inferences
- Serial publishing moved off Amazon after Vella. Substack, Kindle-adjacent apps and direct Patreon/BookFunnel models are the remaining options, but no hard 2025-26 data was found on their size.
- Low-cost AI narration (Spotify/ElevenLabs, free for 5 titles) and AI translation (Kindle Translate) mean a debut ebook can be repackaged into audio and Spanish/German editions at near-zero cost. That also means more competition from AI-produced audio and translations.
- The 50% print royalty below $9.99 pushes indie paperbacks to $9.99 or more. The ebook remains the main margin product for a debut author.

- Not verified: whether Amazon changed ebook (35%/70%) royalty terms or KENP rules in 2025-26 (no reports found); and the Feb 2025 removal of Kindle "Download & Transfer via USB," which was not researched within budget.
