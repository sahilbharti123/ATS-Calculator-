# Fiction Genre and Subgenre Momentum, 2025-2026 (Ebook and Kindle Unlimited Focus)

Research date: October 3, 2026. US / English-language market. All dates are publication dates of the cited source. "Print" figures are Circana BookScan, which tracks about 85% of US print and does **not** cover ebooks or Kindle Unlimited (KU). The ebook/KU picture comes mostly from K-lytics (Amazon rank-based estimates), Written Word Media's author survey, and Amazon's own year-end posts. Where these two pictures disagree, that is noted.

---

## 1. Which fiction genres led sales and growth in 2025 and 2026 so far? Is romantasy still growing or plateauing?

### Takeaway
Adult fiction is the healthy part of the US print market: +1% in 2025 and +5.7% in H1 2026, while nonfiction and YA fell. The romance/romantasy boom peaked in early-to-mid 2025 (Onyx Storm) and then slowed sharply. Romance print was only +3.9% for full-year 2025 after running +24% at mid-year, fantasy print fell 8.7% in 2025, and romance dropped 25.4% in Q1 2026 against the Onyx Storm comparison. Growth has moved to science fiction, horror, manga/graphic novels and dark romance subtypes. Romantasy is plateauing and changing shape rather than collapsing, and two new Sarah J. Maas books (Oct 27, 2026 and Jan 12, 2027) will cause a short-term spike.

### Cited Findings
**2025 full year (print, Circana BookScan via PW)**
- Total US print units in 2025 were 762.4M, +0.3% vs 2024. The peak was 839.7M in 2021. Adult fiction rose just 1% in 2025. — [Publishers Weekly, "Print Book Sales Rose Slightly in 2025," Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)
- 2025 adult fiction by category: romance +3.9% to nearly 44M units; fantasy −8.7% to 24.1M; science fiction +22.1% to just over 6M; suspense/thriller −2.2% to 25.5M; graphic novels +9.2% to 25.9M. — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)
- Top 2025 print sellers: #1 *The Let Them Theory* (Mel Robbins, 2.8M), #2 *Sunrise on the Reaping* (Suzanne Collins, 2M), #3 *Onyx Storm* (Rebecca Yarros, 1.7M for the Wing and Claw edition alone). — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html)

**Mid-2025 romance surge (Circana)**
- Through early June 2025, US YTD romance print sales were +24% YoY. Romance volume had more than doubled vs four years earlier, at 51M units over the trailing 12 months. Romantasy and sports romance each showed triple-digit growth, and suspense romance and contemporary romance were also growing. — [Circana, "Another Year of Romance, with a Dark Twist," Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)
- Same report, related adult fiction: psychological thrillers +29%, dark fantasy +23%, horror +13%. Growth themes named: dark romance, paranormal, anti-hero, dystopian. Growing authors named: H.D. Carlton, Rina Kent, Elsie Silver. — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)
- *Onyx Storm* (Jan 2025) sold 2.7M copies in its first week, the fastest-selling adult fiction title in BookScan's 20-year history. — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)

**2026 so far**
- Q1 2026: romance print sales plunged 25.4%, largely because of *Onyx Storm*'s Q1 2025 sales. This figure comes from a search excerpt of PW's Q1 report; I did not read the full article. — [PW, "Print Sales Fell 3.1% in the First Quarter of 2026"](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100067-first-quarter-print-sales-fell-3-1.html)
- Manga posted a 45% unit gain in Q1 2026 and was the strongest category in the total print market. Circana says "escapist and speculative genres such as science fiction, horror, and Japanese-influenced manga" are performing well, and that YA readers may be moving toward manga and sci-fi. — [Circana, "Turning the Page on 2026 Book Trends," May 8, 2026](https://www.circana.com/post/turning-the-page-on-2026-book-trends)
- H1 2026: total print units −0.3%. Adult fiction **+5.7%**, adult nonfiction −5.8%, YA fiction **−25.6%** (against the *Sunrise on the Reaping* comparison), graphic novels +36.2%. Top three H1 sellers: *Theo of Golden* (Allen Levi, 1,197,952 units), *Project Hail Mary* (Andy Weir, 829,089), *The Let Them Theory* (566,244). PW did not break out romance, fantasy or other genres for H1. — [PW, "Print Book Sales Flat in First Half of 2026," Jul 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/100790-print-book-sales-flat-in-first-half-of-2026.html)

**Romantasy status**
- Everand's second annual State of Reading report, with data from Everand and the Fable app, predicted "romantasy fatigue" for 2026: readers are "straying away from the crowded landscape" that *A Court of Thorns and Roses* and *Fourth Wing* created. — [People via Yahoo, Dec 23, 2025](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/articles/romantasy-fatigue-even-more-special-195901198.html)
- In contrast, BookTok creators surveyed by The Bookseller said romantasy shows "no sign of stopping," with paranormal/supernatural romantasy as the sub-trend to watch. Several creators said epic fantasy and sci-fi (*Red Rising*, *The Will of the Many*) "may overtake romantasy." — [The Bookseller, Katie Fraser, Jan 16, 2026](https://www.thebookseller.com/books/books-on-booktok-2026-genre-predictions)
- Goodreads (Jan 1, 2026) still called romantasy "one of the hottest areas in all of publishing." Its 2026 anticipated list included Sarah A. Parker's *The Ballad of Falling Dragons* (May), Devney Perry's *Rites of the Starling* (Apr), Kerri Maniscalco's *Throne of Nightmares* (Feb) and several KU-badged indie titles. Themes it highlighted: dragons, mermaids, tarot magic, fae, dark romance, academy settings. — [Goodreads, "Readers' Most Anticipated Romantasies for 2026," Jan 1, 2026](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/3052)
- A secondary source (Atmosphere Press blog, undated in my results) says the 2023-24 template of "dragon academy, generic fae court, chosen one" is "cooling fast" while darker hybrids (dark romantasy, romance-horror, "femgore") rise. Low-authority source; treat as opinion. — [Atmosphere Press](https://atmospherepress.com/romantasy-trends-2026/)
- Sarah J. Maas announced on the *Call Her Daddy* podcast that ACOTAR Book 6 releases **Oct 27, 2026** and Book 7 on **Jan 12, 2027**. The story was written as one long work split across volumes, with at least one more book after that. — [Scary Mommy, Mar 5, 2026](https://www.scarymommy.com/lifestyle/sarah-j-maas-acotar-books-6-7-release-dates-revealed)
- As of August 2026, Yarros had announced no date or title for Empyrean Book 4. Fan sites say she began actively writing it around March 2026. — [Books of Brilliance (fan site), Aug 8, 2026](https://booksofbrilliance.com/2026/08/08/fourth-wing-book-4-release-date/) (low-authority fan source)

**Amazon/Kindle (UK data; I found no US equivalent)**
- Amazon UK's Kindle "Year in Reading 2025": *The Housemaid* (Freida McFadden) was the most-read fiction title. Crime/thriller/mystery was the most popular genre. Most-binged series were The Housemaid (McFadden) and Elsie Silver's Chestnut Springs and Rose Hill (sports/small-town romance). Top authors were McFadden, Yarros and Alice Feeney. — [About Amazon UK, 2025 (undated page)](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/books-and-authors/kindle-year-in-reading-most-read-books-2025)

### Inferences
- The romance number to quote is the slowdown, not the 24% mid-2025 growth figure. Romance went from +24% YTD in June 2025 to +3.9% for the full year, which means H2 2025 was roughly flat or down. Q1 2026 then fell 25% against a one-off blockbuster comparison. Romance is a very large, mature category that is no longer growing broadly, apart from tentpole releases (Maas, Yarros) and specific subtypes.
- Fantasy fell 8.7% in print in 2025 even though *Onyx Storm* was the year's top fiction title. That points to slowing breadth in mainstream romantasy below the top few authors.
- Sci-fi's +22.1% (2025) and *Project Hail Mary*'s #2 spot in H1 2026 likely reflect film and TV tie-ins (*Project Hail Mary*, *Sunrise on the Reaping*) and the BookTok shift toward "bigger" SFF. This is traditional print data. Indie Kindle sci-fi looks different (see section 3).
- For an indie ebook author, the Maas releases (Oct 2026 / Jan 2027) will bring a temporary surge of romantasy readers. A comp-title or "if you're waiting for ACOTAR" angle could be timed to it.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 Circana breakdown by romance subgenre (romantasy vs. sports vs. dark). The PW H1 2026 article reported only top-level categories.
- I found no US Amazon Charts year-end 2025 "most sold/most read" fiction list. Only UK Kindle data was retrieved.
- BookScan excludes ebooks and KU, so ebook-specific genre share for 2025-26 (e.g., from BookStat) was not found.

## 2. Which subgenres are rising or cooling?

### Takeaway
**Rising, with evidence:** dark romance (mafia, stalker, anti-hero, "dark dystopian romance"), sports/hockey romance (sharply boosted by HBO's *Heated Rivalry*, Nov 2025), queer/LGBTQ+ romance, monster romance and omegaverse/why-choose as indie-to-trad pipelines, cozy fantasy (the best supply/demand ratio in K-lytics' summer 2026 fantasy data), LitRPG/progression fantasy, dystopian (print and indie), horror (+7-13% in print, with femgore, romance-horror and literary horror as sub-trends), psychological thrillers, and epic fantasy/space opera among BookTok's most engaged readers.
**Cooling:** generic romantasy (dragon academy/fae court clones), indie sci-fi romance (−56%), colonization sci-fi (−96%), military space marine sci-fi (−84%), and myths & legends fantasy (−90%). These declines come from K-lytics' Kindle data.

### Cited Findings
**Dark romance / dark dystopian**
- Circana named dark romance with paranormal, anti-hero and dystopian themes as a 2025 growth driver, and cited H.D. Carlton and Rina Kent (both indie-origin dark romance authors). — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)
- Mafia romance is said to drive much of the dark romance trend on Amazon. This comes from a low-authority aggregator. — [SelfPublishing.com, 2026](https://selfpublishing.com/most-popular-book-genres-on-amazon/)

**Sports / hockey / queer romance**
- *Heated Rivalry* (Rachel Reid's Game Changers series; HBO/Crave series premiered Nov 28, 2025): unit sales of the book rose nearly 1,000% between the weeks ending Nov 29, 2025 and Jan 10, 2026. The trade paperback sold just under 23,000 copies in the week ended Jan 10, ranking #7 overall on BookScan, against about 42,000 for all of 2025. The Game Changers series rose nearly 4,000% to 206,000 copies in that window. *The Long Game* went from 2,495 to 30,567 release-to-date units. — [PW, "Rachel Reid's Heated Rivalry Is Feeling the Love," Jan 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/page-to-screen/article/99467-rachel-reid-s-heated-rivalry-is-feeling-the-love.html)
- BookTok creators predicted renewed sports romance and more LGBTQ+ contemporary romance in 2026 because of *Heated Rivalry* and the Feb 2026 Winter Olympics. Comps named: *The Wild Card* (Stephanie Archer), *Skate It Till You Make It* (Rufaro Faith Mazarura). — [The Bookseller, Jan 16, 2026](https://www.thebookseller.com/books/books-on-booktok-2026-genre-predictions)
- Sports romance had triple-digit print growth in mid-2025. — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)

**Indie-origin subgenres that traditional publishers acquired in 2025-26** (a strong signal of proven demand)
- PW's May 2026 roundup of self-pub-to-trad romance deals: Chelsea Curto (D.C. Stars hockey, Atria); Liz Tomforde (Windy City sports romance, Entangled/Amara); Brittanée Nicole (Boston Bolts hockey, Podium/Putnam); Emilia Emerson & Eliana Lee (A Pack series, **omegaverse/polyamory**, Putnam); Ashley Bennett (Leviathan Fitness **monster rom-com**, Berkley); Rebecca Thorne (*Can't Spell Treason Without Tea*, **cozy romantasy**, Bramble); C.R. Jane (*Dark Horse*, **dark romance**, Entangled/Amara); Lucy Smoke (Sons of Salem, **dark academia/dark romance**, St. Martin's Griffin); T.C. Kraven (Greek-myth paranormal romance, Diversion); Amber V. Nicole (Gods & Monsters **romantasy**, Kensington); Natasha Madison and Siera London (**small-town**); Jennie Goutet (Georgian **historical**, Baker/Haven). — [PW, "The Self-Pub to Trad Pipeline for Romance," May 8, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/new-titles/adult-announcements/article/100347-the-self-pub-to-trad-pipeline-for-romance.html)
- Editor quotes from the same piece: "The indie space allows for a lot of experimentation" (Kate Dresser, Putnam); "Indie authors can push the envelope faster and more aggressively" (Eileen Rothschild, St. Martin's). — [PW, May 8, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/new-titles/adult-announcements/article/100347-the-self-pub-to-trad-pipeline-for-romance.html)

**Cozy fantasy, LitRPG, and fantasy/sci-fi on Kindle (K-lytics summer 2026, June 2026 snapshot)**
- Cozy fantasy had the **highest sales-to-competition ratio in the report (23.1)**: about 2,576 competing titles, top-20 titles averaging about 60 sales/day each. — [Author Media "Zeitgeist" summary of K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- LitRPG: ratio 14.8, about 8,620 competing titles. K-lytics called it the "most structurally undersupplied success story." — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Other fantasy niche ratios: Fantasy-Romantic 10.2, Gaslamp 6.2, YA Dark Fantasy 6.1, Military Fantasy 5.0. Falling: Myths & Legends −90%, Norse/Viking −38% (12-month). — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Sci-fi subgenres rising on Kindle: Sci-Fi Adventure +49%, LGBTQ+ Sci-Fi +48%, **Dystopian +45%**, Space Exploration +27%. Falling: Colonization −96%, Military Sci-Fi Space Marine −84%, **Sci-Fi Romance −56%**. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- On Kindle, fantasy outsells sci-fi nearly 4:1. Top-20 fantasy titles average about 931 copies/day (rank about 40) vs about 237/day for sci-fi (rank about 331). — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* (LitRPG; Book 1 Oct 2020) moves an estimated 8,700 combined daily sales/borrows, with 6M+ estimated total sales. Dinniman keeps ebook/audio rights and licensed print to Ace in 2024. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/). (One search summary misspelled the author "Matt Denman"; the fetched article says Dinniman.)
- LitRPG is going mainstream with traditional publishers: Tor is publishing Will Wight's *Overworld*, Harper Voyager is publishing pirateaba's *The Wandering Inn* (a web serial), and a *Dungeon Crawler Carl* adaptation is in development at Peacock. "The LitRPG breakthrough is no longer an idea. It's happening right now." — [Reactor, Matthew Byrd, Sep 3, 2026](https://reactormag.com/the-rise-of-litrpg/)

**Horror**
- US horror print sales rose 7% in the first nine months of 2025 vs 2024 (BookScan). Booksellers point to gothic/classic horror, psychological dread, LGBTQ+ horror and women-authored horror. Titles cited: *Tender Is the Flesh* (Bazterrica), *American Rapture* (CJ Leede), *The Last House on Needless Street* (Catriona Ward). — [PW, "Booksellers Ride the Horror Wave," Oct 10, 2025](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/98826-entrepreneurs-ride-the-wave-of-popularity-of-the-horror-genre.html)
- Horror was +13% YTD as of June 2025 (Circana). — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports)
- BookTok creators predicted continued horror growth in 2026, especially "weird-girl horror," **femgore**, historical horror and literary horror. — [The Bookseller, Jan 16, 2026](https://www.thebookseller.com/books/books-on-booktok-2026-genre-predictions)
- Goodreads Choice 2025 Horror winner: *Witchcraft for Wayward Girls* (Grady Hendrix). — [Goodreads, Dec 2025](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/3030-meet-the-winners-of-the-2025-goodreads-choice-awards)

**Dystopian revival**
- BookTok creators predicted dystopian growth driven by "world uncertainty" and adaptations (*Sunrise on the Reaping*, *Red Rising*, *I Who Have Never Known Men*). — [The Bookseller, Jan 16, 2026](https://www.thebookseller.com/books/books-on-booktok-2026-genre-predictions)
- Indie dark dystopian romance *Daggermouth* (H.M. Wolfe) went viral; see section 4. Dystopian is +45% on Kindle sci-fi (K-lytics, above).

**Thrillers**
- Psychological thrillers were +29% YTD in mid-2025 (print) — [Circana, Jun 5, 2025](https://www.circana.com/post/another-year-of-romance-with-a-dark-twist-circana-bookscan-reports). Overall suspense/thriller was −2.2% for 2025 — [PW, Jan 9, 2026](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/financial-reporting/article/99417-print-book-sales-rose-slightly-in-2025.html). On Kindle UK, Freida McFadden's *The Housemaid* was the most-read fiction title of 2025 — [About Amazon UK](https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/news/books-and-authors/kindle-year-in-reading-most-read-books-2025).
- K-lytics (Aug 2026) estimates non-cozy female-detective mystery/thriller/crime on Kindle moves about 12,978 copies/day. This is from a search excerpt; I did not read the full article. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Aug 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/?p=32449)

**BookTok trope rotation (2026)**
- Current BookTok trope rotation: romantasy with dragons, why-choose polyamory, hockey grumpy-sunshine (*Icebreaker* clones), dark-mafia-billionaire, and professor-hero rom-coms. Low-authority aggregator. — [The Romance List, 2026](https://theromancelist.com/articles/best-booktok-romance)
- Paranormal romance keywords on Kindle (K-lytics, late 2025): fated, rejected, alpha shifter, reverse harem, omegaverse, fae, bonds. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Dec 20, 2025](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-christmas-romance-vs-paranormal-romance/)

### Inferences
- The strongest "underserved" signals for a new indie are **cozy fantasy** (ratio 23.1, relatively few competitors) and **LitRPG/progression fantasy** (14.8, structurally undersupplied). Both reward long series and are KU-heavy.
- Romance is where the most money is but also the most competition. The subtypes with fresh proof of demand in 2025-26 are hockey/sports (especially queer hockey after *Heated Rivalry*), dark/mafia/dystopian dark romance, monster rom-com, omegaverse/why-choose and cozy romantasy. Each now has at least one 2025-26 indie-to-trad deal.
- Traditional print and indie Kindle disagree on sci-fi. Print sci-fi is up 22%, driven by Weir, Collins and adaptations. Indie Kindle sci-fi romance is down 56%, and colonization and military space marine have collapsed. An indie should not read the print sci-fi boom as a reason to enter indie sci-fi romance. Dystopian and sci-fi adventure are the exceptions.
- Horror is growing but mostly in traditional and literary formats. Romance-horror blends ("horromance," femgore) are where it meets the KU audience.

### Gaps
- I found no hard 2025-26 data on cozy mystery growth. It is said to be stable, with high-earner presence (see section 6), but I found no growth metric.
- I found no data on folk horror, eco-horror, Asian-inspired fantasy, novella-length fiction or slow-burn/enemies-to-lovers as distinct trends. These were searched indirectly and did not appear in quality sources.
- I found no 2026 literary fiction trend data beyond Goodreads winners and *Theo of Golden*.
- Monster romance has a deal signal (Ashley Bennett/Berkley) but I found no sales or growth metrics.

## 3. Which genres dominate Kindle Unlimited and indie bestseller lists? What is saturated and what is underserved?

### Takeaway
Romance dominates indie publishing, and fantasy on Kindle is overwhelmingly KU-driven (78 of the top 100 fantasy titles are in KU, earning 83% of royalties). Supply is exploding (+18% per year in SF&F titles, about 490 new SF&F books a day) and AI-generated junk floods romance subgenres (62% of September 2025 paranormal romance releases were judged likely AI junk). The saturated areas are paranormal/shifter romance, seasonal/Christmas romance, generic romantasy and indie sci-fi romance. Undersupplied: cozy fantasy, LitRPG, gaslamp, fantasy-romantic, dystopian.

### Cited Findings
- KU penetration in fantasy: 78 of the top 100 Kindle fantasy titles are in KU, accounting for 83% of royalties. Sci-fi: 59 of 100 (41% wide). — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Revenue benchmarks (June 2026): the Kindle fantasy top 100 earns about $4.47M/month in royalties and the #100 title about $3,221/month. Sci-fi top 100 earns about $1.14M/month and #100 about $613/month. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Supply: English-language SF&F titles grew from 983,093 to 1,162,272 (+18%, about 179,000 per year, about 490 per day). Six-month bestseller list turnover is 51% in fantasy and 77% in sci-fi. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Christmas romance (K-lytics, Dec 2025): about 32,000 titles; about $1.83M/month in royalties excluding KU page reads; **about 84% of royalties come from KU**. The top title sells about 2,300/day and the #1,000 title about 3.9/day. About 50% of September 2025 releases had 0-1 reviews, and October 2025 saw a supply spike of low-quality AI titles. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Dec 20, 2025](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-christmas-romance-vs-paranormal-romance/)
- Paranormal romance: "62% of new paranormal romance titles released in September [2025] were likely junk AI books." — [Author Media/K-lytics, Dec 20, 2025](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-christmas-romance-vs-paranormal-romance/)
- Written Word Media's 2025 survey (1,346 indie authors): romance authors are about 21% of respondents but about 44% of the $10k+/month group. **Paranormal romance appears at about 2.5x its expected share in the top earning tier.** Cozy mystery shows up at 6-9% in every bracket above $500/month. — [Written Word Media, Dec 2, 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- High earners ($5k+/month): 43% are all-KU, 15% fully wide, the rest mixed. Across all respondents, 38% are KDP Select-exclusive and 30% fully wide. — [Written Word Media, Dec 2, 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- AI in KU: Book Riot reported a romance (*Darkhollow Academy: Year 2*, Lena McDonald) published with an AI prompt left in the text asking for a rewrite "in another author's voice." Amazon caps publishing at three books per day per account and requires AI disclosure to Amazon, but does not show it to readers. Because KU pays per page read, AI titles reduce human authors' share and visibility. — [Book Riot, Leah Rachel von Essen, Jun 30, 2025](https://bookriot.com/can-kindle-unlimited-survive-ai/)
- A low-authority source claims formulaic pseudonymous titles make up about 11-14% of new KU romance releases monthly. I could not verify this; treat it as unconfirmed. — [Alibaba product-insights page, undated](https://www.alibaba.com/product-insights/are-ai-written-romance-novels-flooding-kindle-unlimited-or-quietly-reshaping-genre-expectations.html)
- An unverified aggregator claims romantasy "dominates 57% of self-published sales." I found no primary source; I recommend not using this figure. — [SelfPublishing.com, 2026](https://selfpublishing.com/most-popular-book-genres-on-amazon/)

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** paranormal/shifter romance and seasonal romance. Both are lucrative for established authors (paranormal is over-represented among top earners), but new entrants compete with heavy AI spam and very long tails: the #1,000 Christmas romance sells about 4 copies a day. Generic fae/dragon romantasy is also crowded, as is indie sci-fi romance, which is shrinking.
- **Underserved:** cozy fantasy, LitRPG/progression fantasy, gaslamp, fantasy-romantic hybrids, dystopian, and sci-fi adventure. These are the clearest "worth entering" niches on supply/demand alone.
- In fantasy and romance on Kindle, KU enrollment is close to mandatory for a newcomer: 80%+ of royalties flow through it.
- The AI flood raises the value of human-trust signals: reviews, an author brand, an email list, a BookTok presence and good covers.

### Gaps
- I found no recent K-lytics romance-wide report with sales-to-competition ratios for hockey, dark/mafia, monster, why-choose or small-town (only Christmas and paranormal).
- I found no Amazon-published KU statistics (KDP Select Global Fund per-page rates for 2026 were not researched).

## 4. What are BookTok, Bookstagram and Goodreads driving now? Notable indie breakouts in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Goodreads 2025 winners were mostly established traditional names (Backman, Emily Henry, Yarros, Schwab, Hendrix). The breakout stories of 2025-26 came from outside traditional publishing: *Theo of Golden* (self-published 2023, 2.5M+ copies, #1 print seller of H1 2026), *Daggermouth* (self-published Dec 2025, 22.3M KU page reads, then a seven-figure US auction), and *Alchemised* (fanfiction turned into a 1M+ copy Del Rey novel). BookTok is now pushing dark romance, hockey/queer sports romance, paranormal romantasy, epic SFF, dystopian and femgore horror.

## 5. Serial fiction and new formats: Royal Road, Wattpad, the Kindle Vella shutdown, Substack, Patreon

### Takeaway
Amazon closed Kindle Vella (stopped new publishing and token sales Dec 4, 2024; fully closed Feb 2025) because it "hasn't caught on." Serial fiction has instead consolidated around Royal Road, then Patreon, then KU, then audio for LitRPG/progression fantasy (the strongest pipeline, which produced *Dungeon Crawler Carl* and *He Who Fights with Monsters*), Substack for literary or voice-driven serials, and AO3/Wattpad as a young, fandom-driven discovery layer (which produced *Alchemised*). Earnings are very concentrated at the top.

### Cited Findings
- Kindle Vella (launched 2021; episodes of 600-5,000 words, paid with tokens) shut down in Feb 2025. Dec 4, 2024 was the last day to publish or buy tokens, and unused tokens were refunded. Amazon said it had not caught on as hoped. — [GeekWire, Oct 2024](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/amazon-to-shut-down-kindle-vella-serial-book-platform-saying-it-hasnt-caught-on-as-wed-hoped/); [SelfPublishing.com](https://selfpublishing.com/kindle-vella-shutdown/)
- *The Wandering Inn* (pirateaba), an "enormously popular" web serial, is being published by Harper Voyager, and Will Wight's *Overworld* by Tor. Audiobook catalogs are "increasingly likely to feature multiple LitRPG titles." — [Reactor, Sep 3, 2026](https://reactormag.com/the-rise-of-litrpg/)
- The Royal Road → Patreon (advance chapters) → Amazon KU → Audible pipeline is described as the dominant LitRPG monetization model. *Dungeon Crawler Carl* and *He Who Fights with Monsters* started as Royal Road serials. — [Wikipedia, "Progression fantasy"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progression_fantasy); [Wikipedia, "Dungeon Crawler Carl"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Crawler_Carl)
- Aethon Books and Vault Comics formed a partnership (2025, reported by PW) to adapt LitRPG/progression fantasy into graphic novels, citing "millions of collective sales." This comes from a search summary citing PW; I did not retrieve the PW piece. — [Wikipedia, "Progression fantasy"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progression_fantasy)
- Royal Road earnings (low-authority estimate): about $50-300/month at 500 followers, $200-600 at 1,500, and $1,000-3,000 at 5,000+, mainly via Patreon. One count found only 15 of 2,717 serializing fictions earning over $4,000/month (0.55%). — [PlotWrite, 2025](https://www.plotwrite.ink/answers/how-much-do-royal-road-writers-make) (low-authority; unverified)
- Substack: the app has 5.5M monthly and 1.5M daily active users, and new discovery features have helped fiction serials. Examples: Elle Griffin earned $19,000 in her first year serializing novels; Simon K Jones has 6,000+ subscribers; John Pistelli's *Major Arcana* (serialized 2023-24) was picked up by Belt Publishing in 2025. — [Compact, "Substack Has Revived the Serial Novel"](https://compactmag.com/article/substack-has-revived-the-serial-novel) (date not confirmed)
- Wattpad: about 94M monthly users and 1B+ uploads (low-authority source, unverified). The audience skews 13-25 and monetization is invitation-only. The Wattpad Originals program offers up to $5,000 advances plus a paid-content revenue share. — [shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc/fandom/wattpad) (low authority); [BusinessWire, Sep 3, 2024](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20240903263926/en/Calling-All-Authors-Wattpad%e2%80%99s-Originals-Program-is-Now-Accepting-Pitches/); [FictionMaker, 2026](https://fictionmaker.com/blog/best-platforms-to-publish-fiction-online-2026)
- AO3 fanfiction as an incubator: *Manacled* had 20M+ downloads before being turned into *Alchemised*. — [Pasadena Now](https://www.pasadenanow.com/weekendr/a-fanfiction-phenomenon-lands-in-pasadena-senlinyu-brings-alchemised-to-city-of-roses/)

### Inferences
- Serial fiction works best as an audience-building funnel for genres with long series and fast updates (LitRPG, progression fantasy, cultivation). It rarely works as a standalone income source.
- Romance and romantasy serial readers shifted toward Wattpad/AO3 discovery and then to KU after Vella closed. Substack fits literary or voice-led fiction with a direct-subscription model.

## 6. Reader expectations per genre for indie success (release cadence, series length, word count, price)

### Takeaway
Indie success depends on series and speed. Authors earning $5k-20k/month typically release 3-4 books a year. Authors with 25+ books have a median income of about $3,000/month. 89% of Kindle fantasy bestsellers are in series, averaging 537 pages. The indie price band for fantasy is $4.99-$6.99 ($7.99-$8.99 is a "dead zone"), sci-fi's sweet spot is $5.99, and Christmas romance is $3.99-$5.99 at 200-300 pages. LitRPG and cozy mystery readers expect very long series (cozy mystery series of 30+ books are common, per a low-authority source).

### Cited Findings
- Release cadence: authors earning $5,000-$20,000/month commonly release three to four books a year. Backlist size, a working mailing list and reinvested ad spend are the clearest income drivers. — [Written Word Media 2025 survey, Dec 2, 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/) (cadence figure via search summary of the same page)
- Catalog size: authors with 25+ books report a median of about $3,000/month, and 40%+ of them earn $5,000+/month. High earners typically have deep series (10+ connected titles), about 18,327 email subscribers on average (in the $10k+ group), spend about $4,500/month on marketing (vs about $81 for authors earning under $100/month), spend $250-$1,000 on covers, and rarely skip paid editing. Authors with an email list have a median income of about $300/month vs about $15 without. Income spread: 44% earn ≤$100/month, 13% earn more than $5k, 8% more than $10k. — [Written Word Media, Dec 2, 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- Formats used by indie authors: ebook 98%, paperback 97%, audiobook 51%, hardcover 36%, collector's editions 8%. — [Written Word Media, Dec 2, 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/)
- Fantasy (Kindle): 89% of bestsellers are in series, averaging 537 pages. Indie price lane is $4.99-$6.99, traditional is $11-$17, and $7.99-$8.99 is a "dead zone." Sci-fi: 74% in series, averaging 428 pages; $5.99 is the sweet spot (13% of slots, 21% of royalties). — [Author Media/K-lytics, Jul 2026](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Christmas romance: top titles average $3.99-$5.99 with occasional $0.99 promos; 200-300 pages; series outperform standalones; covers without people currently outperform covers with people. — [Author Media/K-lytics, Dec 20, 2025](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-christmas-romance-vs-paranormal-romance/)
- Rapid release as a traditional-publishing strategy: *Daggermouth* is moving from ebook (Feb 2026) to sequel (Nov 2026) on a deliberately "rapid release schedule." — [Pan Macmillan, Feb 18, 2026](https://trade.panmacmillan.com/tor-bramble-have-acquired-the-phenomenal-indie-author-h-m-wolfe-and-will-publish-her-dark-dystopian-romance-and-viral-sensation-daggermouth-and-sequel-python-on-a-rapid-release-schedule)
- Cozy mystery series of 30+ books are common, and the genre has a dedicated, series-loyal readership. Low-authority aggregator. — [SelfPublishing.com, 2026](https://selfpublishing.com/most-popular-book-genres-on-amazon/)
- Royal Road serials typically update several times a week to build a following before moving to KU. — [Wikipedia, "Progression fantasy"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progression_fantasy)

### Inferences
- A realistic minimum plan for a new KU genre author: commit to a series of at least 3-5 books before expecting traction, release every 3-4 months, price book 1 low or use KU and promos, keep later books at $4.99-$6.99, and invest in a professional cover and editing.
- Length varies by genre: romance and seasonal romance run short (about 200-300 pages), while fantasy runs long (about 500+ pages). LitRPG readers tolerate or expect very long books and series.
- Most indie authors earn under $100/month. Choosing a niche matters less than consistent output, series depth and building an email list.
