# Competitors and prices: UK "flat check before you pay" service

Research date: 3 October 2026. Written in plain English.

**How to read this:**
- Every fact has a link next to it. Click it to check.
- **(estimate)** means it is my own guess or calculation. It is not a published fact.
- **(old)** means the source is from before 2024.
- **(anecdotal)** means one person's story. It is not hard data.
- Rupee amounts use the rate on 3 Oct 2026: **£1 = ₹127.4**, $1 (US) = £0.76, A$1 = £0.53, C$1 = £0.53 ([open.er-api.com, 3 Oct 2026](https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/GBP)). Rates move, so check again before you set prices.

## Short answer (read this first)

1. **The closest competitor is Kinpany, and it covers London only.** It charges **£55 per flat (about ₹7,000)**. Before the visit it checks who owns the flat on the Land Registry and whether the HMO licence is in place. Then someone visits in person, takes a video and photos, and writes a report that ends with "go ahead / be careful / walk away". The report takes **2–5 working days**. Its page does not say it does a live video call ([Kinpany services](https://www.kinpany.co.uk/services/)).
2. **Viewber is the big UK "viewing agent" company.** Anyone can book "View a property on my behalf" **from £38 + VAT (about £45.60, or ₹5,800)**. But Viewber is set up for landlords, letting agents and investors. It does not speak to students or tenants, and its full price list is only visible after you sign up ([Viewber price page](https://viewber.co.uk/landlord-checking-services), [Viewber investors page](https://viewber.co.uk/landlords-investors)).
3. **ViewForYou** was a UK student-run viewing service. It offered a "You View" live video tour for **£64.99 (cut from £99.99)** ([viewforyou.uk, search result](https://viewforyou.uk/)). **Its website did not load on 3 Oct 2026, so it may have closed.**
4. **Student housing platforms (Amber, UniAcco, University Living, Casita, uhomes, Fly Homes) do NOT check private flats you found yourself.** They are free for students. Their money comes from commission paid by the property owners. They only check and sell their own listings, which are mostly big student blocks ([Careers360, 2023](https://news.careers360.com/study-abroad-uk-us-australia-canada-student-accommodation-amber-student-university-living-overseas-education), [UniAcco blog, 2026](https://uniacco.com/blog/how-to-spot-student-accommodation-scams-in-the-uk), [uhomes, Dec 2025](https://en.uhomes.com/company-news/us-property-inspections-authentic-listings-and-pricing)).
5. **I could not find any Indian Instagram or WhatsApp service that sells paid UK flat checks with a public price.** (Web search cannot search inside Instagram well, so check this by hand. See "Gaps" at the end.)
6. **Prices in other countries:** View For You NYC charges **$49 (about ₹4,700)**. Gander Tour in NYC charges **$125 (about ₹12,000)**. Propzee in Melbourne charges **A$199 (about ₹13,300)** ([View For You NYC](https://www.viewforyounyc.com/), [Gander Tour](https://www.gandertour.com/), [Propzee](https://propzee.com/)).
7. **What a partner would cost:** Viewber pays its viewers about **£19 for a standard 30-minute viewing** (2025 job ads). Airtasker UK jobs to "view a property" usually cost **£25–£50, with a median of £30** ([DWP Find a Job listing](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/17999999), [Airtasker UK](https://www.airtasker.com/uk/services/real-estate)).
8. **The gap:** I found no one who does **all of these together**:
   - works in **any UK city** (not just London);
   - checks the **landlord's ID against the Land Registry owner**;
   - checks the key papers (**deposit protection, gas safety, EPC, HMO licence**);
   - does a **live video call** during the visit;
   - sends a **written report within 48 hours**;
   - is **sold in India, in rupees, on Instagram and WhatsApp, with call times that suit India**.
9. **A fair price is about £55–£65 per flat (₹6,999–₹8,299)** **(estimate)**. That matches Kinpany and ViewForYou, and still leaves a profit after paying the partner.

### SpareRoom and the big listing sites
- SpareRoom says it was the first flatshare site to offer **Video Ads**, which landlords make themselves ([SpareRoom about](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/content/about/about-spareroom-new/)). It has also added tools to **book and track viewings** inside the site ([SpareRoom blog](https://blog.spareroom.co.uk/new-viewing-features/)). Using SpareRoom is free for room seekers, and it does **not** offer an independent check by someone else.
- SpareRoom's own advice is: **"Never pay a deposit without viewing the room in person"**, and ask the landlord to show ID (driving licence or passport) before you pay anything ([SpareRoom scams page](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/content/info-flatsharing/scams-to-look-out-for/), [SpareRoom safety tips](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/content/info-flatsharing/safety-tips-for-room-seekers/)). That is exactly the job your service does for someone who is still in India.
- OpenRent explains how landlords can show a flat over a video call ([OpenRent help](https://help.openrent.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/360007525177-How-to-Conduct-a-Property-Viewing-Online)). But that tour is run **by the landlord**. Nobody independent is checking.

### Free things a student can already do (these compete with you on price)
- **Students' Union contract checks:** many unions will check a tenancy contract for free before you sign. Examples: Worcester SU, UAL, BCU SU ([Worcester SU](https://www.worcsu.com/helpandadvice/housing/contractchecking/), [UAL](https://www.arts.ac.uk/study-at-ual/accommodation-services/private-accommodation-advice/contracts-deposits-and-rent), [BCU SU](https://bcusu.com/homes/useful-information/tenancy-agreements)). They only check the contract. Nobody visits the flat.
- **Check the owner yourself:** the Land Registry title register (it shows who owns the property) costs **£7 online (about ₹890)**. It went up from £3 on 9 Dec 2024 ([GOV.UK fees](https://gov.uk/guidance/changes-to-fees-for-hm-land-registrys-information-services), [GOV.UK search the register](https://gov.uk/get-information-about-property-and-land/search-the-register)). Most Indian students do not know this exists, which is a good topic for a Reel.
- **"Send a friend":** university advice says to send a friend to see the property if you are abroad ([King's College London](https://self-service.kcl.ac.uk/article/KA-01267/en-us)). Your service is the paid version of "a friend in the UK".

## Q3. Indian or UK services doing paid in-person checks for students

- **Study-abroad consultants (Leverage Edu, Yocket, IDP, AECC):** IDP lists "accommodation" among its pre-departure help ([Yocket blog listing consultants](https://yocket.com/blog/top-7-study-abroad-consultants-in-kolkata)). Leverage Edu runs Fly Homes (see above). **I found none of them selling a paid in-person check of a private UK flat.** Their accommodation help means booking through partner platforms.
- **Instagram / WhatsApp Indian student pages:** **I found none with a public price** for UK flat checks. Search tools cannot search inside Instagram properly, so this needs a manual check (see "Gaps").
- **helpmeroom (Intervesta Housing):** a not-yet-launched app where a local student views a flat for a remote student. Price **$20 a viewing (about ₹1,930)**. The local student gets **$15–17**, and the app keeps about 20% ([helpmeroom](https://intervestahousing.wixsite.com/helpmeroom)). No landlord checks, and the country is not stated. This shows a "cheap student helper" version of the idea.
- **Kinpany and ViewForYou** (see Q1) are the only UK businesses I found that were built around international students.

## Why students need this (short background)
- Fraudsters target students with fake rental adverts and ask for deposits up front. International students are especially at risk because they search from far away and do not know the UK rules ([Action Fraud](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/news/students-scammed-into-renting-bogus-houses-and-flats), [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/help-my-university-accommodation-turned-out-to-be-a-scam-aKYG08R48f5V)).
- SpareRoom itself says never pay a deposit without viewing in person ([SpareRoom](https://www.spareroom.co.uk/content/info-flatsharing/scams-to-look-out-for/)). Your service is how a student in India can follow that advice.

## Gaps in this research (what I could not confirm)
1. **Instagram and WhatsApp Indian services:** web search cannot see inside Instagram. **To do by hand:** search Instagram for "UK accommodation help", "UK flat verification", "UK house viewing for students", "London room check", and look at Indian student pages in UK cities. Message 5–10 of them to ask their price.
2. **Reddit:** my searches found no useful Reddit threads (r/IndiansInUK, r/HousingUK, r/international_students) about paying someone to view a flat. Search Reddit directly.
3. **ViewForYou:** its website did not load on 3 Oct 2026, and its price (£64.99) comes only from a search snippet. It may have closed.
4. **Viewber's full price list for private individuals** is only shown after you sign up (free). Sign up to see exact tenant-side prices.
5. **Kinpany:** I don't know how many customers it has or whether it plans to go beyond London. No Trustpilot reviews were checked.
6. **ISSA Canada C$260** comes from a search snippet. The page itself did not show details.
7. **Trustpilot reviews and The PIE News** were not checked, to stay within the search limit.
8. **Partner pay in cities other than London** (Manchester, Birmingham and so on) was not confirmed from job ads. The £25 figure is an estimate.
9. **Let UK Home's** viewing price is not published. You would have to ask them for a quote.
