# UK Flat Check Service: Where to Find Customers, How to Sell, and Do the Numbers Work?

Research date: 3 October 2026. Written in plain English.

**How to read the tags:**
- **(Estimate)** means my own calculation or guess. It is not a published fact.
- **(Anecdotal)** means one person's or one blog's view.
- **(Unverified)** means I saw it in a search summary but could not open the original page.

Everything else has a source link next to it.

---

## 0. The short answer

1. **The market is real but small.** About 82,000 to 98,000 Indian students get UK study visas each year. Most of them choose halls or big student buildings, which already have booking platforms. Your customer is the smaller group who pick a **private** flat or room on their own.
2. **Paid ads will lose money at this price.** Study-abroad leads on Instagram and Facebook cost ₹600 to ₹2,000 each. A ₹7,000 service cannot pay for that. Your customers have to come from your own Reels, from groups, and from people who refer you.
3. **About ₹7,000 per check works.** After paying the UK partner, their travel and fees, you keep about ₹2,200 to ₹2,450 per check. You break even at **about 11 checks a month**, or **about 32 a month** if you also pay yourself ₹40,000.
4. **₹3 lakh is enough** to launch for January 2027 and still be running when the big September 2027 season starts in June, provided you keep monthly costs near ₹20,000.

## 1. Background facts to know first

- **How many Indian students go to the UK each year.** Indian nationals got 98,014 study visas in the year to June 2025, down 11% ([Galvanize](https://galvanizetestprep.com/news/uk-study-visas-to-indians-drop-11-percent-2025/), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/uk-student-visas-for-indians-drop-11-as-work-visas-fall-48-in-2025-125082200461_1.html)). They got 95,231 in calendar year 2025, which was 23% of all UK study visas ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/nri/study/story/indians-top-uk-visas-in-2025-with-over-95000-study-permits-leading-graduate-and-worker-routes-519709-2026-03-09)). A search summary said 82,523 for the year to June 2026 **(Unverified)**.
- **Most are Master's students.** 81% of Indian students come for a Master's degree ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/study-abroad-uk-student-visa-18-pc-higher-2025-first-half-new-data-india-china-pakistan-dependent-extension-masters-united-kingdom)). That means a one-year course and one tenancy, so you get one chance to sell a check and one chance to sell a move-out check.
- **Universities with the most Indian students (UniAcco estimates, 2026)** ([UniAcco](https://uniacco.com/blog/uk-universities-with-most-indian-students)):

  | University | City | Indian students |
  |---|---|---|
  | BPP | London, Manchester, Leeds | 13,840 |
  | Hertfordshire | Hatfield | 8,515 |
  | University of East London | London | 6,720 |
  | Coventry | Coventry | 6,485 |
  | Bedfordshire | Luton | 5,900 |
  | Greenwich | London | 4,085 |
  | De Montfort | Leicester | 3,715 |
  | Anglia Ruskin | Cambridge, Chelmsford, London | 3,705 |
  | Northumbria | Newcastle | 2,870 |
  | Leeds Beckett | Leeds | 2,785 |

  **What this means for you:** London, Coventry, Leicester and Leeds are clearly on your list. **Hatfield, Luton and Newcastle** probably matter more than Glasgow or Sheffield. Add them to your partner network early.
- **How much money a student risks.** Rooms in London cost about £260 to £300 a week ([University Living](https://www.universityliving.com/blog/accommodation/affordable-london-accommodation-indian-students/)). In England a landlord can ask for a **holding deposit of up to 1 week's rent** and a **deposit of up to 5 weeks' rent** ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/private-renting/deposits)). So a London student can lose roughly £1,300 to £1,800 (about ₹1.6 to ₹2.3 lakh) to a fake landlord **(Estimate)**.
- **New law: no more big rent payments upfront.** Under the Renters' Rights Act, landlords in England **cannot ask for rent before the tenancy is signed**. After signing, they can ask for **only one month's rent in advance** ([GOV.UK guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/guide-to-the-renters-rights-act/guide-to-the-renters-rights-act)). The GOV.UK renting pages now describe "assured periodic tenancies" as the standard type ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/private-renting/tenancy-agreements)), which shows the change has taken effect. I could not confirm the exact start date; my memory says 1 May 2026 **(Unverified)**.
  - **Reel idea:** "If a landlord in England asks for 6 months' rent before you sign, that is a red flag." This is simple, scary and true, which makes it ideal for Reels.
  - **Scotland (Glasgow) has different rental rules.** I did not check them; verify before selling there.
- **Scams are common.**
  - Students are often scammed with fake listings and asked to pay before a viewing ([Action Fraud](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/news/students-scammed-into-renting-bogus-houses-and-flats); the page blocked my reader, so I could not get its numbers).
  - Greater Manchester Police warned in August 2024 that victims lost up to £2,000 each **(Unverified, seen in a search summary)**.
  - Facebook Marketplace ads, Telegram groups and Instagram "student accommodation agents" are flagged as common scam sources ([Goniyo](https://goniyo.com/blog/ultimate-guide-student-housing-indian-students-uk)).
  - Some education agents tell students not to worry about housing until they arrive in the UK, when few options are left ([Study International](https://studyinternational.com/?p=322838)).

## 3. Study-abroad consultants and agents: will they refer you?

**What they earn now:**
- **Accommodation platforms pay per booking.** Amber's business model is a commission from property owners on each booking ([Amber affiliate listing](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/amber-student-affiliate-program/)). Amber's student ambassadors earn **up to £60 per booking**, paid about 2 months after the booking ([Amber blog](https://amberstudent.com/blog/post/introducing-the-amber-ambassador-program)).
- **University Living gives agents referral links.** The student applies through the agent's link so "your agent receives credit for the referral" ([ApplyBoard help page](https://assist.applyboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/36272465149709-Students-How-to-Apply-for-Accommodation-with-University-Living)). University Living also signs up partners for airport transfers, SIM cards, forex, insurance and banking ([University Living job post](https://universityliving.keka.com/careers/jobdetails/3194)). They might list your service as one of these extras.
- **UniAcco** reportedly shares 40% of its revenue with partners **(Unverified; the source page did not load)**.
- **Consultants also charge parents directly.**
  - The typical range is ₹10,000 to ₹50,000. Independent consultants charge ₹25,000 to ₹1,50,000.
  - IDP: free to ₹40,000. Edwise: ₹30,000 to ₹70,000. The Chopras: about ₹50,000.
  - Sources: [Ambitio](https://ambitio.club/blog/study-abroad-consultants-fees/), [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/how-study-abroad-counselling-beneficial-for-students-paid-vs-free). These come from a blog and a search summary, so treat them as **Anecdotal**.

**Will they refer you? (Estimate)**
- **The catch:** consultants earn when a student books a big student building through a platform. A private flat earns them nothing, so they may not push it.
- **Your pitch:** "When your student has **already decided** on a private flat, send them to us. You earn ₹1,000, and if the flat turns out to be a scam, nobody blames you."
- **Big chains (IDP, AECC, Leverage Edu, Yocket)** have head-office partnership teams. They will want proof, such as 50+ completed checks and reviews, before signing. Do not chase them before the September 2027 season.
- **Local agents in your city and in Gujarat, Punjab, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kerala** decide faster. Start with 10 to 20 of them.

**Suggested terms (Estimate):**
- **Referral fee:** ₹1,000 per paid check, about 14% of a ₹6,999 check. Pay monthly by UPI and track each agent with their own discount code.
- **Wholesale option:** the agent buys checks from you at ₹5,500 and adds them to their premium package at any price they like.
- **Co-branded report:** the agent's logo goes on the PDF report. This costs you nothing and makes the agent look good.

## 4. Universities and Indian student societies: partnership options

- **University international offices.** They warn students about scams and run their own services: airport pickup ([Leicester's is free](https://reslife.le.ac.uk/story/39730281/airport-collection-service)), housing advice and lists of approved landlords. They are **unlikely to recommend a paid, unproven service** in year one (Estimate). What you can ask for is to be listed on their "useful services" page, or to speak at their online pre-departure sessions.
- **NISAU UK** signs formal agreements with universities, including UEL ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/university-of-east-london-partners-with-nisau-uk-to-strengthen-indian-student-welfare-and-employability)) and York, the first Russell Group university to do so ([University of York](https://york.ac.uk/news-and-events/news/2026/quality/india-education-agreement/)). It works with corporate partners and the British Council ([British Council](https://opportunities-insight.britishcouncil.org/news/opportunities/nisau%E2%80%99s-india-uk-achievers-honours-and-conference)), and it cares about student welfare.
  - **Pitch:** offer free scam-awareness content and a members' discount, for example 10% off. You become their "safe housing" partner.
  - **Cost:** sponsorship terms are not public. Ask them.
- **INSA chapters and university Indian societies.** Societies always need sponsors for freshers' events (Estimate).
  - **Offer:** ₹500 to the society for each paid check that uses their code, plus a free check for one committee member's flat, filmed as content.
  - **Where to start:** Coventry, Leicester (DMU), Hertfordshire, UEL, Greenwich, Leeds Beckett and Northumbria, because they have the most Indian students (Section 1).

## 6. Partner costs and the maths

### 6a. What to pay a UK partner (Estimate, based on the sources below)

**What the market pays:**
- The UK minimum wage for workers aged 21+ is **£12.71 an hour from 1 April 2026** ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/national-minimum-wage-rates)).
- Viewber paid its viewers **£10 to £20 per viewing** in 2017, plus travel **up to £4 in London** or **25p per mile** elsewhere ([The Negotiator](https://thenegotiator.co.uk/?p=26480)). A search summary of Viewber's help page says £12 for a short viewing, £18 to £19.49 for a standard 30-minute viewing, and £40 to £48 for photo jobs (**Unverified**; the page now shows a 404 error).
- Buses outside London cost a **maximum of £3 for a single journey** across England until at least March 2027 ([Bus News](https://bus-news.com/uk-3-bus-fare-cap-extended-to-march-2027/), [Oxford Bus](https://www.oxfordbus.co.uk/leading-bus-operators-confirm-ps3-fare-cap-until-end-2026)).

**Your job is longer than a Viewber viewing.** It includes travel, a 30 to 45 minute visit, a live video call, photos of the landlord's ID and documents, and filling in your report template. That is about 2 hours in total, so you should pay more than Viewber does.

**Important rule on who you can hire:** people on a UK Student visa **cannot be self-employed** ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)). Do **not** pay current students as freelancers. Recruit instead from:

- British Indians,

Check each person's right to work before they start.

### 6c. Cost of winning one customer (Estimate)

- **From paid ads:**
  - Study-abroad businesses pay about **₹600 to ₹2,000 per lead** on Facebook and Instagram, and only **3% to 5% of leads become customers** ([Sotros Infotech](https://sotrosinfotech.com/blog/meta-ads-cost-per-lead-education-india-southeast-asia-2026/)).
  - General education leads in India cost ₹150 to ₹800 ([same source](https://sotrosinfotech.com/blog/meta-ads-cost-per-lead-education-india-southeast-asia-2026/)).
  - Worldwide, an education lead averages about $21 ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-lead/education/india)).
  - So even if 10% of your leads buy, one customer from ads costs **₹6,000 to ₹20,000. That is more than the ₹2,447 you earn per check, so ads alone lose money.**
- **From Reels, groups and referrals:** your main cost is your own time, plus ₹1,000 when an agent sends the customer or ₹500 when a society or creator does. If half your sales come through referrers, the average cost is **about ₹500 per customer**.
- **Per check after that average:** ₹2,447 − ₹500 = **about ₹1,950 outside London**, and ₹2,230 − ₹500 = **about ₹1,730 in London**. Assuming 70% of checks are outside London and 30% in London, the blend is **about ₹1,880 per check**.

### 6d. Monthly costs and break-even (Estimate)

**Running costs: about ₹20,000 a month**

| Item | Per month |
|---|---|
| Phone, data, Canva, Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business and a booking page | ₹4,000 |
| Small ad budget to boost your best Reels | ₹8,000 |

| Partner sign-up, training and trial visits | ₹5,000 |

**Break-even:**
- **Without paying yourself:** ₹20,000 ÷ ₹1,880 ≈ **11 checks a month**.
- **If you pay yourself ₹40,000 a month:** ₹60,000 ÷ ₹1,880 ≈ **32 checks a month**.

**Plan for the ₹3 lakh**

One-off costs: about ₹1.25 lakh

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Website, payment page and report template | ₹20,000 |
| Terms and disclaimer ("we are not surveyors or lawyers"), partner contract and UK privacy notice | ₹25,000 |
| 10 paid trial checks across 5 cities, at about ₹4,000 each | ₹40,000 |
| Phone mic, light and editing apps | ₹10,000 |
| UK liability insurance, first-year guess (get real quotes) | ₹30,000 |

- **That leaves about ₹1.75 lakh in the bank.** At ₹20,000 a month, it lasts **about 8 to 9 months even with zero sales**, which carries you to June 2027 and the start of the September-intake rush.

**Rough first-year sales (Estimate):**
- January-intake season (Nov to Jan): 30 to 60 checks.
- February to May: 5 to 10 checks a month.
- June to September 2027: 50 to 150 checks a month, if Reels and agents work.
- About 300 checks in year one × ₹1,880 ≈ ₹5.6 lakh, minus ₹2.4 lakh of running costs ≈ **₹3.2 lakh before paying yourself**.

**Market size check (Estimate):** about 85,000 Indian students a year × perhaps 30% who choose private rooms or flats × half of those who arrange it from India ≈ 12,000 to 14,000 possible buyers a year. Winning 1% to 3% of them means 120 to 400 checks. **This can be a good side business. To grow bigger it needs the extra products in Section 7.**

## 8. Timing calendar

**When students book housing:**
- **January intake:** research June to August, book August to October.
- **September intake:** research October to January, book January to April.
- Source: [Admistay](https://admistay.com/intelligence-hub/how-early-should-you-book-student-accommodation).

These dates are mainly for **halls and student buildings**. Private flats and rooms are usually advertised only **4 to 8 weeks before the move-in date**, because the current tenant has to give notice first. Under the new rules, tenants give **two months' notice** ([GOV.UK guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/guide-to-the-renters-rights-act/guide-to-the-renters-rights-act)). So your busy season comes **later** than the halls booking season (Estimate). Guides suggest searching for private housing 3 to 6 months ahead ([UniAcco](https://uniacco.com/blog/when-to-start-looking-for-student-accommodation), [Edvoy](https://edvoy.com/articles/your-student-accommodation-guide-for-january-intake)), but real private listings appear much closer to move-in.

## 9. How to sell (Estimate, based on the facts above)

1. **Sell to parents, but talk to students too.** Parents pay, and their fear is fraud. Make Reels in Hindi and English, and in regional languages if you can, explaining "the 5 signs a UK flat ad is fake".
2. **Lead with the free Red-Flag Check.** Ask people to "send us the listing link on WhatsApp". Most fake ads can be spotted in minutes. When an ad looks real, sell the ₹6,999 visit.
3. **Show proof.** Post a sample report PDF, the partner's face and name, a live-call screen recording (with the landlord's permission), and a promise: "report within 48 hours or your money back".
4. **Quote the law.** "In England a holding deposit is at most 1 week's rent and a deposit at most 5 weeks' rent ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/private-renting/deposits)). Anyone asking for more is a red flag."
5. **Get referrals from people already in the UK.** Seniors in IPLUK and in the university groups get these questions every day. Give them a code and ₹500 per sale.
6. **Hold paid ads back** until you know how many people who message you go on to pay. Use ads only to reach people who have already watched your Reels.
