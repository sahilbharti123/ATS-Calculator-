# 100 New Business Ideas for India

**Every idea here already works and makes money in another country, and is rare or missing in India.** None of them repeats the earlier list. Each one is explained on its own card in plain words.

## How to read each card

- **In one line:** what the business is.
- **What's missing in India today:** the everyday problem it solves.
- **How it works:** what the customer does, what you do, and who does the actual work. In every idea, a partner, freelancer or supplier does the hands-on work while you find customers and run things.
- **Who pays and how much:** the customer and a rough price.
- **Proof it works abroad:** a real company doing it, with a link.
- **Who does this in India now:** the Indian businesses we found doing the same thing.
- **To start:** roughly how much money you need, how many weeks until the first paying customer, and the biggest risk.

## Three honest notes

1. **The competitor checks are quick web searches.** Small businesses that only sell on Instagram don't show up in them. Before you choose an idea, spend half a day searching Instagram, Justdial and Google Maps in your own city.
2. **Every price and cost marked "about" is an estimate**, not a quote from a supplier.
3. A few "proof abroad" lines are marked "(not checked)" because we could not open a source for them.

## My 10 picks for you

These suit someone who is good at selling and making Reels, wants partners to do the work, has ₹3 lakh, and wants to start by December.

1. **A printed family newspaper for grandparents, every month (#59).** Children abroad pay every month. No one does it in India, and it costs about ₹60,000 to start. It's the same feeling as the voice-note book you liked.
2. **Flight companions for parents flying alone to visit their children abroad (#61).** It solves a real fear, and December is peak travel season. NRI children pay.
3. **Head-lice removal at home for children (#1).** Every parent knows the problem, no one offers the service, it costs about ₹50,000 to start, and it makes very shareable Reels.
4. **Real-grass toilet patches for dogs in flats (#19).** Owners pay every month, and no one does it in India.
5. **Film cameras on every table at weddings (#34).** It's wedding season now, it costs under ₹1 lakh, and couples love the photos.
6. **Café passport: one drink at 15 independent cafés (#29).** It's cheap to start and made for Instagram, and cafés get new customers for free.
7. **Picture books read aloud in a grandparent's own voice (#60).** An emotional gift for NRI families and for Christmas.
8. **Exam-week care boxes for students in hostels (#33).** Parents pay, and board and college exams come in February and March.
9. **Job-offer negotiation help, paid only if your offer goes up (#40).** It's easy to sell because the customer pays nothing unless they gain. You would partner with ex-recruiters.
10. **Hot tubs delivered for winter house parties (#81).** Best in Delhi NCR and other cold cities. A December launch fits, and the Reels sell themselves.
