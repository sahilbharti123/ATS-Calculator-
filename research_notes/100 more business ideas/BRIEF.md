# Brief for the "100 more ideas" batches

## Who the ideas are for
- A first-time founder in a big Indian city (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata or Ahmedabad; the exact city is not known yet).
- Works full-time on this. Good at selling, talking to people and making Instagram Reels. Has NO hands-on skills: cannot cook, bake, decorate, repair, code, design or give treatments. Partners, freelancers, staff or suppliers must do the actual work while the founder finds customers and runs things.
- Has ₹3,00,000 in total, including money kept aside as a safety reserve.
- Wants to start within about 2 months (by early December 2026). Today is October 2026.
- Wants ideas that already work and make money in another country but are missing or very rare in India (ideally 0–3 Indian businesses doing the same thing).

## What the founder liked and disliked
- They liked these when they were explained in simple words: a website where anyone can buy a day pass to use a 5-star hotel's pool; a service that sends parents a weekly question on WhatsApp, they answer by voice note, and it becomes a printed book of their life story; renting baby cribs, car seats and prams to NRI families visiting India for a few weeks.
- They said the last list of ideas was "very unclear… just a name… what do they do that's missing?" So every idea must be explained so a 16-year-old would understand it in one read.

## Writing rules (very important)
- Plain, simple English. Short sentences. No jargon. Do not use words like D2C, B2B, SKU, MOQ, CAC, omakase, concierge, aggregator, asset-light or vertical without explaining them in the same sentence. Prefer "a service that…" and "you would…".
- Give each idea a plain name that says what it is (e.g. "Pool day passes at 5-star hotels", not "ResortPass for India").
- Use ₹ for all money in India. Mark your own estimates with "about".
- Do not invent facts. Only state a company, number or fact abroad if you found it in a source; give one link. If you only know it from memory and could not confirm it, say "(not checked)".

## Exact format for every idea
Use this exact Markdown block for each idea, with no tables:

### [plain idea name]
**In one line:** [what it is, in under 20 words]
**What's missing in India today:** [the everyday problem or wish, and why people can't easily get it now — 1–2 sentences]
**How it works:** [3–4 short numbered steps: what the customer does, what you (the founder) do, who your partner is and what they do]
**Who pays and how much:** [who the customer is, and a price in ₹]
**Proof it works abroad:** [company name, country, one fact] ([source](link))
**Who does this in India now:** [names you found, or "No one found in a quick search"]
**To start:** [about ₹ needed, weeks to first paying customer, and the main risk in a few words]
